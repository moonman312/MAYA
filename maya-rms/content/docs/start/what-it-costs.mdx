<InPlainWords>
Per room, per month, and the price per room drops as the property grows. There is no minimum: 5 rooms is $27.50 a month. Yearly billing saves 10% from 21 rooms up. Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax. Every size gets the same MAYA.
</InPlainWords>

## The brackets

Find the row your room count lands in. Every room in the property pays that row's rate.

| Rooms | Per room, per month | Yearly |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 20 | $5.50 | 12 times the monthly price, paid once a year |
| 21 to 40 | $5.00 | 10% off |
| 41 to 60 | $4.00 | 10% off |
| 61 to 80 | $3.00 | 10% off |
| 81 to 500 | $2.50 | 10% off |

The whole property bills at one bracket: the one its room count falls into. There is no minimum charge and no other fee. Any adjustment for a room count change shows on your next invoice. Prices exclude tax, and any tax that applies is yours to pay under the Terms. Each property is its own subscription.

<PriceCalculator />

The calculator on the get-maya.com home page uses the same brackets. Both use the room count you type. After you connect, MAYA checks it against the rooms in your system, and how it counts them depends on the system: read [how the bill is worked out](#how-the-bill-is-worked-out-after-you-sign-up).

<Callout kind="example" title="Some worked examples">
The Harbour Inn has 20 rooms: 20 times $5.50 is $110 a month, or $1,320 a year paid once. A 21-room property is in the next bracket: 21 times $5.00 is $105 a month, or $1,134 a year with the 10% off. 40 rooms: $200 a month or $2,160 a year. 100 rooms: $250 a month or $2,700 a year. A 3-room B&B pays $16.50 a month.
</Callout>

## Why a step, not a slope

You may have spotted that 21 rooms costs less than 20. That is on purpose. The bracket is a step: when a property crosses into the next bracket, every one of its rooms moves to the lower rate. There is no blended or "first 20 rooms at one price, the rest at another" arithmetic to work out. One room count, one rate, one number.

## Monthly or yearly

You choose at signup, on the <Ui>Billing</Ui> control of the payment screen, between <Ui>Monthly</Ui> and <Ui>Yearly</Ui>.

- **Monthly** bills the bracket price each month, in advance, at the start of each month of service.
- **Yearly** is one invoice for twelve months at the per-room rate, less 10% from 21 rooms up. The screen says so beside the button: <Ui>save 10%</Ui> for 21 rooms and up, and <Ui>pay once a year</Ui> for 1 to 20 rooms, because the smallest bracket has no yearly discount.

The yearly price is still worked out per room, per month, and then multiplied by twelve. The <Ui>Billing</Ui> page shows it as one price per year. To switch between monthly and yearly later, open <Ui>Update card, receipts & cancellation</Ui> on the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page and change the plan in Stripe's portal. A switch starts a new billing period that day. Moving to yearly charges the full year at once, less a credit for the unused part of your month. [Switching between monthly and yearly](/docs/billing/cards-payments-and-invoices#switching-between-monthly-and-yearly) has the detail.

Either way, each period is charged at its start, on its renewal date. After a free trial, the first charge falls on the day the trial ends. A change to your room count is not charged on the day: the difference goes on your next invoice. Read [When you are charged](/docs/billing/cards-payments-and-invoices#when-you-are-charged).

**Commitment.** There is no contract term beyond the period you have paid for. A monthly plan can be cancelled any time and ends at the next renewal date. A yearly plan runs to the end of the year you paid for, with no refund for the rest of it. Read [Cancelling, restarting and leaving](/docs/billing/cancelling-restarting-and-leaving).

## The free trial

Without a code, the only free trial comes with connecting from the Cloudbeds Marketplace. A code we give you can also grant one. The Marketplace trial is 7 days, for a property's first signup only: a restart after cancelling is billed when checkout completes. You add a card to start it, nothing is charged until it ends, and you can cancel any time before then. When the trial ends, the subscription starts by itself at your bracket price. Stripe may email a reminder before the first charge, depending on its settings. The date is always on your <Ui>Billing</Ui> page, and the charge goes ahead whether or not the email arrives.

Accounts created directly at maya-rms.com (ThinkReservations, Mews, or Cloudbeds by code) get no trial unless a code grants one. Every property, trial or not, starts in simulation, where MAYA runs your rules and sends nothing, so you can watch before anything reaches your system. Read [Codes and the free trial](/docs/pay/codes-and-the-free-trial).

**Do I pay while I am in simulation?** Yes. Your subscription runs from checkout, or from the end of the trial, whether your property is in simulation or live. Going live costs nothing extra, and there is no other fee.

<Callout kind="careful">
A card is always collected, trial or not. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, it becomes a paid subscription and the card is charged. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid period, and there are no refunds for part of a period.
</Callout>

## Refunds

What you pay is not refunded, including for part of a billing period, rooms or features you did not use, a move to a smaller plan, or time when MAYA or your property system was down. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and MAYA keeps working to the end of the period you paid for. Lowering your room count gives a credit against future invoices, never money back. A free trial costs nothing if you cancel before it ends.

The Terms name the exceptions. If we end your subscription for our own reasons, on 30 days' notice, or stop offering MAYA altogether, we refund the unused part of the period you paid for. And where the law gives you a refund, you get it. Read [Terms and legal](/docs/reference/terms-and-legal#fees-cancelling-and-refunds-in-brief), and clauses 3.5, 7.10 and 12.3 of the [Terms of Service](https://www.get-maya.com/terms).

## Codes

A code is something we give you. It can grant a trial of some number of days, a percentage off (for a set number of months, or for as long as you stay), or a dollar amount off each month (for a set number of months, or for as long as you stay). The payment screen tells you exactly what your code grants the moment you type it, and the price panel re-prices in front of you.

Four things to expect:

- One code per property. A code cannot be used twice on the same property.
- A code's trial replaces the Marketplace trial. The two never stack.
- There is no referral programme. Recommending MAYA to another hotel earns nothing today, and discounts come only from codes we issue.
- Whether you need a code at all depends on your property system. Cloudbeds Marketplace arrivals never do. Otherwise the payment screen tells you: the field reads <Ui>Your code</Ui> when one is needed and <Ui>Your code (optional)</Ui> when it is not.

## Groups of properties

Each property is its own subscription at its own bracket. Two 15-room properties are two subscriptions at $5.50 a room, not one 30-room property at $5.00. Brackets are never pooled. If your Cloudbeds login covers several properties, one card is taken for the first and offered for each of the others, and you pay for them one at a time. There is no volume discount beyond what a code grants. Read [Paying for a group of properties](/docs/pay/paying-for-a-group).

## What is included

Everything, at every size: the rule builder, starter rules built from your history, the calendar, the Rate Simulator, the change log, and prices sent to your system once you go live.

With Mews, prices are worked out and shown but not sent yet, and there are no starter rules, because Mews has no history import. A Mews property pays the same brackets. Read [Mews: what works today](/docs/connect/mews).

The one thing that scales with the bracket is how many people can be on the property's team:

| Rooms | People on the team |
|---|---|
| 1 to 20 | 2 |
| 21 to 40 | 4 |
| 41 to 60 | 5 |
| 61 to 80 | 6 |
| 81 to 500 | 8 |

Read [Inviting and removing people](/docs/team/inviting-and-removing-people).

## How the bill is worked out after you sign up

You type a room count at signup. After you connect, MAYA counts the rooms again on every sync, counting only room types you have ticked as rooms. Where that count comes from depends on your system.

<PmsTabs>
<PmsTab pms="cloudbeds">
Cloudbeds reports how many rooms each room type has, so the count is read from Cloudbeds.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="thinkreservations">
MAYA counts the rooms in each room type from ThinkReservations' list of rooms, leaving out rooms marked there as inactive, as not a room, or as not included in occupancy.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="mews">
Mews does not normally report how many rooms each room category has, so MAYA uses one number for every category, set with us when we connect Mews. The count is that number times the categories you have ticked as rooms. Check the <Ui>Room count</Ui> section of the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page after we connect you, and [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Mews%20room%20counts) if it is higher than the rooms you run.
</PmsTab>
</PmsTabs>

If the count is higher than the rooms you pay for, you get an email and 7 days to set the count yourself. After that MAYA raises it to the measured number and adjusts your next invoice. Nothing is charged on the day. That is how it works today. Under the Terms, we can also charge the difference separately, and bill for rooms that were in use but not paid for over the previous 12 months. So set the count right as soon as the email arrives.

If you pay for more rooms than you run, MAYA never lowers it for you. You lower it on the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page and the next invoice drops. Read [Your room count and what you pay](/docs/billing/your-room-count).

## Tax

Prices exclude tax. Any sales tax or value added tax (VAT) that applies comes on top. Under the Terms, it is yours to pay.

## How you pay

Stripe hosts the payment page and holds your card. MAYA never sees the card number. You can always pay by card. US bank debit (ACH) is not accepted, and MAYA does not send invoices for you to pay by hand. Stripe's page shows the ways you can pay.

## Over 500 rooms

Self-serve signup takes any whole number from 1 to 500 rooms. Above 500, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Over%20500%20rooms) and we set it up by hand.

## Will the price go up?

Under the Terms, we can change prices, brackets or plans by giving at least 30 days' notice. A change applies from the start of your first billing period that begins after the notice period, which on a yearly plan means your next renewal. If you do not agree, you can cancel before then.
