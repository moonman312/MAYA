<InPlainWords>
There are two ways in: click <Ui>Connect App</Ui> in the Cloudbeds Marketplace, or create an account at maya-rms.com, usually with a code. With Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations you connect your system, MAYA reads your booking history, you review what it found, and you choose when to go live. With Mews we connect it for you, there is no history import or review yet, and we switch it live when you ask. Your part is a few short screens, the import runs on its own, and nothing is sent to your system until you say so.
</InPlainWords>

## Two ways in

Which way you take depends on your property system.

<PmsTabs>
<PmsTab pms="cloudbeds">
Both ways work. The short one is the Cloudbeds Marketplace: click <Ui>Connect App</Ui> there, create your account, set up payment with the 7-day free trial, and you are in. You can also create an account first at maya-rms.com and connect Cloudbeds from inside MAYA, which may need a code (the payment screen tells you) and has no trial.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="thinkreservations">
One way: create an account at maya-rms.com, set up payment (a code may be needed), then connect by signing in to ThinkReservations from inside MAYA. There is no Marketplace hand-over for ThinkReservations, and no trial unless a code grants one.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="mews">
Mews is connected by us, not from a screen. Pay first at maya-rms.com, choosing <Ui>Mews</Ui>, then [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Connecting%20Mews) and we connect it with you, using keys from your own Mews dashboard. You are welcome to email before paying if you want to talk first. The connect screen inside MAYA lists Mews under <Ui>Coming soon</Ui>, so there is nothing for you to click there. Read [Mews: what works today](/docs/connect/mews) first.
</PmsTab>
</PmsTabs>

## What you need

- **A login for your property system.** For Cloudbeds, a login that can install apps from the Marketplace. For ThinkReservations, your ThinkReservations sign-in. For Mews, nothing to click: we connect it.
- **A work email and a password** of at least 8 characters. The person who creates the account and pays becomes the property's Hotel Admin, with full control, including inviting the rest of the team.
- **A card.** Stripe holds it and MAYA never sees it. US bank debit (ACH) is not accepted, and there is no paying by invoice.
- **Some booking history**, if you want MAYA to build your first rules. Starter rules need about 60 days of past stays. With less, occupancy and booking window rules still work from day one, and you write them yourself.
- **Maybe a code.** The payment screen tells you whether your system needs one. Cloudbeds Marketplace arrivals never do. Read [Codes and the free trial](/docs/pay/codes-and-the-free-trial).
- **A plan for your old revenue tool**, if you use one. Nothing is imported from it. It can keep running while MAYA simulates, but switch it off before you go live, or the two work against each other on your base rate. Read [switching from another revenue tool](/docs/start/is-maya-right-for-you#switching-from-another-revenue-tool).

Nothing is installed anywhere: MAYA is a website. With Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations the connection is an approval or a sign-in on their site. With Mews we use keys from your Mews dashboard.

## Way 1: from the Cloudbeds Marketplace

<Steps>
<Step title="Connect from the Marketplace">
In Cloudbeds, open the Marketplace, find MAYA and click <Ui>Connect App</Ui>. Cloudbeds shows you what MAYA will be able to see and do, and you approve. Cloudbeds then hands you to MAYA. At this point MAYA has read your property's name, time zone and currency, and nothing else. If your Cloudbeds login covers several properties, all of them are connected in one go.

<Callout kind="good-to-know" title="The hand-over link lasts 24 hours">
If you come back later and see "That connection link has expired", nothing is lost. Go back to the Marketplace and click <Ui>Connect App</Ui> again.
</Callout>
</Step>
<Step title="Create your account, or sign in">
You land on a sign-up page whose subtitle reads "Your Cloudbeds property is connected. Create your account to finish setting it up." Type your email, choose a password, confirm it, tick the box that reads <Ui>I agree to the Terms of Service and acknowledge the Privacy Policy</Ui>, and click <Ui>Create Account</Ui>. You may be asked to confirm your email first: the screen says "Check your email" and, in that case, "Your Cloudbeds property is saved and will be waiting."

Already have a MAYA account? Switch to <Ui>Sign in</Ui> instead. The property attaches to whichever account signs in, and that account becomes its Hotel Admin.
</Step>
<Step title="Set up payment">
The next screen is headed with your property's name and the words "is connected". The line under the heading says how many days the free trial lasts (7). Type your room count in <Ui>How many rooms?</Ui>, any whole number from 1 to 500. Pick <Ui>Monthly</Ui> or <Ui>Yearly</Ui> and click <Ui>Set up payment</Ui>. Stripe's own page takes the card.

With the trial, the price panel reads "Nothing today" and names the date of your first charge. You can cancel any time before then. No code is needed this way. The box labelled <Ui>Your code (optional)</Ui> can be left blank, or you can type a code if we gave you one.

While you do this, MAYA has already started reading your booking history in the background.

<Callout kind="good-to-know">
If you leave Stripe's page before finishing, you come back to the same screen with the note "No charge was made" and can pick up where you left off. If several properties came over together, the screen is headed <Ui>Property 1 of 3</Ui> and you pay for them one at a time, with the same card offered for each. <Ui>Not now</Ui> sets a property aside for later.
</Callout>
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a path">
Two buttons: <Ui>Hold My Hand</Ui>, marked <Ui>(Recommended)</Ui>, and <Ui>Let Me Drive</Ui>. Hold My Hand asks five short optional questions, then walks you through what MAYA found in your history. Let Me Drive goes straight to your dashboard and skips both. Your history is read and your starter rules are built either way. A blue banner on the dashboard links to the review only while it has cards for you to answer. The review, where the go-live button sits, is always at <AppLink to="review">maya-rms.com/onboarding/review</AppLink>. [Hold My Hand or Let Me Drive](/docs/review/hold-my-hand-or-let-me-drive) compares them.
</Step>
<Step title="Answer five optional questions">
The header reads <Ui>A few quick questions · 1 of 5 · all optional</Ui>. They ask you to confirm your property's name, to give a rough cost of turning over a room, the lowest rate you would take on a slow night, the most you would ever charge, and how you feel about your current pricing. Skip any one with <Ui>Skip this question</Ui>, or all of them with <Ui>Skip the rest →</Ui>. Your lowest answer becomes every room type's floor, and your highest becomes the ceiling of every room type that has not sold above it. The import's progress bar sits under the questions. [The five questions](/docs/review/the-five-questions) explains each one.
</Step>
<Step title="Wait for the import, then review">
The progress page shows how many room nights (one room for one night) have been read, how many years they cover, and the oldest stay. It says you can close the page, and you can: the import runs on our side. When there is something to look at, the page moves on to the review by itself.

The review has two passes. First, <Ui>First: does this match reality?</Ui>: MAYA shows what it found that needs a yes or no from you, such as a stretch when you seem to have been closed, or a room type that may not be a room. Then <Ui>Recommendations from your data</Ui>: a strip showing which room types count as rooms, and the starter rules when you have about 60 days of history. Untick what is not a room, then click <Ui>Finish</Ui> to go to your dashboard. Nothing is sent to your system from here. [Checking what MAYA found](/docs/review/checking-what-maya-found) walks through the cards.

Where your answers left a floor or ceiling open, the import fills it from your own rates, if that room type has enough history: at least 30 nights for a floor and 200 for a ceiling. It skips a room type that may not be a room. A limit still at $1.00 or $99,999.99 stops nothing in most currencies (in yen or rupiah, the default ceiling can hold real rates down), and shows in the <Ui>Guardrails</Ui> column of the <Ui>Rate Simulator</Ui> tab. To set one, answer the floor and ceiling questions again, or use <Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui> on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab, before or after going live. [Floors and ceilings](/docs/review/floors-and-ceilings) has the detail.
</Step>
<Step title="Go live when you are ready">
On the review screen, <Ui>Turn them on for real</Ui> opens a dialog headed <Ui>Send prices to Cloudbeds?</Ui>. Click <Ui>Go live</Ui> and, from the next cycle, MAYA sends the prices for tonight and the next 395 nights. Beside the button the screen says "Or leave them in simulation and watch for a while. That works too." Only a General Manager or Hotel Admin can go live. The <Ui>Turn them on for real</Ui> button only appears once MAYA has built starter rules from your history (about 60 days of it). Without them, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Go%20live) and we switch the property live. The review stays at <AppLink to="review">maya-rms.com/onboarding/review</AppLink>, so you can come back to the button whenever you are ready. [Going live](/docs/live/going-live) covers what happens next. The button stays on the review after anyone clicks <Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui> on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Way 2: create an account at maya-rms.com, usually with a code

<Steps>
<Step title="Create your account">
get-maya.com is the website about MAYA. The app you sign in to lives at maya-rms.com. Go to maya-rms.com and click <Ui>Create your account</Ui> (the <Ui>Continue to payment</Ui> button under the price calculator on get-maya.com takes you to the same page). Type your email, choose a password of at least 8 characters, confirm it, tick the Terms box, and click <Ui>Create Account</Ui>. You may be asked to confirm your email.

<Callout kind="good-to-know">
The room count you typed into the calculator on get-maya.com is not carried into the app. You type it again on the payment screen.
</Callout>
</Step>
<Step title="Set up payment">
The screen is headed <Ui>Let's get you set up</Ui>. Four short choices:

1. **Rooms.** Type your room count in <Ui>How many rooms?</Ui>. The price panel shows your price before you commit to anything.
2. **Monthly or yearly.** Pick <Ui>Monthly</Ui> or <Ui>Yearly</Ui>.
3. **Your system.** Under <Ui>Which property management system do you use?</Ui> pick <Ui>Cloudbeds</Ui>, <Ui>Mews</Ui>, <Ui>Think Reservations</Ui> or <Ui>Something else</Ui>.
4. **Your code.** The field reads <Ui>Your code</Ui> when your system needs one, and <Ui>Your code (optional)</Ui> when it does not. <Ui>Something else</Ui> always needs one.

Then click <Ui>Continue to payment</Ui> and Stripe takes the card. There is no trial this way unless your code grants one. [Setting up payment](/docs/pay/setting-up-payment) covers the trial, codes and every message on this screen.
</Step>
<Step title="Connect your property system">
The next screen is headed <Ui>Connect your property system</Ui>. It shows a card for each system you can connect yourself, reading <Ui>Sign in with your Cloudbeds account</Ui> or <Ui>Sign in with your Think Reservations account</Ui>, with <Ui>Connect →</Ui> beside it. Click it, sign in on their site, and you are brought straight back. Mews sits under <Ui>Coming soon</Ui> with <Ui>Not yet available</Ui>: we connect it for you.

Once you are back, MAYA reads your property's name, time zone and currency, renames the property after it, makes you its Hotel Admin, and starts the history import.

<Callout kind="good-to-know">
The sign-in link to your system is valid for 15 minutes. If you take longer, you see "That sign-in link ran out after 15 minutes. Start again from MAYA." Nothing was set up, so start again. If your Cloudbeds or ThinkReservations login covers several properties, this step refuses and asks you to email us: from inside MAYA, one property is connected at a time.
</Callout>
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a path, review, go live">
From here the two ways are the same: choose <Ui>Hold My Hand</Ui> or <Ui>Let Me Drive</Ui>, answer the five optional questions, let the import finish, review what MAYA found, and go live when you are ready. For ThinkReservations the go-live dialog is headed <Ui>Send prices to Think Reservations?</Ui>. A Mews property has no history import, so there is no review to wait for and no starter rules. There is no go-live button for a Mews property today, because the button sits with the starter rules. Going live would not change anything in Mews anyway.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Your first day

With Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations, your own part is a few screens: create the account, pay, connect, the five optional questions and the review. The import runs on its own while you do them, and takes as long as your system's history needs ([how long it takes](/docs/review/the-history-import#how-long-it-takes)). You can close the page. Your current bookings come in first, so the calendar fills with real occupancy before the older years are read. About every five minutes MAYA runs your rules and writes the result in the <Ui>Change Log</Ui> tab, sending nothing. Once the import has read your last three years (or all your history, if you have less), a blue banner links to the review if there is anything to check. If there is nothing, open the review at <AppLink to="review">maya-rms.com/onboarding/review</AppLink>. [The history import](/docs/review/the-history-import) has the whole first day, step by step, including what each property system reports about your room counts.

## Simulation first, in one line

Every property starts in simulation: MAYA runs every rule and writes every change in the log, but sends nothing to your system until a General Manager or Hotel Admin clicks <Ui>Turn them on for real</Ui>, or asks us to switch the property live where there is no button. There is no rush. Watch for a day or a month. [Simulation mode](/docs/live/simulation-mode) says what changes when you go live.
