## Sign in and account

<Group>

- **"Sign in to MAYA"** and **"Create your MAYA account"**: two modes of the one sign-in card. The links "New to MAYA? Create your account" and "Already have an account? Sign in" switch between them.
- **"Forgot password?"**: under the <Ui>Sign In</Ui> button. It opens **"Reset your password"**, with "Type the email you sign in with, and we'll send you a link to set a new password." and a <Ui>Send reset link</Ui> button. **"Remembered it? Sign in"** goes back. [Reset your password](/docs/recipes/reset-your-password)
- **"Welcome back."**: the ordinary sign-in intro.
- **"Set a password and you're on your way. Your property comes next."**: the create-account intro. After the account, you pay, then connect.
- **"Your Cloudbeds property is connected. Create your account to finish setting it up."**: you arrived from the Cloudbeds Marketplace. Create an account, or sign in to attach the property to an account you already have.
- **"Your Cloudbeds connection is active again. Sign in to pick up where you left off."**: you pressed <Ui>Connect App</Ui> in the Marketplace for a property MAYA already had. Sign in; everything is as you left it. [Connection lost](/docs/wrong/connection-lost)
- **"Passwords don't match."**: retype both password fields.
- **"That email already has an account. Sign in instead."**: the card has switched to sign-in mode. [Account and billing problems](/docs/wrong/account-and-billing-problems)
- **"Invalid login credentials"**: wrong email or password. Passwords are at least 8 characters. Forgotten it? Click <Ui>Forgot password?</Ui>. [Account and billing problems](/docs/wrong/account-and-billing-problems)
- **"Check your email"**: after creating an account, a confirmation link is on its way. Open it, then come back and sign in. **"Your Cloudbeds property is saved and will be waiting."** means the Marketplace property is kept for you meanwhile.
- **"Check your email"** with **"If name@yourhotel.com has a MAYA account, a link to set a new password is on its way. Open it in this browser. The link works once."**: you asked for a reset. The words are the same whether or not the address has an account. No email after a few minutes? Check spam. [Reset your password](/docs/recipes/reset-your-password)
- **"Sending..."**: the reset link is being asked for. It takes a moment.
- **"Back to sign in"**: returns you to the card after either "Check your email" message.
- **"Set a new password"** with **"Choose a new password for"** your address: the page the reset link opens. Type the new password twice and press <Ui>Save new password</Ui>.
- **"Checking your link…"**: the reset link is being checked. Wait a moment.
- **"This link has expired or was already used. If you set your new password already, sign in. If not, ask for a new link."**, on the "Set a new password" page: each reset link works once and runs out. Sign in, or ask for a new link. [Reset your password](/docs/recipes/reset-your-password)
- **"Open this link in the browser where you asked for it. Or ask for a new link from this one."**: the reset link was opened in a different browser or on another device. Open it where you asked for it, or ask again from here.
- **"We couldn't check this link just now. Reload the page to try again."**, on the "Set a new password" or "Set your password" page: the check got no answer. Reload; the link still works.
- **"That's the password you have now. Choose a different one."**: pick a password you do not use today.
- **"That password is too easy to guess. Choose a different one."**: pick a less common password.
- **"Could not save your new password. Try again, or ask for a new link from the sign-in page."**: the new password did not save. Press <Ui>Save new password</Ui> again. If it fails again, ask for a new link.
- **"Your new password is set. Taking you to MAYA…"**: done. MAYA opens, signed in.
- **"Could not finish connecting your property."**: the Marketplace hand-over failed after sign-in. Go back to the Marketplace and press <Ui>Connect App</Ui> again.
- **"That connection link is not valid."**: the hand-over link is broken. Press <Ui>Connect App</Ui> in the Marketplace again.
- **"That connection has already been claimed."**: another account owns the property. Ask them to invite you. [Account and billing problems](/docs/wrong/account-and-billing-problems)
- **"That connection link has expired. Reconnect the app from the Cloudbeds Marketplace."**: the link lasts 24 hours. Press <Ui>Connect App</Ui> again.
- **"Sign in to finish connecting."**: sign in first, then the property attaches.
- **"Before you continue"**: the Terms of Service have a new version. Tick "I agree to the Terms of Service and acknowledge the Privacy Policy" and press <Ui>Continue</Ui>. You are asked once. [The Terms checkbox](/docs/account/the-terms-checkbox)
- **"Tick the box to agree."**: the checkbox is empty.
- **"The terms have been updated. Reload the page to see them."**: a tab was open across a new version. Reload.
- **"We couldn't save that"** (please try again): the acceptance did not save. Press <Ui>Continue</Ui> again.
- **"Set your password"**: the invitation page. Set a password, tick the box, press <Ui>Set password & continue</Ui>.
- **"Verifying your invite…"**: the link is being checked. Wait a moment.
- **"This link has expired or was already used. If you set your password already, sign in. If not, email us for a new link."**, on the "Set your password" page: the invitation link works once and runs out. If you already set your password with it, click <Ui>sign in</Ui>. If not, click <Ui>email us</Ui>, which writes to us from your own email, and we send a new link to the invited address. [Inviting and removing people](/docs/team/inviting-and-removing-people)
- **"Password must be at least 8 characters."**: choose a longer one.
- **"Password set. Redirecting to the app…"**: done. The dashboard opens.
- **"Hotel not accessible."**: you tried to open a property you are not a member of.
- **"Couldn't switch to Sea View Inn. You're still on The Harbour Inn. Try again in a moment."**: under the <Ui>Property</Ui> dropdown, with your properties' names. The switch did not go through and nothing changed. Try again, and reload the page if it keeps failing. [Several properties on one account](/docs/account/several-properties)
- **"Sign Out"**: ends your session and returns to the sign-in page.

</Group>


## Payment, codes and checkout

<Group>

- **"Let's get you set up"**: the subscribe screen. Type your room count, pick monthly or yearly, and continue. [Setting up payment](/docs/pay/setting-up-payment)
- **"The Harbour Inn is connected"** (with your property's name) with **"Try MAYA free for 7 days. Nothing is charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel any time."**: a Cloudbeds Marketplace arrival with the trial. Press <Ui>Set up payment</Ui>. [Codes and the free trial](/docs/pay/codes-and-the-free-trial)
- **"Set up a payment method to start using MAYA."**: the same screen without a trial. Press <Ui>Continue to payment</Ui>.
- **"Card details are handled by Stripe. They never touch MAYA."**: Stripe hosts the card form.
- **"How many rooms?"** and **"A whole number between 1 and 500."**: type the number of guest rooms. Over 500 is not sold self-serve; email us.
- **"save 10%"** beside Yearly: from 21 rooms. **"pay once a year"**: 1 to 20 rooms pay 12 months at the monthly rate.
- **"Your code"** with **"MAYA is invite-only for now"** (you will have been given a code): a code is required for this property system. **"Your code (optional)"**: a code is not required; enter one if you have it.
- **"Checking…"**: the code is being checked.
- **"Enter your code to continue."**: the field is empty and a code is required.
- **"We don't recognize that code"** (check it for typos): not a code we issued. [Account and billing problems](/docs/wrong/account-and-billing-problems)
- **"That code has been turned off."**, **"That code has expired."**, **"That code has already been used the maximum number of times."**: the code no longer works. Ask us for a current one.
- **"This property already used that code."**: one redemption per property.
- **"Too many code attempts. Wait a few minutes and try again."**: 20 checks per 10 minutes.
- **"Your session expired"** (sign in again and retry): your sign-in lapsed while the page was open.
- **"Couldn't check that code"** (try again in a moment) and **"That code didn't work."**: the check failed. Try again.
- **"14 days free, then your normal rate."**, **"20% off for your first 3 months."** and similar green lines: what a valid code grants.
- **"No charge was made"** (you left checkout before finishing): you closed Stripe's page. Nothing was taken. Continue when ready.
- **"Accept the Terms of Service to continue."**: tick the Terms box on the screen that appears, and checkout continues by itself.
- **"Tell us how many rooms you have"** (any number from 1 to 500): the room count is missing.
- **"This property already has a subscription. Manage it at /account/billing."**: one subscription per property. Open <Ui>Billing</Ui>.
- **"Your subscription is on hold. Email us at info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com and we'll get it running again."**: the property's subscription is paused in Stripe, so a new one is not started beside it. [Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Paused%20subscription).
- **"Too many checkout attempts. Wait a few minutes and try again."**: 10 checkout starts per 10 minutes.
- **"That property isn't yours to pay for."**: you are not a manager of that property.
- **"We couldn't start checkout"** (please try again in a moment): Stripe could not be reached. Try again.
- **"Payment received"** with **"Setting up your account"**: Stripe has confirmed; MAYA is catching up. It moves on by itself. **"Your payment went through, but our side is taking longer than usual to catch up."** and **"...confirming it here has stalled."**: press <Ui>Check again</Ui>, or leave and sign in later. You have not been charged twice. [Account and billing problems](/docs/wrong/account-and-billing-problems)
- **"Property 2 of 3"**: a group of Cloudbeds properties is paid for one at a time. [Paying for a group](/docs/pay/paying-for-a-group)
- **"Not now"** (set this property up later): skip this property for now. It appears under <Ui>Properties not set up yet</Ui> on the billing page.
- **"This is the only property left to set up."**, **"This property is already set up."**, **"This property is already paid for."**, **"This property isn't waiting on a Marketplace setup."**: "Not now" does not apply to this property.
- **"Manage billing or cancel"**: opens Stripe for a property that is paid for but not yet connected. **"Cancels at the end of the period"**: that property is already set to cancel.

</Group>


## Connecting your property system

<Group>

- **"Connect your property system"**: pick Cloudbeds or Think Reservations and press <Ui>Connect →</Ui>. Mews shows **"Coming soon"** and **"Not yet available"**: we connect Mews for you. [Which systems work](/docs/connect/which-systems-work)
- **"Sign in with your Cloudbeds account"**: you sign in on Cloudbeds' own site, nothing to install.
- **"Payment is needed before connecting a PMS."**: set up payment first, then connect.
- **"Cloudbeds needs an access code to connect"** (use the system you signed up with, or get in touch): you paid without a code for this system. Email us. [Codes and the free trial](/docs/pay/codes-and-the-free-trial)
- **"This Cloudbeds login covers 3 properties"** (MAYA signs one property up at a time): a group login cannot connect from inside MAYA. Cloudbeds groups connect from the Marketplace; ThinkReservations groups are set up by us. Email us. [Add a second property](/docs/recipes/add-a-second-property)
- **"We hit a snag connecting your property"**: the connection did not finish. Read the sentence under it, then press <Ui>Try again</Ui>. Sentences you may see: "We connected to your account but couldn't read your property details", "Could not create your property. Try again in a moment.", "Could not store your connection securely. Try again in a moment.", "This connection link belongs to a different account. Please sign in and try again." [Cloudbeds](/docs/connect/cloudbeds)
- **"Cloudbeds connection failed"** with **"Return to Command Center"**: the sign-in at Cloudbeds (or ThinkReservations) did not finish. Start again the way you started. The sentence under the heading gives the reason. [Reconnect and disconnect](/docs/connect/reconnect-and-disconnect)
- **"Think Reservations connection failed"** with **"think does not support marketplace-initiated connections."**: the ThinkReservations sign-in took longer than 15 minutes, so its link ran out. Nothing is wrong and nothing has changed. Go back to the connect screen (or the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab, for a reconnect) and start again. [ThinkReservations](/docs/connect/thinkreservations)
- **"Cloudbeds did not report a property for this authorization."**: Cloudbeds sent no property with the connection. Press <Ui>Connect App</Ui> again for the right property.
- **A plain text error instead of a page**: the connect step refused in plain words, such as the payment or access code messages above. Read the words, then go back to MAYA.
- **"This login is for a different property."**: reconnect with a Cloudbeds login that reaches the same property. [Connection lost](/docs/wrong/connection-lost)
- **"Cloudbeds didn't answer. Try connecting again in a moment."**: Cloudbeds was unreachable. Try again.
- **"Reconnect this property from the Cloudbeds Marketplace."**: press <Ui>Connect App</Ui> there.

</Group>


## The import and the review

<Group>

- **"Import paused"**: the history import stopped after repeated errors and tries again by itself at the time the bar gives, from where it had got to. "We've been told" means an automatic alert reached our team. Nothing to do. [The history import](/docs/review/the-history-import)
- **"Import stopped"**: the history import stopped before it finished and does not try again by itself. With "We've been told.", our team has had an automatic alert. With "Email us and we'll restart it.", nothing reached us: email us. [The history import](/docs/review/the-history-import)
- **"Hold My Hand"** and **"Let Me Drive"**: the two paths. The first asks five optional questions and walks you through the review. [Hold my hand or let me drive](/docs/review/hold-my-hand-or-let-me-drive)
- **"A few quick questions · 1 of 5 · all optional"** and **"Skip the rest →"**: every question can be skipped. [The five questions](/docs/review/the-five-questions)
- **"Did we get your property's name right?"**: whatever you type wins. **"That property name is already taken"**: add your city or neighbourhood.
- **"Your floor of $200 wasn't saved for Deluxe King: its ceiling is $150, and a floor can't be above the ceiling."** or **"Your ceiling of $150 wasn't saved for Suite: its floor is $180, and a ceiling can't be below the floor."**: every other room type took your answer, and the one named kept its own number. Type another number, or skip the question to leave it that way. [The five questions](/docs/review/the-five-questions)
- **"Your ceiling price needs to be above your floor price of $80."** or **"Your floor price needs to be below your ceiling price of $300."**: the answer clashes with the other one you gave, so nothing was saved. Type another number or skip the question. [The five questions](/docs/review/the-five-questions)
- **The blue card ending "Review →"**: your history has been read and there are findings to look at. Press it. [Checking what MAYA found](/docs/review/checking-what-maya-found)
- **"First: does this match reality?"** and **"Recommendations from your data"**: the two passes of the review screen.
- **"Is "Meeting Room" actually a room?"**: confirming takes it out of occupancy and your billed room count. [Room types and rooms out of service](/docs/watch/room-types-and-rooms-out-of-service)
- **"Were you closed ... → ...?"** with **"Yes, we were closed"** / **"No, we were open"**, and **"Is your property normally closed ...?"** with **"Yes, that's our season"**: confirming keeps that stretch out of comparisons. Dismissed dates are not asked again. [Similar nights](/docs/rules/similar-nights)
- **"Hid duplicate room type"** with **"Good call"** / **"Undo"**: an empty duplicate was hidden. Press <Ui>Undo</Ui> to bring it back. After <Ui>Good call</Ui>, email us. [Checking what MAYA found](/docs/review/checking-what-maya-found)
- **"Some "Standard" rates look like typos"** (with your room type's name), **"Some stays have a $0 rate"**, **"Some old stays reference deleted room types"**: notes about your history. Nothing to do.
- **"Finding was already resolved"**: another tab or a colleague answered that card first. Reload.
- **"Only a Revenue Manager or above can answer this."** on a review card: your role is below Revenue Manager, so the card stays. Ask a Revenue Manager or above to answer it. [Roles](/docs/team/roles)
- **"Only a Revenue Manager or above can finish the review."** with **"Go to my dashboard"**: <Ui>Finish</Ui> needs a Revenue Manager or above, so the review is not marked done and the dashboard banner stays. The link opens the dashboard anyway. [Checking what MAYA found](/docs/review/checking-what-maya-found)
- **"Couldn't finish the review. Try again."** or **"Your review couldn't be marked as finished. Reload the page and try again."**: <Ui>Finish</Ui> did not save, so you are still on the review. Press it again, reloading first for the second one.
- **"We're counting 3 room types as rooms"** (anything here that isn't?): untick anything nobody sleeps in.
- **"While you were here, we built your first pricing rules"**: your starter rules, running in simulation. [Your starter rules](/docs/review/your-starter-rules)
- **"Turn them on for real"**: go live. Needs General Manager or Hotel Admin. **"Or leave them in simulation and watch for a while. That works too."**: nothing is sent until you press it. [Going live](/docs/live/going-live)
- **"✓ Live: your rules are now managing prices"**: you are live.
- **"Send prices to Cloudbeds?"** with **"Go live"** / **"Not yet"**: the confirm dialog. "MAYA starts sending its prices for the next 396 nights to Cloudbeds on the next cycle, in about 5 minutes." and "Each price it sends replaces that night's rate in Cloudbeds, and a night is sent again whenever its price changes."
- **"Going live sends these rates to your PMS automatically. You're confirming you've reviewed your rules and limits (Terms 3.3)."**: what your click confirms.
- **"Go live?"** with **"MAYA doesn't send rates to Mews yet, so nothing is sent. The hotel only shows as live."**: Mews is read-only today.
- **"Taking pricing live requires General Manager access"** and **"You need admin access on this property to go live."**: ask a General Manager or Hotel Admin.
- **"Get suggestions from my data"**: re-reads your history and offers changes you accept one by one. The panel **"Here's exactly what happens"** explains it. When the read is done the button reads **"3 suggestions ready →"**. [Getting suggestions later](/docs/review/getting-suggestions-later)
- **"Reading your history…"**: a read you or a colleague started is running. Leave the tab open or come back later: the button changes by itself when it ends. [Getting suggestions later](/docs/review/getting-suggestions-later)
- **"Your last read paused"** and **"Your last read stopped"**: the last read stopped before it finished. "Paused" means it tries again by itself at the time given. "Stopped" means it will not: with "We've been told." our team has had an automatic alert, and with "Email us and we'll restart it." please email us. "...because your property system is no longer connected" means reconnect, then press the button again. [Getting suggestions later](/docs/review/getting-suggestions-later)
- **"Not available for Mews yet. MAYA can't read its booking history."**: <Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui> is greyed out on a Mews property, because MAYA does not read Mews history yet. [Mews](/docs/connect/mews)
- **"You need Revenue Manager access or higher to run this."**: ask a Revenue Manager, General Manager or Hotel Admin to press <Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui>. [Roles](/docs/team/roles)
- **"A read was just started. Let that one finish before starting another."**: <Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui> was pressed more than 5 times in 15 minutes on this property. Wait, then press it again. [Getting suggestions later](/docs/review/getting-suggestions-later)
- **"Tune "Busy nights"?"** with **"Make that change"** / **"Leave it as is"**: changes the rule's occupancy threshold in place. Accepting undoes nothing by itself, but from the next run nights that no longer meet the new threshold lose the rule's change.
- **"Remove "Quick pickup"?"** with **"Remove it"** / **"Turn it off, keep it"** / **"Leave it running"**: remove deletes the rule and undoes its changes; turn off pauses it and keeps them.
- **"Add a rule: "Warm-date bump"?"** with **"Add this rule"** / **"No thanks"**: adds a rule switched On.
- **"Set a floor for "Standard"?"** and **"Set a ceiling for "Standard"?"** with **"Set it to 80"**: type your own number or accept. [Floors and ceilings](/docs/review/floors-and-ceilings)

</Group>


## Rules and the rule builder

<Group>

- **"Pricing Rules"**: the Rules tab. Filters **"all"**, **"enabled"**, **"disabled"**. [The rule builder](/docs/rules/the-rule-builder)
- **"How rules behave"**: three short animations (a rule that raises and waits, an occupancy rule with no wait, and a stronger rule covering a weaker one), with **"Try the box"** to play them with the undo box ticked or unticked.
- **"+ Add a rule"** and **"Add Rule"**: open the builder, and save. A new rule is On.
- **"Rule name"**: required.
- **"Conditions"** with **"Matches when all of the following are true."**: every condition must hold at once. **"+ Add condition"** adds the next kind; four at most, one of each.
- **"Sellable occupancy (%)"**, **"Booking speed (recommended)"**, **"Booking window (days to stay)"**, **"Pickup count (advanced)"**: the four things a rule can measure. [The rule builder](/docs/rules/the-rule-builder)
- **"Compare"** with **"Greater than"** / **"Less than"**, and **"Threshold"**: strict comparisons. Greater than 80 means more than 80.
- **"Speed"**: Stalled, Much Slower Than Normal, Slower Than Normal, Normal, Faster Than Normal, Much Faster Than Normal, Surging. [Booking speed](/docs/rules/booking-speed)
- **"Measured over"** with **"Past day"** / **"Past week"** / **"Past month"**: the window of bookings counted.
- **"Then waits (advanced)"** with **"1 day"** to **"2 weeks"**, and **"(pickup holds it to 3 days)"**: how long the rule leaves a night alone after acting. A rule with a pickup row and a booking speed row waits the longer of their two waits.
- **"Lookback window"** and **"Pickup measures"** with **"Room nights (units)"** / **"Revenue (currency)"**: pickup count settings. [Pickup count](/docs/rules/pickup-count)
- **"Rate adjustment"**, **"Direction (required)"** with **"Increase the rate"** / **"Decrease the rate"**, **"Adjust by percent (%)"**, **"Adjust by fixed amount ($)"**: a rule has one amount, so typing in one box greys out the other.
- **"Apply to room types"** with **"Select all"** / **"Clear"**, and the tag **"not a room"**: which room types the rule measures and changes.
- **"Change prices on different room types"** with **"Measure"** and **"Change"**: watch one set, price another. [Measure one set of room types, change another](/docs/rules/measure-one-set-change-another)
- **"Add at least one condition with a valid operator and threshold."**: a threshold is empty or negative.
- **"Choose whether this rule increases or decreases the rate."**: the direction dropdown is blank.
- **"Enter a percent or a fixed amount."**: both amount boxes are empty.
- **"Enter the percentage as a positive number."** and **"Enter the amount as a positive number."**: the direction dropdown sets increase or decrease, so the amount is always positive. An amount of 0 is refused on save.
- **"Select at least one room type."**, **"Pick at least one room type to measure."**, **"Pick at least one room type to change."**, **"Invalid room types."**: fix the room type lists.
- **"Could not save the rule. Check your connection and try again."** and **"Could not save the rule. Try again in a moment."**: the save failed. A Viewer sees a red refusal here, because Viewers cannot change rules.
- **"Rule must include at least one valid condition."** and **"Invalid payload."**: the rule was sent without a usable condition. Add one.
- **"You don't have access to this property."**: your account has no property membership. Ask to be invited.
- **"Fired"** with **"3×"** or a dash: how many times the rule has acted, per night and room type, including changes that came off. **"What counts as a fire"** is its help.
- **"Occupancy above 80%"**, **"Booking window below 7 days"**, **"Pickup above 5 bookings"**, **"booking speed at least Much Faster Than Normal (past week), then waits 2 days"**: the conditions column. A pickup rule always says "bookings" here even when it measures revenue.
- **"Watches Standard, Deluxe · Changes Suite"**: the rule measures one set and changes another.
- **"+10%"**, **"-$25"**: the price change column. It always shows $.
- **"On"** / **"Off"**: the status switch. Off keeps the changes the rule made. [Off, delete or change](/docs/rules/off-delete-or-change)
- **"Stopped on 3 nights"** with **"Let it run again"**: you told the rule to stop on those nights from the amber banner. The link puts it back. [Rules that keep adjusting](/docs/rules/rules-that-keep-adjusting)
- **"Delete "Busy nights"?"** with **"Delete Rule and Undo Its Changes"** / **"Turn It Off and Keep Its Changes"** / **"Cancel"**, and **"Deleting cannot be undone."**: delete reverts every change the rule made; off keeps them.

</Group>


## The Rate Simulator

<Group>

- **"Make up a night and see what your rules would do to it."**: nothing here is saved or sent. [The Rate Simulator](/docs/rules/the-rate-simulator)
- **"Stay date"** with **"Sets days to arrival"** (and which date windows and weekdays apply): pick a future night.

## The Team page

<Group>

- **"Your team"** with **"Add someone"**, **"Their email"**, **"What they can do"**, **"Send invitation"**: invite a colleague. [Inviting and removing people](/docs/team/inviting-and-removing-people)
- **"2 of 4 seats used · 24 rooms"**: seats come with your room count.
- **"You've used every seat your plan includes. Remove someone below, or raise your room count on the billing page if the property has grown."**: the property is full.
- **"Invitation sent to name@yourhotel.com. They'll get an email with a link to join."**: an email with a link to set a password went out, and they are on the property already.
- **"Added. name@yourhotel.com already uses MAYA and will see this property next time they sign in."**: they have signed in to MAYA before, so no email went out. They are on the property already.
- **"Enter a valid email address."**, **"Pick a role."**: fix the form.
- **"You can't give someone a role above your own."** and **"That's above your own access level."**: a General Manager cannot grant or change Hotel Admin. [Roles](/docs/team/roles)
- **"That person is already on this property."**, **"They already have an invitation pending."**: nothing to do, or cancel the pending one first.
- **"Your plan covers 4 people at 24 rooms."**: the seat limit in full.
- **"Too many invitations"** (try again shortly): 20 invitations per property per hour.
- **"Could not send that invitation. Try again in a minute."**: nobody was added. Send it again in a minute. If it keeps failing, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Invitation%20failed).
- **"Could not send that invitation."**, **"Could not change that role."**, **"Could not remove them."**, **"Could not cancel that invitation."**: the connection dropped or the change did not save. Try again.

- **"That person isn't on this property."**, **"That invitation isn't on this property."**: reload.
- **"This is the property's only Hotel Admin. Make someone else one first."**: the last Hotel Admin cannot be demoted or removed.
- **"We couldn't load your team"** with **"Try again"**: the list could not load.
- **"Who can access this property is managed by its General Manager or Hotel Admin. Ask one of them if you need someone added or removed."**: your role cannot manage the team.
- **"Staff"**: an older name for the Viewer role. [Roles](/docs/team/roles)
- **Email "You have been invited to The Harbour Inn on MAYA"** with **"You're invited"** and **"Accept the invite"**: the invitation. The link works once and expires. If it has expired and they have not set a password yet, the email says to email us for a new link.
