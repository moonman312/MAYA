<InPlainWords>
An amber banner reading "MAYA has lost its connection to Cloudbeds" means your system stopped accepting MAYA's connection: a General Manager or Hotel Admin presses <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> and signs in at Cloudbeds again. When the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab reads Degraded instead, MAYA is still reading and sending, and one calm line says "Your Cloudbeds connection needs a refresh soon. Prices are still updating." While the connection is down nothing is read, priced or sent, and the rates already in your system, your rules, history and settings stay as they are. Mews connects with keys rather than a login, so email us and we re-enter them with you.
</InPlainWords>

## It says "MAYA has lost its connection to Cloudbeds"

Your system no longer accepts MAYA's connection. The usual causes: the app was uninstalled or disabled in your system, the connection was revoked there, or your system answered MAYA's calls with a refusal.

The banner shows when the connection reads Error or Disconnected. Here is what stops: reading bookings, running rules and sending prices, all of it. Disconnected stays that way until someone reconnects. Error does not have to: MAYA keeps trying on its own, waiting longer after each failed try (twice as long each time, up to an hour between tries). The first try that works turns the card back to Connected, and reading, pricing and sending carry on by themselves, including any prices that were held back. If your system goes on refusing, the card stays at Error until someone reconnects. What stays: the rates already in your system (MAYA reverts nothing), your rules, your history, your room type answers and your manual prices.

What to do: a General Manager or Hotel Admin presses <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> on the banner or on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab. It sends you to Cloudbeds to sign in and confirm, then back to MAYA. The banner text says the same: "Reconnecting takes one click and sends you to Cloudbeds to confirm. Your rules, history and settings are all untouched." Read [Reconnect your property system](/docs/recipes/reconnect-your-property-system).

After you reconnect, the next cycle reads your bookings again and sends any prices that were held back.

## It says "Your Cloudbeds connection needs a refresh soon"

The <Ui>Connection</Ui> card on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab reads Degraded. The full line is "Your Cloudbeds connection needs a refresh soon. Prices are still updating.", and it means what it says: MAYA is still reading your bookings and, when you are live, sending prices. Nothing has stopped and nothing is lost.

It usually clears by itself on the next read. If it is still there in an hour, a General Manager or Hotel Admin clicks the quiet <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> link at the end of the line and signs in with a login for this property only. It does no harm. Everyone else sees the line without the link.

## Why does the banner say to ask my General Manager?

Only a General Manager or Hotel Admin can reconnect Cloudbeds or ThinkReservations. Everyone else sees the same banner with "Ask this property's General Manager or Hotel Admin to reconnect it" and no button, so they can tell the right person. A reconnect link someone forwards does not get round this: it stops with "Reconnecting needs General Manager access or higher on this property." Read [Roles](/docs/team/roles).

## I uninstalled the app in Cloudbeds by accident

Go back to the Cloudbeds Marketplace and press <Ui>Connect App</Ui> for MAYA again. For a property that started in the Marketplace, you land on MAYA's sign-in page with "Your Cloudbeds connection is active again. Sign in to pick up where you left off." Sign in, and the property is back on the schedule. For a property you connected from inside MAYA, sign in to MAYA first as its General Manager or Hotel Admin: <Ui>Connect App</Ui> then brings you to its <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab with "Reconnected." Anyone else sees "This property is already in MAYA, so nothing new was added." and nothing changes. Nothing was deleted by the uninstall. The <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> button in MAYA works too.

## Someone removed MAYA's permission to update rates

Reading still works, so no connection banner appears. Instead the change log shows "Prices not reaching Cloudbeds" with "Cloudbeds won't let MAYA change Deluxe rates because MAYA doesn't have permission to update rates" and the action "Give MAYA permission to update rates in Cloudbeds, then reconnect on the PMS tab." Restore the permission in your system, then press <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab. Held prices go out on the next cycle. Read [Prices are not reaching your property system](/docs/wrong/prices-not-reaching-your-pms).

## Why did MAYA disconnect after my Cloudbeds subscription changed?

When Cloudbeds answers MAYA's calls with a refusal, or with its wording for an app that is no longer connected, MAYA marks the connection disconnected and shows the banner. A change to your Cloudbeds subscription or apps can cause that. Reconnect as above. If a change to your Cloudbeds login stops the connection, the same banner appears and <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> fixes it.

## ThinkReservations lost its connection, how would I tell?

MAYA shows the banner and the <Ui>Reconnect Think Reservations</Ui> button when the connection stops working and its status changes. Because ThinkReservations does not tell MAYA, that can lag: MAYA's reads fail first and are retried with a growing gap of up to an hour, and the banner follows once the status is marked. If <Ui>Last sync</Ui> on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab is hours old and no banner shows, press <Ui>Reconnect Think Reservations</Ui> there anyway, with a login for this hotel only. It is harmless on a working connection.

## It says "Mews needs its keys re-entered"

Mews uses keys rather than a sign-in, and only we can enter them. The banner has no button, and everyone on the property sees the same words, whatever their role: "Ask us to re-enter the Mews keys: email info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com." Click the address, or [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Mews%20keys), and it takes a couple of minutes together.

This banner shows only after we remove the keys. If the keys stop working on their own, MAYA does not mark the connection as lost, so no banner appears and the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab can keep reading "Connected". Watch <Ui>Last sync</Ui> on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab instead. If it is more than an hour old, email us.

## What Connected, Pending, Degraded, Error and Disconnected mean

The <Ui>Connection</Ui> card on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab shows one of these.

| Status | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Connected | Healthy. Bookings are read every cycle. | Nothing. |
| Pending | Connected but not yet paid for. Nothing is read or priced until the subscription starts. | Finish setting up payment. |
| Degraded | MAYA is still reading and sending. One calm line says "Your Cloudbeds connection needs a refresh soon. Prices are still updating." | If it is still there in an hour, reconnect with a login for this property only. It does no harm. |
| Error | Your system rejected MAYA's saved sign-in. Nothing is read, priced or sent while it lasts. MAYA keeps trying, up to an hour between tries, and the card goes back to Connected by itself once a try works. | If it has not cleared within a couple of hours, press <Ui>Reconnect</Ui>. Reconnecting sooner does no harm. |
| Disconnected | The connection is gone. The property is off the schedule. | Press <Ui>Reconnect</Ui>. |
| unknown | MAYA has no status on file. | Open the tab again in a moment, then reconnect if it stays. |

The banner appears for Error and Disconnected. Degraded shows the calm line instead. A Mews connection never reads Degraded or Error. It keeps reading Connected even while its reads fail, so on Mews watch <Ui>Last sync</Ui>.

## Why is "Last sync" old?

<Ui>Last sync</Ui> is the last time bookings were read successfully. On a healthy connection it is a few minutes old. After a failed read MAYA waits longer before each retry, doubling each time up to an hour, so a system that is down for a while leaves <Ui>Last sync</Ui> up to an hour behind before it catches up. A disconnected property stops reading altogether until someone reconnects. A paused subscription stops it too.

## Why does Health say "Having trouble"?

<Ui>Health (last 24 hours)</Ui> counts MAYA's requests to your system over the last day: "Healthy" when 98% or more succeeded, "Degraded" from 80%, "Having trouble" below that, with the figure beside it, for example "76% of 412 requests OK". "Having trouble" usually means your system was down or refusing for part of the day. If the connection card reads Connected and <Ui>Last sync</Ui> is recent, the trouble has passed. If not, reconnect.

"No recent activity" means no requests were recorded in the last 24 hours. On ThinkReservations and Mews the card reads "Not tracked for this system" instead, because MAYA keeps a log of its requests to Cloudbeds only. Read [Calendar and change log questions](/docs/wrong/calendar-and-log-questions).

## The log says "Reconnect on the PMS tab" but I see no banner

The banner shows only while the connection itself is marked as lost. When Cloudbeds refuses to let MAYA change rates, reading still works and the connection stays Connected, so the change log's line is the only prompt. MAYA reads the connection when the dashboard opens, when you switch property and when you open the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab, so a connection that broke while the page was open shows once you reload or open that tab. Open the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab: a General Manager or Hotel Admin always finds <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> there.

## After reconnecting, how do I know it worked?

A successful reconnect brings you back to the dashboard's <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab, on the property you reconnected, with a short note: "Reconnected." Close it with the ×. The banner stops showing once the connection reads Connected, and a few minutes later <Ui>Last sync</Ui> shows a fresh time.

If you land on a page with a sentence instead, the reconnect did not go through and nothing changed. The sentence says why, for example "This login is for a different property." or "That sign-in link ran out after 15 minutes. Start again from MAYA." Read [Messages you might see, explained](/docs/wrong/messages-explained).

## It says "This login is for a different property"

A property on Cloudbeds must be reconnected with a Cloudbeds login that reaches the same Cloudbeds property, however it was first connected. The login you used reaches a different one, so the reconnect stopped before anything changed: the old connection is exactly as it was. A General Manager or Hotel Admin presses <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> in MAYA again, then signs in at Cloudbeds with a login for this property. Any Cloudbeds login that can reach it works, a group login included, not only the one that first connected it. Three related messages: "Cloudbeds didn't answer. Try connecting again in a moment." (Cloudbeds was unreachable, so try again), "We couldn't check this login just now. Try connecting again in a moment." (try again) and "Reconnect this property from the Cloudbeds Marketplace." (use <Ui>Connect App</Ui> there). Read [Which login to use](/docs/connect/reconnect-and-disconnect#which-login-to-use) for what other logins do.

<Callout kind="careful" title="On ThinkReservations, reconnect with a login for this hotel only">
There is no screen for moving a MAYA property to a different property in your system. Cloudbeds reconnects check this for you. ThinkReservations reconnects do not: reconnecting with a login that reaches one other hotel moves MAYA onto it, and nothing on screen warns you. Always reconnect ThinkReservations with a login for this hotel only.
</Callout>

There is no disconnect button in MAYA. To disconnect, remove the app in Cloudbeds. Disconnecting does not cancel your subscription and deletes nothing.

## What happens to my prices while the connection is down?

Nothing is read, priced or sent. The rates already in your system stay exactly as they are. After you reconnect, the next cycle reads your bookings, prices again and sends what changed. Any price held back by a permission problem goes out then too.
