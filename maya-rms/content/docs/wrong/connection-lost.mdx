<InPlainWords>
An amber banner reading "MAYA has lost its connection to Cloudbeds" means your system stopped accepting MAYA's connection: a General Manager or Hotel Admin presses <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> and signs in at Cloudbeds again. The same banner can show while the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab reads Degraded, when MAYA is still reading and sending (reconnect if it is still there in an hour). While the connection is down nothing is read, priced or sent, and the rates already in your system, your rules, history and settings stay as they are. Mews connects with keys rather than a login, so email us and we re-enter them with you.
</InPlainWords>

## It says "MAYA has lost its connection to Cloudbeds"

Your system no longer accepts MAYA's connection. The usual causes: the app was uninstalled or disabled in your system, the connection was revoked there, or your system answered MAYA's calls with a refusal.

The same banner can show while the connection reads Degraded on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab. Then MAYA is still reading and sending, and nothing has stopped. If the banner is still there in an hour, reconnect with a login for this property only. It does no harm.

When the connection reads Error or Disconnected, here is what stops: reading bookings, running rules and sending prices, all of it, until someone reconnects. What stays: the rates already in your system (MAYA reverts nothing), your rules, your history, your room type answers and your manual prices.

What to do: a General Manager or Hotel Admin presses <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> on the banner or on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab. It sends you to Cloudbeds to sign in and confirm, then back to MAYA. The banner text says the same: "Reconnecting takes one click and sends you to Cloudbeds to confirm. Your rules, history and settings are all untouched." Read [Reconnect your property system](/docs/recipes/reconnect-your-property-system).

After you reconnect, the next cycle reads your bookings again and sends any prices that were held back.

Cloudbeds tells MAYA when you uninstall or disable the app, usually within seconds. Otherwise MAYA reads the refusal on its next call, within about five minutes. Either way the banner appears and the property leaves the schedule until someone reconnects. You can also reconnect from the Cloudbeds Marketplace by pressing <Ui>Connect App</Ui> again.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="thinkreservations">
ThinkReservations does not tell MAYA when access is revoked. MAYA finds out when its calls fail, and shows the banner once the connection's status changes. Until then the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab may still read "Connected" while <Ui>Last sync</Ui> falls behind. A refused rate write does not disconnect ThinkReservations; it appears in the change log as a permission problem instead.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="mews">
Mews connects with keys rather than a login, and we enter them for you. MAYA does not mark a Mews connection as lost when its reads fail, so the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab can keep reading "Connected" with no banner. <Ui>Health (last 24 hours)</Ui> always reads "No recent activity" on Mews, so <Ui>Last sync</Ui> is the one sign. If <Ui>Last sync</Ui> on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab is more than an hour old, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Mews%20keys).

The status changes to Disconnected only when we remove the keys. Then a General Manager or Hotel Admin sees "Mews needs its keys re-entered", with no button. Email us and we walk through it with you. Read [When the keys stop working](/docs/connect/mews#when-the-keys-stop-working).
</PmsTab>

## Why does the banner say to ask my General Manager?

Only a General Manager or Hotel Admin can reconnect. Everyone else sees the same banner with "Ask this property's General Manager or Hotel Admin to reconnect it" and no button, so they can tell the right person. Read [Roles](/docs/team/roles).

## I uninstalled the app in Cloudbeds by accident

Go back to the Cloudbeds Marketplace and press <Ui>Connect App</Ui> for MAYA again. You land on MAYA's sign-in page with "Your Cloudbeds connection is active again. Sign in to pick up where you left off." Sign in, and the property is back on the schedule. Nothing was deleted by the uninstall. The <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> button in MAYA works too.

## Someone removed MAYA's permission to update rates

Reading still works, so no connection banner appears. Instead the change log shows "Prices not reaching Cloudbeds" with "Cloudbeds won't let MAYA change Deluxe rates because MAYA doesn't have permission to update rates" and the action "Give MAYA permission to update rates in Cloudbeds, then reconnect on the PMS tab." Restore the permission in your system, then press <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab. Held prices go out on the next cycle. Read [Prices are not reaching your property system](/docs/wrong/prices-not-reaching-your-pms).

## Why did MAYA disconnect after my Cloudbeds subscription changed?

When Cloudbeds answers MAYA's calls with a refusal, or with its wording for an app that is no longer connected, MAYA marks the connection disconnected and shows the banner. A change to your Cloudbeds subscription or apps can cause that. Reconnect as above. If a change to your Cloudbeds login stops the connection, the same banner appears and <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> fixes it.

## ThinkReservations lost its connection, how would I tell?

MAYA shows the banner and the <Ui>Reconnect Think Reservations</Ui> button when the connection stops working and its status changes. Because ThinkReservations does not tell MAYA, that can lag: MAYA's reads fail first and are retried with a growing gap of up to an hour, and the banner follows once the status is marked. If <Ui>Last sync</Ui> on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab is hours old and no banner shows, press <Ui>Reconnect Think Reservations</Ui> there anyway, with a login for this hotel only. It is harmless on a working connection.

## It says "Mews needs its keys re-entered"

Mews uses keys rather than a sign-in, and only we can enter them. The banner has no button. [Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Mews%20keys), and it takes a couple of minutes together.

This banner shows only after we remove the keys. If the keys stop working on their own, MAYA does not mark the connection as lost, so no banner appears and the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab can keep reading "Connected". Watch <Ui>Last sync</Ui> on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab instead. If it is more than an hour old, email us.

## What Connected, Pending, Degraded, Error and Disconnected mean

The <Ui>Connection</Ui> card on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab shows one of these.

| Status | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Connected | Healthy. Bookings are read every cycle. | Nothing. |
| Pending | Connected but not yet paid for. Nothing is read or priced until the subscription starts. | Finish setting up payment. |
| Degraded | MAYA is still reading and sending. The lost-connection banner may show. | If it is still there in an hour, reconnect with a login for this property only. It does no harm. |
| Error | Your system rejected MAYA's saved sign-in. A person must reconnect. | Press <Ui>Reconnect</Ui>. |
| Disconnected | The connection is gone. The property is off the schedule. | Press <Ui>Reconnect</Ui>. |
| unknown | MAYA has no status on file. | Open the tab again in a moment, then reconnect if it stays. |

The banner appears for Degraded, Error and Disconnected. A Mews connection never reads Degraded or Error. It keeps reading Connected even while its reads fail, so on Mews watch <Ui>Last sync</Ui>.

## Why is "Last sync" old?

<Ui>Last sync</Ui> is the last time bookings were read successfully. On a healthy connection it is a few minutes old. After a failed read MAYA waits longer before each retry, doubling each time up to an hour, so a system that is down for a while leaves <Ui>Last sync</Ui> up to an hour behind before it catches up. A disconnected property stops reading altogether until someone reconnects. A paused subscription stops it too.

## Why does Health say "Having trouble"?

<Ui>Health (last 24 hours)</Ui> counts MAYA's requests to your system over the last day: "Healthy" when 98% or more succeeded, "Degraded" from 80%, "Having trouble" below that, with the figure beside it, for example "76% of 412 requests OK". "Having trouble" usually means your system was down or refusing for part of the day. If the connection card reads Connected and <Ui>Last sync</Ui> is recent, the trouble has passed. If not, reconnect.

"No recent activity" means no requests were recorded. For ThinkReservations and Mews it always reads that, because only Cloudbeds requests are recorded on this tab. Read [Calendar and change log questions](/docs/wrong/calendar-and-log-questions).

## The log says "Reconnect on the PMS tab" but I see no banner

The banner above the calendar appears once the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab has been opened in your session. Open the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab: the connection panel and the <Ui>Reconnect</Ui> button are always there.

## After reconnecting I landed on the dashboard with no message

That is normal. A successful reconnect brings you back to the dashboard, and the banner stops showing once the connection reads Connected. Open the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab to see the status and, a few minutes later, a fresh <Ui>Last sync</Ui>.

## It says "This login is for a different property"

A property that came through the Cloudbeds Marketplace must be reconnected with a Cloudbeds login that reaches the same Cloudbeds property. A General Manager or Hotel Admin presses <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> in MAYA, then signs in at Cloudbeds. Any Cloudbeds login that can reach that property works there, not only the one that first connected it. Two related messages: "Cloudbeds didn't answer. Try connecting again in a moment." (Cloudbeds was unreachable, so try again) and "Reconnect this property from the Cloudbeds Marketplace." (use <Ui>Connect App</Ui> there). Read [Which login to use](/docs/connect/reconnect-and-disconnect#which-login-to-use) for what other logins do.

<Callout kind="careful" title="Reconnect with a login for this property only">
There is no screen for moving a MAYA property to a different Cloudbeds property. But on a property you connected from inside MAYA, and on ThinkReservations, reconnecting with a login that reaches one other property moves MAYA onto it, and nothing on screen warns you. Always reconnect with a login for this property only.
</Callout>

There is no disconnect button in MAYA. To disconnect, remove the app in Cloudbeds. Disconnecting does not cancel your subscription and deletes nothing.

## What happens to my prices while the connection is down?

Nothing is read, priced or sent. The rates already in your system stay exactly as they are. After you reconnect, the next cycle reads your bookings, prices again and sends what changed. Any price held back by a permission problem goes out then too.
