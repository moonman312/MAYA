<InPlainWords>
Give a new price two cycles, about ten minutes, then open the <Ui>Change Log</Ui> tab. A problem that needs you, such as a lost connection or a rate rule in your system, sits at the top at once as "Rates not reaching Cloudbeds", with the cause and what to do. One that clears on its own is shown only if it lasts 2 hours, and a time zone mismatch usually not at all. Nothing is sent while you are simulating, nothing is ever sent to Mews, a price of 0 is never sent, and a night past the 396-night window (tonight and the next 395) waits until it enters it.
</InPlainWords>

## I went live and nothing is being sent

Check these in order.

1. **Wait two cycles.** Prices go out about every five minutes, and only prices that changed since the last send. The first send to a night MAYA has never sent to also waits until a fresh read of your own rates has succeeded, within the last hour, so that MAYA writes over a rate it has freshly read. The first live cycle reads your rates before it sends.
2. **Check that you are live.** There is no badge on the dashboard. Open the review page and find the card headed "While you were here, we built your first pricing rules". The green pill "✓ Live: your rules are now managing prices" in it means you are live, and the button <Ui>Turn them on for real</Ui> means you are simulating. The card appears only when MAYA built starter rules for you at setup, which needs at least 60 days of booking history. If your review page has no such card, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Am%20I%20live) and we check. [Going live](/docs/live/going-live#where-the-button-is) says how to go live without the card, and [Tell whether you are live or in simulation](/docs/recipes/tell-if-you-are-live) has the other clues.
3. **Look for a problem item.** On the <Ui>Change Log</Ui> tab, an ongoing problem sits above every run, headed "Rates not reaching Cloudbeds" (or "Rates not reaching Think Reservations"), with the cause and what to do. [When your property system does not accept a price](/docs/live/when-your-pms-does-not-accept-a-price) explains each.
4. **Check the connection.** Open the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab and read the <Ui>Connection</Ui> card. Error or Disconnected means your system stopped accepting MAYA's connection. Nothing is read from it or sent to it until a General Manager or Hotel Admin reconnects. Do not wait for the amber banner above the calendar, "MAYA has lost its connection to Cloudbeds": it shows only once the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab has been opened in your visit. [Connection lost](/docs/wrong/connection-lost) explains each status, and what an old <Ui>Last sync</Ui> means.
5. **Check the subscription.** A red banner reading "MAYA has paused work on this property" means prices are not worked out or sent until the subscription is running again. Read [Account and billing problems](/docs/wrong/account-and-billing-problems).

## MAYA shows a new price but Cloudbeds still has the old one

Five reasons, most common first.

- **It has not gone yet.** A price changed in the last few minutes goes on the next cycle. The first send to a night waits for a fresh read of your rates.
- **You are simulating.** Prices are worked out and shown, and never sent. See the check above.
- **A problem is holding it.** Look for "Rates not reaching Cloudbeds" at the top of the change log. The item says what is wrong and what to do.
- **Cloudbeds took it and never finished.** Cloudbeds applies a rate write as a job. If a job is still unfinished 45 minutes later, MAYA treats it as not applied and sends the night again. You are told only if it keeps happening.
- **ThinkReservations applies it later.** ThinkReservations accepts a write and applies it afterwards. MAYA confirms it on the next hourly read.

## My base rate moved but my non-refundable rate did not

MAYA writes one rate per room type and no other: the base rate in Cloudbeds, the Best Available rate type in ThinkReservations. Your non-refundable rate, like every other plan, moves only by your system's own rules. MAYA never writes to it.

Check three things in your system.

1. **The base rate itself.** Open the night. If the base rate still shows the old price, the new one has not landed yet. Read [the five reasons above](#maya-shows-a-new-price-but-cloudbeds-still-has-the-old-one).
2. **Whether the non-refundable rate follows the base.** A plan set to follow another is what both systems call derived. Only a derived plan moves when the rate it follows moves. A plan with prices of its own stays where you set it, whatever MAYA sends.
3. **What it follows, and by how much.** A derived plan follows one rate, by the amount set in its own settings. If it follows a different plan, or the amount is not what you expect, change that in your system.

At The Harbour Inn, "Non-refundable" follows Standard Rate at 10% off. On Fri, Nov 13 2026, "Busy nights" raises Standard from $150 to $165, and MAYA sends $165 to Standard Rate. Cloudbeds moves Non-refundable from $135 to $148.50 on its own. MAYA wrote only the $165.

To make your non-refundable rate move with MAYA's price, set it in your system to follow the rate MAYA writes to. Your system then works it out from each new base rate.

## Why did MAYA overwrite the rate I set in Cloudbeds?

On a live property, a rate you change in your system is kept as a manual price when all of these hold: the night is one MAYA had already sent to, MAYA's send there was confirmed, the send is at least 60 minutes old, and your rate differs from MAYA's price by more than rounding. The day card then reads "Changed in Cloudbeds · $180.00", rules that had already moved the night are paused, and MAYA does not write over it. Read [Rates changed in your property system](/docs/watch/rates-changed-in-your-pms).

Your change was written over for one of these reasons:

- **Within the hour.** You changed it less than 60 minutes after MAYA's send. MAYA reads your rates about once an hour, and again before re-sending a night it last sent to over an hour ago, so a change made inside that hour can be written over when MAYA's own price for the night changes.
- **A night MAYA had never sent to.** Your rate there is read as the night's starting point, not as a manual price. Your rules work from it, and MAYA sends the result. This is also what happens to every rate you change while simulating.
- **A ThinkReservations night MAYA had not read back yet.** A send to ThinkReservations counts as confirmed only once MAYA's hourly read finds it. Until then a different rate there is not read as your change.
- **Nearly every night differs by the same ratio.** When 80% or more of the nights MAYA sent to (10 at least) differ from MAYA's price by one shared ratio (a system reporting prices with tax included, or a markup), MAYA reads that as your system's own arithmetic, not as edits, and keeps sending its own prices.

<Callout kind="good-to-know" title="Change prices in MAYA when you can">
A price typed on the <Ui>Calendar</Ui> tab is sent now, shows who set it, and pauses the rules already on that night at once. A change made in your system is picked up within the hour, and only on nights MAYA has already sent to.
</Callout>

## Why did MAYA price a night I closed in my system?

A night whose rate is 0 in your system, with no price typed in MAYA, is left unpriced. MAYA never opens a closed night at the floor.

The timing matters. MAYA reads your rates about once an hour and when your property's date changes. A night you close after MAYA has sent to it is read within the hour: its starting price becomes 0, the night is left unpriced, and any manual price on it is cleared. Between your change and that read, MAYA's price may still be there. A night closed before MAYA ever sent to it is left unpriced from the first run.

## Why was a night I set to 0 not sent?

MAYA does not send a price of 0 to any property system. A manual price of 0 is a comp night: no rule raises it, and the save confirmation says "Saved. MAYA doesn't send a price of 0 to Cloudbeds, so set the night to 0 there yourself." The change log carries an item that says the same until your system has the night at 0. Set it there yourself. Read [Make a night free](/docs/recipes/make-a-night-free).

## Why was a night 70 days out not sent?

MAYA prices and sends tonight and the next 395 nights, on your property's own calendar. A night 70 days out is not priced, refreshed or sent until it enters the window. A manual price you type further out is saved now and sent when the night enters the window, and the confirmation says so: "Saved. It will be sent when the date enters the 396-day push window."

## Every "Rates not reaching" sentence, and what to do

The item's second line names the cause, and the item itself says what to do. [When your property system does not accept a price](/docs/live/when-your-pms-does-not-accept-a-price) lists every one of those sentences with its meaning, the fix, and how MAYA retries.

A new price for tonight that never lands, while later nights go through, usually means your system and MAYA hold different time zones for your property. This one rarely shows in the change log. [The time zone case](/docs/live/when-your-pms-does-not-accept-a-price#the-time-zone-case) has what to check.

<Callout kind="not-yet">
There is no per-night "sent" or "confirmed" mark on the calendar, and no email or notification for a sending problem. Problems live in the change log only. The one email is for a lost connection, once it has been down about an hour. To confirm a price landed, open the night in your system.
</Callout>

<Example title="A minimum rate at The Harbour Inn">
Sam sets a minimum of $120 on Deluxe in Cloudbeds. MAYA's floor for Deluxe is $110. On a slow Tuesday, "Last minute" cuts Deluxe from $140 to $119. The floor lets that through, and Cloudbeds refuses it. The change log shows at once: "Rates not reaching Cloudbeds", "Cloudbeds refused MAYA's price for Deluxe because it breaks a rate rule set in Cloudbeds, such as a minimum or maximum rate.", "1 night, 1 room type · Still happening", and "Check the rate limits for this room type in Cloudbeds."

Priya types $120 on that night on the <Ui>Calendar</Ui> tab, and it is sent. Cloudbeds takes it. The item then reads "Ended" with the time, because a new price replaced the refused one.

There is no screen to change one room type's floor. So that later cuts are not refused, Sam lowers the Cloudbeds minimum to $110 to match MAYA's floor. Answering the floor question again with $120 would fix Deluxe but move every other room type too: Standard's floor would rise from $80 and Suite's would fall from $180. To raise only the Deluxe floor to $120, Priya would [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Floor%20and%20ceiling) with the room type and the number.
</Example>
