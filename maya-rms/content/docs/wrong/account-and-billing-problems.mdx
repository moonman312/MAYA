<InPlainWords>
Most sign-in and billing problems have a one-line fix, and the message on screen says what to do. There is no password reset link yet, so email us from the address you signed up with. A card check moves no money, so you were not charged twice. The billing page and the Team page need General Manager or Hotel Admin access, and a property that is not paid for never appears in the property dropdown.
</InPlainWords>

## I cannot sign in

The sign-in card shows "Invalid login credentials" when the email or password is wrong. Check the email address you signed up with and retype the password. Passwords are at least 8 characters.

If you have forgotten your password, there is no reset link yet. [Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Password%20reset) from the address you signed up with, and we send you a link to set a new one. Read [Reset your password](/docs/recipes/reset-your-password).

<Callout kind="not-yet">
There is no forgot-password link, no way to change your email address or password inside the app, no two-factor sign-in and no Google or single sign-on. Email us for a password reset.
</Callout>

## It says "That email already has an account. Sign in instead."

You pressed <Ui>Create Account</Ui> with an address that already has a MAYA login. The card has switched to sign-in mode for you. Sign in with that address. If a colleague created it and you do not have the password, ask them, or email us for a reset.

## I never got the confirmation email

You may be asked to confirm your address when you create an account. The card reads "Check your email" with "A confirmation link is on its way to" your address. Look in spam. For a Cloudbeds Marketplace arrival the property waits for you: "Your Cloudbeds property is saved and will be waiting." If nothing arrives after a few minutes, press <Ui>Back to sign in</Ui> and try to sign in. If that fails, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Confirmation%20email).

## The Cloudbeds connection link expired

The hand-over link from the Cloudbeds Marketplace lasts 24 hours. After that you see "That connection link has expired. Reconnect the app from the Cloudbeds Marketplace." Go back to the Marketplace and press <Ui>Connect App</Ui> again. A fresh link brings you back to MAYA.

## It says "That connection has already been claimed"

Another MAYA account already owns that Cloudbeds property. Ask whoever set it up to invite you from the <Ui>Team</Ui> page. If you set it up yourself with a different email, sign in with that one.

## A colleague already signed up for our property, how do I get in?

They invite you. On the <Ui>Team</Ui> page they enter your email, pick your role and press <Ui>Send invitation</Ui>. You get an email with a link to set a password and join. If you already have a MAYA account, you are added at once with no email, and the property appears the next time you open MAYA. Read [Inviting and removing people](/docs/team/inviting-and-removing-people).

## The code field says "Your session expired"

The page has been open long enough for your sign-in to lapse. Sign in again and retype the code.

## My code is not recognised

The message under the field says why:

- "We don't recognize that code" (check it for typos): the code is not one we issued. Codes are letters, digits and dashes, 3 to 40 characters, and capitals do not matter.
- "That code has been turned off." or "That code has expired.": that code is no longer valid. Ask us for a current one.
- "That code has already been used the maximum number of times.": the same.
- "This property already used that code.": one redemption per property. Restarting a cancelled subscription cannot reuse the old code.
- "Too many code attempts. Wait a few minutes and try again.": 20 checks per person per 10 minutes.

Codes come from us. A property arriving from the Cloudbeds Marketplace needs none. Read [Codes and the free trial](/docs/pay/codes-and-the-free-trial).

## Why did signing in take me to a payment page?

Because a property attached to your account is connected but not paid for. A Cloudbeds Marketplace property is connected first and paid for second, so until payment is set up, signing in lands on the subscribe screen. Set up payment, or press "Not now" if it is one of several properties and you want to come back to it. Read [Setting up payment](/docs/pay/setting-up-payment).

## Why did the dashboard send me to onboarding?

Your account has no property that is set up and active yet. Onboarding is where you pay, connect and review. If a colleague has the property, ask them to invite you.

## The "Payment received" screen is stuck

After Stripe confirms, MAYA usually takes you straight on. When confirmation is slow you see "Payment received" with "Setting up your account" and a moving bar. After 25 seconds it reads "Your payment went through, but our side is taking longer than usual to catch up." with a <Ui>Check again</Ui> button, and after 5 minutes it stops checking.

Your payment went through and you have not been charged twice. You can leave the page and sign in again later; the property is waiting. The screen also says we will email you when it is ready. No such email exists today, so do not wait for one. If the screen is still stuck an hour later, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Payment%20received%20stuck).

## Was I charged twice?

No. MAYA checks your saved card twice with a zero-amount check that moves no money: once right after checkout, and again 48 hours after the subscription starts. Some banks briefly show a pending line that then disappears. It is never charged. The receipt for a real charge comes from Stripe.

## It says "Your saved card stopped working"

Your card did not pass one of the two card checks: the one right after checkout or the one 48 hours later. Nothing has been charged and nothing has failed yet, but the next charge will. The billing page says when it was checked and the bank's short reason. Press <Ui>Update card, receipts & cancellation</Ui> to open Stripe and update the card. The warning clears by itself when a payment goes through. MAYA keeps working meanwhile. Read [Cards, payments and invoices](/docs/billing/cards-payments-and-invoices).

## It says "You're billed for 20 rooms but running 24"

On every cycle MAYA adds up the rooms in your room types ticked as rooms. When that measured count is higher than the count you pay for, the banner appears, and an email with the same numbers goes to the address on your billing account. You have 7 days from that email to put it right. After the 7 days MAYA raises the count you pay for to the measured number, and the difference lands on your next invoice. Nothing is charged today and nothing is charged separately.

The banner clears once you pay for at least the measured count, or the measured count comes down. A number under <Ui>Rooms billed</Ui> that is still below the measured count does not clear it. Above 500 rooms there is no email and no automatic raise: [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Over%20500%20rooms).

Where the measured count comes from depends on your property system.

<PmsTab pms="cloudbeds">
Cloudbeds reports how many rooms each room type has, and MAYA reads that number on every cycle. The measured count is what Cloudbeds holds for your room types ticked as rooms, so a new room type or extra rooms in Cloudbeds raise it.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="thinkreservations">
ThinkReservations does not report how many rooms each room type has. So every room type gets the same number: one rooms-per-type number that we set for your whole property, 100 unless we change it. The measured count is that number times your room types ticked as rooms. At 100, The Harbour Inn, with Standard, Deluxe and Suite ticked as rooms, sees "You're billed for 20 rooms but running 300".

[Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=ThinkReservations%20room%20counts) your real room counts before you rely on this number. If the banner shows a count you do not have, email us straight away, before the 7 days run out. We can change that one number, not set a count for each room type.

Typing your real count on the billing page does not clear the banner while the measured count is higher, and after the 7 days MAYA raises your count to the measured number. If that has already happened, [Your room count and what you pay](/docs/billing/your-room-count#if-the-100-rooms-number-has-already-raised-your-count) shows how to put it right. The same number sets your occupancy, so read [Occupancy looks wrong](/docs/wrong/occupancy-looks-wrong) too.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="mews">
Where Mews gives no room count for a room category, that category gets one rooms-per-type number that we set for your whole property, 100 unless we change it. The measured count then adds that number once for each such category ticked as a room, so it can be far from the rooms you run.

[Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Mews%20room%20counts) your real room counts before you rely on this number. If the banner shows a count you do not have, email us straight away, before the 7 days run out. We can change that one number, not set a count for each category.

Typing your real count on the billing page does not clear the banner while the measured count is higher, and after the 7 days MAYA raises your count to the measured number. If that has already happened, [Your room count and what you pay](/docs/billing/your-room-count#if-the-100-rooms-number-has-already-raised-your-count) shows how to put it right. The same number sets your occupancy, so read [Occupancy looks wrong](/docs/wrong/occupancy-looks-wrong) too.
</PmsTab>

If MAYA is counting something that is not a guest room, untick it under <Ui>Room types</Ui> on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab and the count drops on the next cycle. If you pay for more rooms than you run, MAYA never lowers the count by itself: change it on the billing page under <Ui>Rooms billed</Ui>, and your next invoice drops. Read [Your room count](/docs/billing/your-room-count).

## It says "MAYA has paused work on this property"

The subscription is not paying. Nothing is read, priced or sent until it is. Two cases, and the banner's second line tells them apart:

- "Update your card and the subscription restarts where it left off." The last payments failed and the bank's retries ran out. Press <Ui>Restart MAYA</Ui>, then <Ui>Update card, receipts & cancellation</Ui>, and update the card. Then pay the open invoice in Stripe, and the subscription restarts where it left off. Updating the card alone does not restart it, because nothing tries the payment again by itself.
- "Your subscription was cancelled, so starting again means a new one" (restart below whenever you are ready). Press <Ui>Restart your subscription</Ui> on the billing page. A new subscription starts on the same pricing; the old code cannot be reused.

Your rules, limits, history and settings are kept while paused. Rates already in your system stay as they are. Read [Cancelling, restarting and leaving](/docs/billing/cancelling-restarting-and-leaving).

## It says "This property already has a subscription"

One subscription per property. The message adds "Manage it at /account/billing." Open the billing page from the <Ui>Billing</Ui> button on the dashboard to change the room count, the card or the plan.

## Why is the next charge different from the calculator?

The billing page shows Stripe's own preview of your next invoice, which includes any code you used and, where it applies, tax. The calculator on the website shows the list price for your room count. The two agree once a discount ends. Read [The billing page](/docs/billing/the-billing-page).

## Why can't I see the billing page?

It needs General Manager or Hotel Admin access. Anyone else sees "Billing is handled by this property's General Manager or Hotel Admin. Ask one of them for access if you need to change it." The <Ui>Team</Ui> page works the same way. Read [Roles](/docs/team/roles).

## Why is my property missing from the dropdown?

The <Ui>Property</Ui> dropdown lists only properties you belong to that are paid for and set up. A property you set aside with "Not now" during a group setup is listed on the billing page under <Ui>Properties not set up yet</Ui>, where <Ui>Set up</Ui> takes you back to pay for it. A property someone else owns needs an invitation from them.

## It says "You've used every seat"

Seats come with your room count: 2 seats for 1 to 20 rooms, 4 for 21 to 40, 5 for 41 to 60, 6 for 61 to 80, 8 for 81 to 500. Everyone listed on the <Ui>Team</Ui> page counts, including someone you invited who has not set a password yet. Remove someone, or raise the room count on the billing page if the property has grown.

## My colleague never got the invitation

Two common reasons.

- **They already use MAYA.** An address that already has a MAYA account is added to the property at once, with no email, even though the page says "Invitation sent to". Ask them to sign in. The property is in their <Ui>Property</Ui> dropdown.
- **The link expired or went to spam.** Ask them to check their spam folder first. The invitation link is single-use and expires. There is no resend button, and inviting the address again sends no email. [Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=New%20invitation%20link) with the address and we send a new link.

## It says "This is the property's only Hotel Admin"

You cannot demote or remove the last Hotel Admin, or a property could end up with nobody able to manage its team or billing. Make someone else a Hotel Admin first, then change or remove the original.

## My property name, time zone or currency came in wrong

The name, time zone and currency are read from your property system when you connect, and only once. The name you can fix: the first of the optional onboarding questions asks "Did we get your property's name right?", and whatever you type there wins.

To reopen the questions any time after connecting, go to <AppLink to="questions">maya-rms.com/onboarding/questions</AppLink>. Type the name and press <Ui>Looks right</Ui>. Then press <Ui>Skip the rest →</Ui>, so your floors and ceilings stay as they are. Read [The five questions](/docs/review/the-five-questions).

The time zone and currency cannot be changed on a screen. [Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Time%20zone%20or%20currency) with the right values. The time zone matters: it sets which night is tonight for pricing and sending.
