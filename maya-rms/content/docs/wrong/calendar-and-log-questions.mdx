<InPlainWords>
The calendar colours compare each night's room revenue per room with your own other nights, so a busy night can be red, and every day is amber when there are fewer than 10 nights to compare. The calendar and the bold line of each change in the change log show $ whatever your currency. Only the sentences under each change use your currency's symbol. The change log holds the 10 most recent runs (roughly the last 50 minutes) and 40 changes per run, in your browser's time zone, and hides quiet runs unless you press <Ui>Show All Cycles</Ui>.
</InPlainWords>

## Why is every day amber?

Colours need something to compare with. Each night's room revenue is divided by the property's room count to give revenue per room, and past nights are ranked against your other past nights, future nights against your other future nights. With fewer than 10 nights on a side, MAYA borrows both sides together, and with fewer than 10 in total there is no scale and every night is amber. A new property, or a property whose history is still importing, is amber until it has enough nights. The legend under the grid says "measured by revenue per room, relative to this property's own results".

## Why is a busy night red?

Because the colour is revenue per room, not occupancy. A full night at low rates can earn less per room than a half-full night at high rates. Red is the bottom third of your nights, amber the middle third, green the top third, each side (past, future) ranked on its own. The colour tells you how the night's revenue ranks against your own nights, and nothing else.

## Why did the colour not change after a booking?

The booked counts and revenue on the visible month are always fresh, but the ranking they are coloured against is kept for up to 5 minutes. A colour can lag a new booking by that long. The calendar also refreshes itself every 5 minutes on the <Ui>Calendar</Ui> tab and when you return to the browser tab.

## Why does the calendar show $ when my currency is euro?

The day cards, the day detail, the room type cards, the manual price box, the Rate Simulator and the bold line of each change in the change log all show $ regardless of your property's currency. The numbers are in your own currency. Only the symbol is wrong.

The sentences under each change, the amber alert banner and the manual price error messages use your currency's symbol: $ for US dollars, € for euros, £ for pounds, and the three-letter code in front of the amount for any other currency. So a property in euros sees "$150.00 up to $165.00" in a change's bold line and "€150.00" in the sentence under it, for the same price. Your currency comes from your property system when you connect and cannot be changed in the app.

<Callout kind="not-yet">
The calendar and the change log's bold lines cannot show your currency's symbol yet, and the currency cannot be changed on a screen. If MAYA has the wrong currency for your property, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Currency).
</Callout>

## Why does ADR say $150 when nothing is booked?

The room type card's ADR (average daily rate: that night's room revenue divided by the rooms booked) needs at least one booking. When <Ui>Booked</Ui> reads 0, ignore the ADR line: the $150.00 there is a placeholder, whatever your rates are. It is not a price and it is not sent anywhere. Look at <Ui>Current price</Ui> for the price your rules have set.

## Why does today say "revenue" instead of "revenue on the books"?

The day detail reads "revenue on the books" for today and later, and "revenue" for past days. At properties in the Americas, today's heading can switch from "revenue on the books" to "revenue" in the evening. It is only a label and changes no price. Pricing always uses your property's own date.

## Why are there no weekday headers?

The month grid has none. Click a day and its heading names the weekday: "Friday, November 13, 2026". The grid starts on Sunday.

## Why do past months show zeros?

Nights before your property's imported history have nothing to show. The year picker runs from the first month with a booking or a price to the last.

## Why are there so many "Prices checked" entries?

Pressing <Ui>Show All Cycles</Ui> shows every run, and a run happens about every five minutes whenever bookings were read. A run that changed nothing reads "Prices checked" and nothing needed to change. That is MAYA checking your rules and finding no reason to act. Press <Ui>Show Changes Only</Ui> to go back to the default view, which keeps only runs that changed something, sending problems and your answers.

## Why is the change log empty?

- **Nothing has changed yet.** The default view hides quiet runs. Press <Ui>Show All Cycles</Ui> to see them. A new property with rules that have not fired shows only quiet runs.
- **No run has happened yet.** A property that connected minutes ago runs its first cycle within about five minutes.
- **The subscription lapsed.** A red banner reads "MAYA has paused work on this property". No runs happen until it is fixed.
- **Reads are failing.** A run whose booking read failed prices nothing and writes nothing. Check <Ui>Health (last 24 hours)</Ui> and <Ui>Last sync</Ui> on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab, and look for the connection banner.
- **It could not load.** "Couldn't load your price history." with a <Ui>Try again</Ui> link means the page could not fetch the log. Press <Ui>Try again</Ui>.

Simulation is not a reason: while simulating, every run happens and is written down.

## Why did an older change disappear?

The change log holds the 10 most recent runs, quiet ones included: roughly the last 50 minutes. <Ui>Show Changes Only</Ui> hides the quiet ones among those 10, so it can look empty when nothing changed recently. An older run drops off the bottom as new ones arrive.

A sending problem that is still going on stays at the top of the log until it ends, however long ago it started. Once it has ended, it shows only while it ended within the time those 10 runs cover. Your answers to the amber banner work the same way: each one shows only while it falls within that time.

For an older change, check the night's <Ui>Current price</Ui> and any <Ui>Manual</Ui> badge on the calendar, and [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Why%20did%20a%20price%20change) the night and room type. The detail behind every change is kept for 90 days, and we can read it for you.

## Why are only some of a run's changes listed?

A run lists its 40 biggest moves, largest percentage first. A run that changed more nights than that shows 40 and does not say how many more there were.

<Callout kind="not-yet">
There is no export, no search and no filter by night or room type in the change log, and no way to see runs older than the 10 shown. For a change older than about 50 minutes, email us the night and room type and we read it for you.
</Callout>

## Why is "How did we know?" missing on some changes?

The button appears under a change when the run that made it also took a booking speed reading for that night. While any rule you have switched on uses booking speed (every starter rule does), each run takes that reading on nearly every night in the window. So the button shows under most changes, including ones made by an occupancy or booking window rule, a price you typed, or a rate changed in your system.

Under those changes the reading is background, not the reason the price moved. The sentence under the change names what moved it. The button shows how the night was booking at the time of that run.

The button is missing when that run took no booking speed reading for the night. That happens when none of the rules you have switched on uses booking speed, and on the occasional night no booking speed rule was checking in that run. A booking speed rule that measures only some room types leaves its reading on the room types it adjusts, and nowhere else.

## Whose time zone are the log times in?

Your browser's. "Pricing run · Fri, Sep 18, 2026, 3:05 PM PDT" is the time on your computer's clock, with its zone abbreviation. Hover the time for the exact timestamp. Pricing itself works in your property's own time zone, and the "Updated 3:05:12 PM · live" time on the calendar is also your browser's clock.

## Why does the PMS tab say "No recent activity" on ThinkReservations or Mews?

The <Ui>Health (last 24 hours)</Ui> card and the <Ui>Recent requests</Ui> table are filled from a request log that only Cloudbeds writes to today. On a ThinkReservations or Mews property the card reads "No recent activity" and the table reads "No requests recorded yet" however healthy the connection is. Use <Ui>Connection</Ui> and <Ui>Last sync</Ui> on those properties instead.

## The alert banner is out of date

The amber <Ui>Rules that keep adjusting</Ui> banner loads when the page opens and when you switch property, and not on a timer. A night you answered in another tab, or a night that has since passed, stays on screen until you reload the page.

The billing banner loads only when the page opens. It does not reload when you switch property, so after a switch it can still show the last property's billing message, or show nothing when the new property has one. Reload the page after switching to see the billing banner for the property you are on.

## Switching property did nothing

If the switch fails, nothing is shown on screen and the old property stays. Reload the page and try again. Only properties that are paid for and set up appear in the <Ui>Property</Ui> dropdown: a property you set aside with "Not now" is listed under <Ui>Properties not set up yet</Ui> on the billing page, where <Ui>Set up</Ui> brings it back.
