<InPlainWords>
This is every limit of MAYA today, in one list, each with what to do instead and the page that explains it. The ones people meet first: rules cannot be limited to weekends or dates or be edited, nothing is sent to Mews yet, and nothing can be exported. No dates are promised here. When a limit goes away, this page changes.
</InPlainWords>

Each line says what is missing, then what you can do today. Most limits have a way round them, and the most useful one is often typing a price yourself on the <Ui>Calendar</Ui> tab: it can do for chosen nights nearly everything a rule cannot.

## Rules

- **No weekday rules.** Every rule runs every night, weekends included. Type a price on the nights you want, or use booking speed, which already compares each Friday with other Fridays. [What rules cannot do yet](/docs/rules/what-rules-cannot-do-yet)
- **No date range, season or event rules.** Type a price for the stretch with <Ui>through…</Ui> on the calendar, for any nights from tonight to 399 days ahead. [Hold a price for an event](/docs/recipes/hold-a-price-for-an-event)
- **No editing, renaming or copying a rule**, and no copying rules to another property. Delete it and add it again, or turn it off and add the new one beside it. [Turning a rule off, deleting it, or changing it](/docs/rules/off-delete-or-change)
- **At most 40 rules <Ui>On</Ui> at once.** Rules switched <Ui>Off</Ui> do not count. Switch one <Ui>Off</Ui> to add another, and the changes it made stay. Deleting one also frees a place, but undoes its changes. [Turning a rule off, deleting it, or changing it](/docs/rules/off-delete-or-change#how-many-rules-you-can-have)
- **No "or" between conditions**, and at most one condition of each kind. Make two rules. [What rules cannot do yet](/docs/rules/what-rules-cannot-do-yet)
- **No priority setting.** When two booking speed or pickup rules are true on the same night, a fixed order picks the one that acts. Standard rules all apply. [How rules run](/docs/rules/how-rules-run)
- **No price further than 395 nights ahead by rule.** Type a price for the night (up to 399 days ahead), and it is priced and sent when the night enters the window. [How rules run](/docs/rules/how-rules-run)
- **No rule that copies one room type's price onto another**, or keeps a fixed gap between them. Measure one set of room types and change another by a percentage or an amount. [Let suites follow standard rooms](/docs/recipes/let-suites-follow-standard-rooms)
- **No rule that measures a room type that does not count as a room.** Tick it as a room, or measure your rooms and change it. [Measure one set, change another](/docs/rules/measure-one-set-change-another)
- **No change to the three-times question.** You cannot change the number three or stop a rule on a night before it asks. Type a price for that night. [Rules that keep adjusting](/docs/rules/rules-that-keep-adjusting)
- **No control over booking speed's levels**, guards or how many similar nights are used. [Booking speed](/docs/rules/booking-speed)
- **No floor or ceiling editor**, and no floor or ceiling per night or season. Answer the five questions again, or email us the numbers. [Floors and ceilings](/docs/review/floors-and-ceilings)

## Seasons, holidays and history

- **No holidays outside the US**, and no way to add a holiday, festival or local event. Set the night aside with the reason for a holiday, and say it happens every year. [Handle a holiday MAYA does not have](/docs/recipes/handle-a-holiday-maya-does-not-have)
- **No seasons you set yourself.** You cannot name, move or define one. Set unusual past dates aside with the scope that also changes how seasons are read. [Similar nights](/docs/rules/similar-nights)
- **No page that lists your seasons, similar nights or holidays.** They show inside <Ui>How did we know?</Ui>, for the night in question. [How did we know?](/docs/watch/how-did-we-know)
- **No closed period added by hand**, and a closure still to come is priced as normal. On Cloudbeds or ThinkReservations, set those nights to 0 in your system, which closes them for MAYA too. On Mews a 0 there has no effect in MAYA. [Checking what MAYA found](/docs/review/checking-what-maya-found)
- **No button to restart the history import.** If a lost connection stopped it, reconnecting picks it up. If it stops for any other reason, email us. [The history import](/docs/review/the-history-import)

## Prices and sending

- **Nothing is sent to Mews**, including prices you type. MAYA reads Mews bookings, runs your rules and shows the prices. [Mews: what works today](/docs/connect/mews)
- **No minimum stays, closed to arrival or departure, close-outs, availability or allotments.** MAYA writes nightly prices only. Set the rest in your property system. [What MAYA reads and writes](/docs/connect/what-maya-reads-and-writes)
- **No price per rate plan, per guest count or per package.** MAYA writes one base rate per room type, and plans that follow it move by your system's own rules. [How prices reach your property system](/docs/live/how-prices-reach-your-pms)
- **No price of 0 is ever sent.** For a free night, set 0 in your system yourself. [Make a night free](/docs/recipes/make-a-night-free)
- **No undo button for one price change.** Type the price you want, delete the rule, or stop it on that night from the banner. [When a price change comes off](/docs/rules/when-a-change-comes-off)
- **No per-night "sent" or "landed" mark.** Compare the night in both places, and read the change log for problems. [How prices reach your property system](/docs/live/how-prices-reach-your-pms)
- **No email, text or banner for a sending problem.** It appears in the change log only. [When your property system does not accept a price](/docs/live/when-your-pms-does-not-accept-a-price)
- **No "sync now" button.** MAYA reads your bookings about every five minutes on its own. [The PMS tab](/docs/watch/the-pms-tab)
- **No button back to simulation once you are live.** Email us, or turn your rules off. [Pausing, or going back to simulation](/docs/live/pausing-or-going-back)
- **No competitor rates, weather, event or holiday feeds.** MAYA works from your own bookings and rates only. [What MAYA does](/docs/start/what-maya-does)
- **No watching for emergency declarations or changes in the law.** If a law limits price rises, change your rules or type prices yourself. [Terms and legal](/docs/reference/terms-and-legal)
- **No rounding to whole units** for a currency without cents. Prices are worked out to two decimal places. [Time, weekends, currency and holidays](/docs/reference/time-weekends-currency-and-holidays)

## Property systems

- **Three systems only**: Cloudbeds, ThinkReservations and Mews. No Opera, Little Hotelier, Guesty or others, no spreadsheet or file import, and one system per property. [Which systems work](/docs/connect/which-systems-work)
- **Mews is connected by us**, with no history import, no starter rules, no suggestions, no read of your rates, and no go-live or reconnect from the app. Email us. [Mews: what works today](/docs/connect/mews)
- **ThinkReservations has no Marketplace install**, so no trial without a code, and a login covering several hotels is set up by hand. [ThinkReservations](/docs/connect/thinkreservations)
- **No room blocks, allotments or out-of-order rooms read from any system.** Enter them as rooms out of service on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab. [Room types and rooms out of service](/docs/watch/room-types-and-rooms-out-of-service)
- **No renaming, merging or switching off a room type in MAYA.** Names and unit counts come from your system. Untick <Ui>Counts as a room</Ui> for anything nobody sleeps in. [Room types and rooms out of service](/docs/watch/room-types-and-rooms-out-of-service)
- **No screen that changes your time zone or currency.** Both are read once when you connect, and the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab shows them. Email us to change either. [Time, weekends, currency and holidays](/docs/reference/time-weekends-currency-and-holidays)
- **No disconnect button in MAYA.** Disconnect in your system, or email us. [Reconnecting and disconnecting](/docs/connect/reconnect-and-disconnect)

## Calendar and change log

- **The change log shows the 10 most recent runs**, with no paging back, search, filter or export. For an older change, email us the night and room type. [The change log](/docs/watch/the-change-log)
- **No notifications when a price changes**, by email or otherwise. [Emails MAYA sends](/docs/billing/emails-maya-sends)
- **The change log's bold lines show $ whatever your currency.** So do the <Ui>Price Change</Ui> column on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab and the rule builder's fixed amount box. The numbers are yours, and the calendar, the Rate Simulator and the change log's sentences use your currency's sign. [Calendar and change log questions](/docs/wrong/calendar-and-log-questions)
- **No week or list view, no weekday names above the grid, and no list of the bookings behind a night.** Open the night in your property system. [The calendar](/docs/watch/the-calendar)
- **No live or simulation badge.** [Tell if you are live](/docs/recipes/tell-if-you-are-live) walks through the clues.
- **No booking speed reading before a rule acts.** It appears under <Ui>How did we know?</Ui> after a change. [Booking speed](/docs/rules/booking-speed)
- **No report of what your rules have earned, or would have earned**, and no revenue report. The change log shows each change, and the <Ui>Rate Simulator</Ui> shows what your rules would do to a night you make up, though it cannot replay a real past run. [The Rate Simulator](/docs/rules/the-rate-simulator)

## Account, team and billing

- **No way to change your sign-in email address in the app.** Email us. A password is reset or changed from <Ui>Forgot password?</Ui> on the sign-in page. [Reset your password](/docs/recipes/reset-your-password)
- **No two-factor sign-in, Google sign-in or single sign-on.** [Create an account and sign in](/docs/account/create-an-account-and-sign-in)
- **No custom roles**, and no role that sees billing without also being able to go live and change the team. [Roles](/docs/team/roles)
- **No resend button for an invitation** (for someone who has never signed in, remove them and invite them again, or email us), and no inviting someone to several properties at once. [Inviting and removing people](/docs/team/inviting-and-removing-people)
- **No view of all your properties at once**, no shared rules and no pooled billing. [Several properties](/docs/account/several-properties)
- **No free plan**, and no trial without the Cloudbeds Marketplace or a code. The Marketplace trial comes once, at a property's first signup, never on a restart. Properties over 500 rooms are not sold self-serve. [What it costs](/docs/start/what-it-costs)
- **Cards only**: no bank transfer, ACH or invoicing. [Cards, payments and invoices](/docs/billing/cards-payments-and-invoices)
- **No invoices inside MAYA.** They live in Stripe's billing portal, opened with <Ui>Update card, receipts & cancellation</Ui> on the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page. [The billing page](/docs/billing/the-billing-page)
- **No link to accounting software** such as QuickBooks or Xero. Forward Stripe's receipts to your accountant, or change the billing email in the portal to an address they read. They do not need a MAYA login for invoices. [Cards, payments and invoices](/docs/billing/cards-payments-and-invoices)
- **No pause.** Turn your rules off, ask us to put the property back in simulation, or cancel and restart later. [Cancelling, restarting and leaving](/docs/billing/cancelling-restarting-and-leaving)
- **No email digest, and no email settings.** [Emails MAYA sends](/docs/billing/emails-maya-sends)

## Your data, and everything else

- **No export** of rules, data, the calendar or the change log. Copy what you need from the screen. You can email us for a copy of your data, though one is promised only where the law requires it. [Privacy, security and your data](/docs/reference/privacy-security-and-your-data)
- **No self-serve deletion** of a property or its data. A Hotel Admin or General Manager emails us. [Cancelling, restarting and leaving](/docs/billing/cancelling-restarting-and-leaving)
- **No public API** for your own systems. [What MAYA does](/docs/start/what-maya-does)
- **No mobile app.** MAYA is a website, with nothing to install. [A tour of the app](/docs/start/a-tour-of-the-app)
- **No demo property.** Every property starts in simulation, which runs your rules and sends nothing. [Is MAYA right for your property](/docs/start/is-maya-right-for-you)
- **No phone or chat support, and no status page.** Support is by email. [Contact support](/docs/help/contact-support)
- **No uptime guarantee.** [Terms and legal](/docs/reference/terms-and-legal)
- **No choice of hosting region, no published security certification and no fixed IP addresses.** [Privacy, security and your data](/docs/reference/privacy-security-and-your-data)
