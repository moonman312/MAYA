<InPlainWords>
MAYA runs about every five minutes, prices again each night whose bookings changed, and prices every night from tonight to 395 nights ahead once a day. A rule has up to four conditions and waits between 1 day and 2 weeks after it acts. Booking speed compares with up to eight similar nights from the last three years. The tables below give every other number, with the page that explains it. None of them can be changed from the app.
</InPlainWords>

## The cycle

| What | Number | Read |
|---|---|---|
| How often MAYA reads your bookings and runs your rules on the nights that changed | About every five minutes |
| How often every night in the window is priced again | Once a day, in the first cycles after midnight at your property, nearest nights first |
| When a rule's wait ends, and a pickup lookback moves on | At your property's midnight: they count whole days | [How rules run](/docs/rules/how-rules-run) |
| How often your own rates are read (Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations) | About once an hour, and again when your property's date changes | [What MAYA picks up from your system, and how soon](/docs/connect/what-maya-picks-up-from-your-system) |
| How often the whole booking window is read again | Once a day | [What MAYA picks up from your system, and how soon](/docs/connect/what-maya-picks-up-from-your-system) |
| When a read fails | Tried again with a growing wait, up to an hour between tries | [Connection lost](/docs/wrong/connection-lost) |

## The window and prices you type

| What | Number | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Nights your rules price and MAYA sends | 396: tonight and the next 395, about 13 months | [How rules run](/docs/rules/how-rules-run) |
| How far ahead you can type a price | Up to 399 days, from tonight | [Setting a price yourself](/docs/watch/setting-a-price-yourself) |
| Nights in one price save | At most 366 | [Setting a price yourself](/docs/watch/setting-a-price-yourself) |
| Nights in one block of rooms out of service | At most 366 | [Room types and rooms out of service](/docs/watch/room-types-and-rooms-out-of-service#rooms-out-of-service) |
| Highest price you can type | $99,999,999.99, and never above the room type's ceiling | [Setting a price yourself](/docs/watch/setting-a-price-yourself) |

## Rules

| What | Number | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Conditions in one rule | 1 to 4, at most one of each kind | [The rule builder, field by field](/docs/rules/the-rule-builder) |
| Amount of a rule's change, percent or fixed | Above 0, with no upper limit you would meet (anything under 1,000,000 saves). A cut to 0 or below publishes the floor | [The rule builder, field by field](/docs/rules/the-rule-builder#rate-adjustment) |
| Occupancy threshold | 0 to 100 | [Sellable occupancy](/docs/rules/sellable-occupancy) |
| <Ui>Measured over</Ui> for booking speed | <Ui>Past day</Ui>, <Ui>Past week</Ui> or <Ui>Past month</Ui> | [Booking speed](/docs/rules/booking-speed) |
| <Ui>Then waits (advanced)</Ui> | 1 day to 2 weeks, 1 week when unset | [How rules run](/docs/rules/how-rules-run) |
| Pickup count lookback | 1, 3 or 7 days | [Pickup count (advanced)](/docs/rules/pickup-count) |
| Adjustments before the amber banner asks you | 3 on the same night | [Rules that keep adjusting](/docs/rules/rules-that-keep-adjusting) |
| Rules switched <Ui>On</Ui> at the same time | 40. Rules switched <Ui>Off</Ui> do not count | [How many rules you can have](/docs/rules/off-delete-or-change#how-many-rules-you-can-have) |

| What | Number | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Similar past nights compared | Up to 8, or up to 3 for a night near a holiday | [Which past nights your rules compare with](/docs/rules/similar-nights) |

| Similar nights before MAYA widens the search | 4, away from holidays (with fewer, the search widens a step) | [Which past nights your rules compare with](/docs/rules/similar-nights) |

## How often you can do things

When you go past one of these, the screen asks you to wait a few minutes and try again. Nothing is lost.

| What | Limit | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Saving or clearing typed prices, and room type changes | 30 per person in 10 minutes | [Setting a price yourself](/docs/watch/setting-a-price-yourself) |
| Checking the days a rule will change (the window before it starts acting; a booking speed or pickup rule's check counts three times) | 60 per person in 5 minutes | [Turning a rule on, Apply or Skip](/docs/rules/turning-a-rule-on) |
| <Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui> | 5 runs in 15 minutes | [Getting suggestions later](/docs/review/getting-suggestions-later) |
| Checking a code | 20 per person in 10 minutes | [Codes and the free trial](/docs/pay/codes-and-the-free-trial) |
| Starting checkout | 10 in 10 minutes | [Setting up payment](/docs/pay/setting-up-payment) |
| Invitations | 20 an hour on one property | [Inviting and removing people](/docs/team/inviting-and-removing-people) |

| What | Number | Read |
|---|---|---|
| The Cloudbeds Marketplace hand-over link | Good for 24 hours | [Cloudbeds](/docs/connect/cloudbeds) |
| The sign-in link from inside MAYA | Good for 15 minutes | [How to get started](/docs/start/how-to-get-started) |
