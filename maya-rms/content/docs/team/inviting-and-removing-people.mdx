<InPlainWords>
Click <Ui>Team</Ui>, top right. Type an email under <Ui>Their email</Ui>, pick what they can do, click <Ui>Send invitation</Ui>. They are on the property at once. Someone new to MAYA gets an email with a single-use link to set a password. Someone who already uses MAYA gets no email. Seats follow the room count you pay for (2, 4, 5, 6 or 8) and are not sold on their own. To remove someone, click <Ui>Remove</Ui> beside their name.
</InPlainWords>

## The Team page

<Ui>Team</Ui> is a button at the top right of the dashboard, beside <Ui>Billing</Ui>. The page is headed "Your team", with links to <Ui>Billing</Ui> and <Ui>← Back to MAYA</Ui>. It has two parts: <Ui>Add someone</Ui>, and the list <Ui>On this property</Ui>.

A General Manager or Hotel Admin manages the team. Anyone else who opens the page sees one line: "Who can access this property is managed by its General Manager or Hotel Admin. Ask one of them if you need someone added or removed." If the list fails to load, the page says "We couldn't load your team just now." with a <Ui>Try again</Ui> button.

## Seats

How many people can be on a property follows the rooms you pay for, in the same bands as the price.

| Rooms billed | Seats |
|---|---|
| 1 to 20 | 2 |
| 21 to 40 | 4 |
| 41 to 60 | 5 |
| 61 to 80 | 6 |
| 81 to 500 | 8 |

A seat is used by each person in the list below, including someone you invited who has not set a password yet. The counter at the top right of <Ui>Add someone</Ui> reads "1 of 2 seats used · 20 rooms".

When every seat is used, the form is replaced by: "You've used every seat your plan includes. Remove someone below, or raise your room count on the billing page if the property has grown." If two people invite at the same moment and the second goes past the count, the second sees "Your plan covers 2 people at 20 rooms. Properties over 20 rooms get 4. Remove someone first, or update your room count if the property has grown."

The band uses the room count you are billed for, not the one MAYA measured, so a seat cannot vanish because a room type was switched off overnight. Raising the billed count on the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page raises the seats at the same moment. [Your room count and what you pay](/docs/billing/your-room-count) explains the count.

### No separate seats

There are no seats to buy on their own. Seats come only from the room count you pay for, so the one way to add people is to pay for more rooms. That is allowed. A General Manager or Hotel Admin can set any count from 1 to 500 on the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page, whatever your property system shows, and MAYA never lowers it for you.

<Example title="12 rooms, four people">
A 12-room property billed for 12 rooms pays $5.50 a room, $66 a month, and has 2 seats. Billed for 21 rooms, it pays $5.00 a room, $105 a month, and has 4 seats. Prices are for the monthly plan, before any code.

Afterwards the <Ui>Room count</Ui> section on the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page says "Your PMS currently shows 12 active rooms." and "You're paying for more than that". That is expected, and nothing changes unless you lower the count yourself. If you later lower it to 20 or fewer, nobody is removed, but you cannot invite anyone new until fewer than 2 people are left on the team.
</Example>

Eight seats is the most any property can have, from 81 rooms up. [Change the room count you pay for](/docs/recipes/change-the-room-count-you-pay-for) has the steps.

## Inviting someone

<Steps>
<Step title="Type their email">
In <Ui>Their email</Ui> (the box shows name@yourhotel.com as a hint). Capitals and spaces are tidied away, and the invitation goes to the address in lower case.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick what they can do">
The <Ui>What they can do</Ui> dropdown lists the four roles and starts on Revenue Manager. A line under the form repeats the chosen role's description, and the <Ui>?</Ui> beside the label opens "What each role can do", the same four descriptions in one panel (hover or click to open it, and press Escape or click elsewhere to close it). [Roles: who can do what](/docs/team/roles) has the long version.

The dropdown lists every role for everyone. A General Manager who picks Hotel Admin is refused on send with "You can't give someone a role above your own."
</Step>
<Step title="Click Send invitation">
The button reads "Sending…", then the page says "Invitation sent to" the address, with "They'll get an email with a link to join." The person is on the property from this moment, before they open the email. Their address appears in the list below with their role, a role menu and <Ui>Remove</Ui>, like everyone else.
</Step>
</Steps>

Messages you might see:

- **"Enter a valid email address."** Check the address for a typo.
- **"Pick a role."** Choose a role in <Ui>What they can do</Ui>, then send.
- **"That person is already on this property."** They are on the team already. Change their role in the list instead.
- **"They already have an invitation pending."** Cancel the pending invitation in the list, then invite again.
- **The seat message above.** Remove someone, or raise the room count on the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page.
- **"Too many invitations just now. Try again shortly."** More than 20 in an hour on one property. Wait, then try again.
- **Any other message.** Something else went wrong. It may read "Could not send that invitation." or be in technical words. Try once more, and if it fails again, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Invitation%20failed) with the words you saw. If a second try says "Invitation sent" but no email arrives, email us for a new link.

## The invitation email

The subject is "You have been invited to The Harbour Inn on MAYA". Inside, the heading "You're invited", a line saying who invited them, to which property and as which role, and an <Ui>Accept the invite</Ui> button. The reply-to is the inviter's address, so a reply reaches you. The footer says the link is single-use and expires after a limited time. How long is not a number MAYA sets. If the link has expired or the email is lost, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=New%20invitation%20link) and we send a new one (see [Resending an invitation](#resending-an-invitation) below).

## If they already use MAYA

An address that already has a MAYA account (a colleague at another property, say) gets no email. They are added to the property the moment you click <Ui>Send invitation</Ui>, and the property appears in their <Ui>Property</Ui> selector the next time they open MAYA. The page still says "Invitation sent", but nothing was sent, and there is nothing for them to accept. Tell them to sign in.

## Accepting an invitation

<Steps>
<Step title="Click Accept the invite">
The link opens a page headed "Set your password" with "Welcome to MAYA. Set a password to finish accepting your invite." While the link is checked it reads "Verifying your invite…". If someone else was signed in on that browser, they are signed out first so the invitation lands on the right account.
</Step>
<Step title="Fill in the form">
<Ui>Full name (optional)</Ui>, <Ui>Password</Ui> and <Ui>Confirm password</Ui>, each password at least 8 characters, then the Terms box. The button <Ui>Set password & continue</Ui> waits for the box. Errors: "Password must be at least 8 characters." and "Passwords don't match."
</Step>
<Step title="You are in">
"Password set. Redirecting to the app…" and the dashboard opens a second later, on the property you were invited to.
</Step>
</Steps>

If the link has expired or has already been used, the page shows a message that the link is invalid or has expired, in place of the form. Already set your password with that link? Then sign in with your email and password. If not, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=New%20invitation%20link) from the invited address and we send you a new link. Nobody on your property can resend it, and inviting you again sends no email, because you are already on the property.

<Callout kind="good-to-know" title="You are on the property before you accept">
Sending the invitation adds the person to the property straight away. The email's link is how they set a password. They do not need to create an account of their own.
</Callout>

## On this property

The list shows each person's email and role. Someone you invited is listed straight away, with nothing to mark that they have not set a password yet.

- **Changing a role.** Pick a new role in the dropdown beside their name (its label reads "Role for" their email). It saves at once. Refusals: "That's above your own access level." (you cannot promote someone above yourself, or change someone who already outranks you), "This is the property's only Hotel Admin. Make someone else one first." and "That person isn't on this property." Anything else that fails shows "Could not change that role." or a message in technical words. Try again.
- **Removing someone.** Click <Ui>Remove</Ui>. Their access ends at once. The same rank rules apply. Anything else that fails shows "Could not remove them." or a message in technical words. Try again.
- **Taking back an invitation.** Click <Ui>Remove</Ui> beside their name, as for anyone else. They are off the property and the seat is free.

You can remove yourself, as long as you are not the property's only Hotel Admin. Once removed you cannot get back in until someone still on the property invites you again.

## Resending an invitation

There is no resend button, and inviting the same address again does not send a new link. The person is already on the property, so the page answers "That person is already on this property." Removing them and inviting them again puts them back on the property, but sends no email, because their login already exists.

If the link has expired or the email went astray, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=New%20invitation%20link) with the address and we send a new link.

## When someone leaves

Click <Ui>Remove</Ui> beside their name. Their login no longer opens the property, and their seat is free for the next person. The Terms ask you to remove access promptly when someone should no longer have it. If they were the only Hotel Admin, make someone else one first. If the only Hotel Admin is the one who left, [Roles: who can do what](/docs/team/roles#if-your-only-hotel-admin-has-left) has what to do.

## When the property changes hands

If you sell the hotel, or a new management company takes over, you hand MAYA over from inside the app and let us know. Rules, floors and ceilings, history, settings and the connection to your property system all stay as they are.

<Steps>
<Step title="Invite the new owner as Hotel Admin">
A Hotel Admin does this from the <Ui>Team</Ui> page, because only a Hotel Admin can make another one. If every seat is used, remove someone first. The new owner sets a password from the invitation email, or, if they already use MAYA, finds the property in their <Ui>Property</Ui> selector.
</Step>
<Step title="Move the billing">
The old owner first downloads any invoices they want to keep: once removed, they cannot open <Ui>Billing</Ui>. Then the new owner opens <Ui>Billing</Ui>, clicks <Ui>Update card, receipts & cancellation</Ui>, and in Stripe's billing portal adds their own card as the default and changes the billing email to their own address. Past invoices stay in the portal, where the new owner can see them.

A subscription can keep the card it started with, so the old owner's card may still be charged. When you [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Change%20of%20owner) about the change of owner, ask us to move the subscription onto the new owner's card. [Moving to another card](/docs/billing/cards-payments-and-invoices#moving-to-another-card) explains.
</Step>
<Step title="Remove the old team">
The new owner clicks <Ui>Remove</Ui> beside each person who is leaving, the old owner included. With two Hotel Admins on the property, either one can remove the other.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="careful">
Email us when the property changes hands. Under the Terms, a subscription passes to a new owner only with our written consent, and the old owner stays responsible for the property's subscription and the prices sent for it until they cancel it or the new owner accepts the Terms for it.
</Callout>

**Came from the Cloudbeds Marketplace?** A property you started with <Ui>Connect App</Ui> in the Cloudbeds Marketplace bills on the original owner's account in Stripe, the same account that pays for any other properties they connected that way. Leave the card as it is and [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Change%20of%20owner) instead, and we sort out the billing with you.

## Access to all your properties at once

Invite the person from each property's <Ui>Team</Ui> page. There is no bulk invitation, and roles are per property, so they can be Revenue Manager on one and Viewer on another.

## Is there an audit trail?

Not for the team. There is no screen that lists team changes, and there is no record you can open of who invited, changed or removed whom. The change log does name the person who answered a <Ui>Rules that keep adjusting</Ui> banner.

## Who is signed in

There is no screen that shows who is signed in or when someone last signed in, and no button to sign everyone out. The list under <Ui>On this property</Ui> shows each person's email and role, and no sign-in times.

To cut someone off, click <Ui>Remove</Ui> beside their name. Their access to the property ends at once, even in a browser where they are still signed in. How long a session lasts before someone has to sign in again is set on our side, not by you. [Create an account and sign in](/docs/account/create-an-account-and-sign-in) covers staying signed in and signing out.

If you suspect someone else is using a colleague's login, remove them and [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Suspected%20unauthorised%20access). The Terms ask you to tell us promptly.

<Example title="Sam invites the night manager">
The Harbour Inn pays for 20 rooms, so it has 2 seats, and Sam and Priya fill them. The form is replaced by the "You've used every seat" line. Sam has a choice: remove someone, or, since the annex has opened and the property now runs 24 rooms, raise the room count on the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page to 24. That moves the property to the next band with 4 seats. Sam then invites the night manager as a Viewer and sees "Invitation sent" with "They'll get an email with a link to join." The night manager is in the list at once, as a Viewer, and uses the email's link to set a password.
</Example>

<Callout kind="not-yet">
No resend button: email us for a new invitation link. No stated expiry on the link. No seats sold apart from rooms. No bulk invitation across properties. No team audit trail. No list of who is signed in or when, and no way to sign everyone out. No way to change your own email address.
</Callout>
