<InPlainWords>
MAYA itself sends seven emails: the team invitation, a note that your account is ready once you have paid, the room count email, a nudge before your subscription starts or renews if your property system is not connected, and three to your General Managers and Hotel Admins: once the connection to your property system has been down about an hour, once a day about rates MAYA overwrote under "MAYA's price wins", and once when something other than MAYA seems to be changing your rates. Creating an account also brings a confirmation link from the sign-in service. Anything about money (receipts, failed payments, card expiry, a trial reminder) comes from Stripe, if Stripe's settings send it, to the email address on your billing account. MAYA sends no email when a price changes or a rule fires.
</InPlainWords>

## The seven emails MAYA sends

| Email | When | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| The team invitation | Someone on your team invites an address that has never signed in to MAYA | "You have been invited to The Harbour Inn on MAYA" |
| The account ready email | Your payment is recorded, or your free trial starts, once for each property | "Your MAYA account is ready", or the property's name followed by "is ready in MAYA" |
| The room count email | Your property system shows more rooms than you pay for | "Your MAYA plan covers 20 rooms, and your PMS shows 24", with your own numbers |
| The nudge before a charge | A charge is coming up and your property system is still not connected | "Your MAYA subscription starts" and the date (first charge), or "MAYA renews" and the date, then "and isn't connected to your PMS yet" |
| The connection-down email | The connection to your property system has read <Ui>Disconnected</Ui> or <Ui>Error</Ui> for about an hour | "MAYA has lost its connection to Cloudbeds at The Harbour Inn", with your system and property |
| The overwrites email | Your setting is <Ui>MAYA's price wins</Ui>, and on the day just over MAYA sent its price again over rates changed or removed in your system | "MAYA overwrote rate changes in Cloudbeds at The Harbour Inn" |
| The other pricing tool email | Your setting is <Ui>Keep the change as your price</Ui>, and the changes in your system look like another pricing tool at work | "Something other than MAYA seems to be changing rates in Cloudbeds at The Harbour Inn" |

All seven come from MAYA's own sending address, described under [The sending address](#the-sending-address). Nothing else is sent by MAYA today.

### The team invitation

Sent when a General Manager or Hotel Admin invites an email address that has never signed in to MAYA. Its heading is "You're invited", it names the property, the role and the person who invited you (the reply-to is their address, so a reply reaches them), and it carries an <Ui>Accept the invite</Ui> button. The link works once and expires after a limited time. If it has expired and you haven't set your password yet, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=New%20invitation%20link) for a new one, as the email itself says. The colleague who invited you can also send a fresh one by removing you from the <Ui>Team</Ui> page and inviting you again, as long as you have never signed in.

Everyone invited is added to the property straight away, before they open the email. Someone who has signed in to MAYA before gets no email at all, and the <Ui>Team</Ui> page says "Added." instead of "Invitation sent". [Inviting and removing people](/docs/team/inviting-and-removing-people) covers both cases.

### The account ready email

Sent once for each property, when MAYA records that you have paid or that your free trial has started. This is the email the "Payment received" screen promises when it says we will email you when it is ready. It goes to the address you sign in with, the one that paid, and to the email on your billing account only if that login cannot be found. It usually arrives within a minute or two of paying, whether or not you waited on the screen.

Its heading is "Your account is ready". The subject is "Your MAYA account is ready", or, for a property that already has its name (a Cloudbeds Marketplace property), the name followed by "is ready in MAYA", for example "The Harbour Inn is ready in MAYA". It thanks you for signing up and says your subscription has started, or, on a free trial, that your card is saved, the date the trial runs until, and that nothing is charged before then. Then it gives your next step:

- **Signed up at maya-rms.com:** connect your property management system, after which MAYA reads your booking history. If you use Mews, it asks you to reply so we can connect it with you.
- **From the Cloudbeds Marketplace:** your property system is connected and MAYA has started reading your booking history.

Its button, <Ui>Pick up where you left off</Ui>, takes you to whichever step you are on. It ends by saying that every property starts in simulation, so nothing is sent to your system until you choose to go live. A reply reaches us.

Each property gets it once. Paying for the properties of a group one at a time brings one for each. Restarting a cancelled subscription, or a card update that restarts an unpaid one, does not send another.

### The room count email

Sent after MAYA measures more rooms in your property system than you pay for. The banner on the dashboard and the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page appears at once. The email follows at the next daily check (once a day), so it can arrive the day after. It goes to the address on your billing account, the one that receives invoices, and your 7 days count from the email. Headed "Your room count has changed", it says what your system lists and what your plan covers, what you pay today and what you would pay at the measured count, that you have 7 days to set the number yourself, and the date after which MAYA updates it. Its button, <Ui>Set your room count</Ui>, opens the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page. A new one goes out if the measured number changes again. [Your room count and what you pay](/docs/billing/your-room-count) has the whole sequence.

### The nudge before a charge when nothing is connected

Sent to a property with a subscription, on a trial or paid, whose property system is not connected (never connected, or disconnected), shortly before a charge. It is triggered when Stripe announces the upcoming invoice. How many days ahead that happens is a Stripe setting. A connected property never gets it, even one whose connection is showing a problem.

It says that your first payment (or renewal) of a stated amount is scheduled for a stated date. It goes on to say that your property system is not connected yet, so your rules have not been able to run, and that connecting takes about two minutes. Then it reads "On Mews? We connect it for you. Reply to this email or write to info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com and we'll set it up with you." Its button reads <Ui>Finish setting up MAYA</Ui>. A reply to it reaches us. It also states what you are paying for ("20 rooms at $5.50 per room, per month") and says that if that number is wrong, connecting first opens your <Ui>Billing</Ui> page, where you can change it before the charge.

If your property uses Mews, we connect it for you, and until we have, this email can still arrive. As it says, reply to it or [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Connect%20Mews) and we connect it with you. Its button leads to the connect screen, where Mews is listed as <Ui>Not yet available</Ui>, so pressing it does not connect Mews.

### The connection-down email

Sent once the <Ui>Connection</Ui> card on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab has read <Ui>Disconnected</Ui> or <Ui>Error</Ui> for about an hour. It goes to every General Manager and Hotel Admin on the property, at the address they sign in with, and our team is told at the same time. <Ui>Degraded</Ui> never sends it, because MAYA is still reading and sending then.

It goes once per outage. Moving between <Ui>Disconnected</Ui> and <Ui>Error</Ui> is the same outage. If the connection comes back and later breaks again, a new email follows about an hour after the new break.

Headed "MAYA has lost its connection to Cloudbeds" (or your system), it says:

- **When it went down**, in your property's time.
- **What happened.** For example, the app was uninstalled or disconnected in Cloudbeds, your system refused to renew MAYA's access, or Mews refused MAYA's keys three reads in a row.
- **What it means for your prices.** MAYA can't read your bookings, so your prices aren't updating. When you are live on Cloudbeds or ThinkReservations, it also says no prices are sent, and that the rates already in your system stay as they are.
- **How to reconnect.** On Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations: open the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab, click <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> (or <Ui>Reconnect Think Reservations</Ui>) and sign in with a login for this property only. On Mews: reply to the email and we arrange new keys with you.

Its button, <Ui>Open the PMS tab</Ui>, opens MAYA on that tab, for the property you last had open in MAYA. If you look after more than one property, the email adds a line: if the tab opens on another property, pick this one in the <Ui>Property</Ui> dropdown. A reply reaches us. When the connection reads <Ui>Disconnected</Ui>, the email adds that you can ignore it if you disconnected MAYA on purpose.

It is not sent when your subscription has stopped (cancelled, or unpaid after the bank's retries), because MAYA has paused work on the property anyway. It is not sent when we remove your Mews keys at your request. Read [reconnecting and disconnecting](/docs/connect/reconnect-and-disconnect).

### The overwrites email

Sent only while your setting in <Ui>Settings</Ui> is <Ui>MAYA's price wins</Ui> (Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations). Each time a rate MAYA sent is changed or removed in your system, MAYA sends its own price again ([When MAYA's price wins](/docs/watch/rates-changed-in-your-pms#when-mayas-price-wins)). Once a day is over at your property, in its own time zone, one email lists that day's nights. It goes to every General Manager and Hotel Admin on the property, at the address they sign in with. A day with no overwrites sends none, and there is never more than one a day.

Headed "MAYA overwrote rate changes in Cloudbeds" (or ThinkReservations), it says:

- **What happened.** On that day, rates in your system were changed or removed on nights MAYA had sent a price to, and because your setting is "MAYA's price wins", MAYA overwrote each change again with its own price.
- **Each night and room type once**, on a line with the rate your system had (or "Removed") and MAYA's price, nearest night first, and how many times that day MAYA sent it again. After 40 lines it says how many more there are, and that the <Ui>Change Log</Ui> shows the newest ones.
- **How to set a price by hand**: in MAYA's calendar, not in your system.

Its button, <Ui>Open the calendar</Ui>, opens MAYA's calendar for the property you last had open in MAYA. If you look after more than one property, the email adds a line: if the calendar opens on another property, pick this one in the <Ui>Property</Ui> dropdown.

### The other pricing tool email

Sent while your setting is <Ui>Keep the change as your price</Ui> (as it starts) when the rates changed in your system on nights MAYA sent to look like another pricing tool at work: 20 or more in one read, or changes on 3 or more different days within 7 days ([When something else seems to change your rates](/docs/watch/rates-changed-in-your-pms#when-something-else-seems-to-change-your-rates)). It goes to every General Manager and Hotel Admin, at the address they sign in with, at most once every 7 days. The <Ui>Change Log</Ui> gets the same warning at the same time.

Headed "Something other than MAYA seems to be changing rates in Cloudbeds", it says how many rates in your system were changed on nights MAYA had sent a price to in the last 7 days, that each change is kept as your price so MAYA isn't pricing those nights, and that if you use another pricing tool, you can turn on "MAYA's price wins" in Settings. If you made the changes yourself, there is nothing to do. Its button, <Ui>Open Settings</Ui>, opens Settings at your system's section, for the property you last had open in MAYA, with the same <Ui>Property</Ui> line as the other emails when you look after more than one.

It is not sent if you turn <Ui>MAYA's price wins</Ui> on before it goes out.

Neither of these two is sent when your subscription has stopped.

## The account confirmation email

When you create an account, you get an email with a link to confirm your address. Opening it signs you in. It comes from the sign-in service MAYA uses, not from MAYA's own emails. If it does not arrive, <Ui>Send the link again</Ui> on the "Check your email" screen sends a new one. [Create an account and sign in](/docs/account/create-an-account-and-sign-in#confirming-your-email) shows that screen and what to do next.

## The password reset email

When you click <Ui>Forgot password?</Ui> on the sign-in page and send your address, an email with a link to set a new password goes to that address, if it has a MAYA account. Like the confirmation email, it comes from the sign-in service MAYA uses. Open the link in the browser where you asked for it. It works once. [Reset your password](/docs/recipes/reset-your-password) has the steps and what to do if it does not arrive.

## Emails that come from Stripe

Stripe, not MAYA, sends everything about money: receipts and invoices, a failed-payment message, a card-expiring warning, and the reminder before the first charge at the end of a trial that the Terms promise. Which of these Stripe sends, and when, is set on Stripe's side. MAYA cannot resend them, and your <Ui>Billing</Ui> page is always the place that is right. They go to the email address on your billing account.

## Emails that do not exist

- **A change log digest or a price alert.** There is no email when a night's price changes. The <Ui>Change Log</Ui> tab is the record. The one daily email about prices is the overwrites email, and only under <Ui>MAYA's price wins</Ui>.
- **An email about the <Ui>Rules that keep adjusting</Ui> banner.** The question is asked on the dashboard only.
- **A trial-ending reminder from MAYA.** Any reminder comes from Stripe, if Stripe's settings send one.
- **A "your subscription was cancelled" email from MAYA.** The <Ui>Billing</Ui> page shows the status.

## Changing the billing email

The address that receives invoices, receipts, the room count email and the nudge is the one on your billing account in Stripe. The account ready email goes to the address you sign in with instead, and the connection-down, overwrites and other pricing tool emails to the sign-in address of each General Manager and Hotel Admin. Change the billing email in Stripe's billing portal, opened from <Ui>Update card, receipts & cancellation</Ui> on the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page. Changing it does not change the address you sign in with, which cannot be changed in the app yet.

## The sending address

MAYA's seven emails all come from one sending address that we set on our side. It is not shown anywhere in the app. If your IT team wants to allow our emails through a spam filter, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Sending%20address) and we send the exact address and domain.

In the meantime, two things help. Every subject in the table above contains "MAYA". A reply to the team invitation goes to the person who sent it, not to us. A reply to the account ready email, the nudge before a charge, the connection-down email, the overwrites email or the other pricing tool email comes to us.

The other emails come from elsewhere: anything about money from Stripe, and the account confirmation and password reset emails from the sign-in service. Allow those separately.

## If an email did not arrive

- Check spam, and search for "MAYA" in the subject. To have your IT team allow our emails, read [the sending address](#the-sending-address).
- For an invitation: if the person has signed in to MAYA before, no email is sent (the <Ui>Team</Ui> page said "Added."), because they are on the property already. Ask them to sign in and pick it in the <Ui>Property</Ui> selector. If they have never signed in and the email is lost, remove them on the <Ui>Team</Ui> page and invite them again for a fresh link, or [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=New%20invitation%20link) for a new link.
- For a password reset: check spam, and check you typed the address you sign in with. Wait a minute before asking again. Then [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Password%20reset) from that address.
- For the account confirmation: check spam, then [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Confirmation%20email) with the address you used.
- For the account ready email: it went to the address you sign in with, once, soon after you paid. It is only sent in the first day after you pay, so if confirming the payment took longer than that, none comes. You do not need it to carry on: sign in and MAYA takes you to the next step.
- For the room count email or the nudge: it went to the billing email, which may not be the address you sign in with.
- For the connection-down email: it goes only to General Managers and Hotel Admins, at the address they sign in with, about an hour after the <Ui>Connection</Ui> card first read <Ui>Disconnected</Ui> or <Ui>Error</Ui>, and only once for that outage. <Ui>Degraded</Ui> sends none. Check the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab, which is always up to date.
- For the overwrites email: it goes only under <Ui>MAYA's price wins</Ui>, to General Managers and Hotel Admins, the morning after a day with overwrites (your property's time), at most once a day. The <Ui>Change Log</Ui> shows the newest overwrites as they happen.
- For the other pricing tool email: at most once every 7 days, and not while <Ui>MAYA's price wins</Ui> is on. The <Ui>Change Log</Ui> shows the same warning.
- For a receipt or a Stripe reminder: the same billing email, and it depends on Stripe's settings.
- Still nothing? [Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Missing%20email) and say which email and which address.
