<InPlainWords>
Connect from inside MAYA after paying: pick <Ui>Think Reservations</Ui>, sign in on their site, and you are brought back. MAYA reads your bookings, room types and rate types, and sends nightly prices to your Best Available rate type. ThinkReservations accepts each price and applies it shortly after. MAYA reads your rates back within about an hour to check the price landed.
</InPlainWords>

ThinkReservations connects from inside MAYA and receives prices, so a ThinkReservations property gets the full path: the history import, starter rules, the calendar, going live and sending. This page covers how to connect, what MAYA reads, which rate type it writes to, and what works differently from Cloudbeds, including how you connect, how rooms are counted, how a send is checked, and how a disconnect or a reconnect shows up.

## Connecting

There is one way in. You create your MAYA account, pay, and then connect. ThinkReservations has no marketplace listing for MAYA, so there is no free trial without a code, and a code may be needed at checkout. Read [codes and the free trial](/docs/pay/codes-and-the-free-trial).

<Steps>
<Step>
**Create your account and pay.** At checkout, choose <Ui>Think Reservations</Ui> as your system. If the code field is required, enter the code we gave you.
</Step>
<Step>
**On the screen titled <Ui>Connect your property system</Ui>, click <Ui>Connect →</Ui> beside Think Reservations.** The card says <Ui>Sign in with your Think Reservations account</Ui>. There is nothing to copy and nothing to set up on the ThinkReservations side.
</Step>
<Step>
**Sign in on ThinkReservations' website and approve.** MAYA asks ThinkReservations for permission to read your bookings, rooms, rates and availability, to write rates, and to stay connected without asking you to sign in again. It does not ask for guest details. Finish within 15 minutes. If it takes longer, you land on a page that says "That sign-in link ran out after 15 minutes. Start again from MAYA." with an <Ui>Open MAYA</Ui> link. Nothing is wrong and nothing was set up: go back to the connect screen and start again.
</Step>
<Step>
**You are brought back to MAYA.** MAYA names the property from ThinkReservations, reads its time zone and currency, and starts the history import. A property whose currency MAYA doesn't price in yet stops here instead, with "Your property's system uses KRW. MAYA doesn't price in KRW yet, so nothing was set up or imported. Email us and we'll tell you when it does." (with its code), and nothing is set up. So does a property that is already in MAYA under someone else, with "This Think Reservations property is already in MAYA, so nothing new was added. Ask its General Manager to invite you, or email us." [Which currencies MAYA works in](/docs/reference/time-weekends-currency-and-holidays#currency) has the list. If a property with that name already exists on MAYA, a "(2)" is added. You can rename it in the questions step. Read [the history import](/docs/review/the-history-import).
</Step>
</Steps>

Every property starts in simulation. Nothing is sent to ThinkReservations until you go live. Read [simulation mode](/docs/live/simulation-mode).

Sending to ThinkReservations also has its own switch on MAYA's side, apart from Cloudbeds', and it starts off. Until MAYA switches it on, a live property is priced as usual but nothing is sent. [Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Sending%20to%20ThinkReservations) to ask whether it is on for you.

<Callout kind="good-to-know" title="One permission MAYA asks for but does not use">
The list ThinkReservations shows you includes reading availability. MAYA asks for it but makes no availability call today. It never reads how many rooms are free on a night, and it never changes availability. Read [what MAYA reads and writes](/docs/connect/what-maya-reads-and-writes).
</Callout>

### Messages you can meet on the way in

- **"Think Reservations needs an access code to connect. Use the system you signed up with, or get in touch and we'll sort you out."** You paid without a code, because the system you chose at checkout did not need one, and ThinkReservations does. Any code used at checkout counts, whichever system you chose there. A code can only be entered at checkout, so [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Access%20code%20for%20ThinkReservations) and we sort you out.
- **"Payment is needed before connecting a PMS."** The subscription has not gone through yet. Go back to the payment screen and finish it.
- **A page headed "We hit a snag connecting your property"** with the reason and a <Ui>Try again</Ui> link. Most reasons end in "Try again in a moment." and mean exactly that. Read [messages you might see, explained](/docs/wrong/messages-explained).

### One property per login

If your ThinkReservations login can reach several hotels, the connect step stops with "This Think Reservations login covers 3 properties. MAYA signs one property up at a time right now" (with your own number) and no <Ui>Try again</Ui> link. MAYA cannot tell which of them you paid for, and picking one could price the wrong hotel. Reply to your receipt or [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=ThinkReservations%20group) and we set the properties up by hand, each as its own property in MAYA. There is no group flow for ThinkReservations yet.

## What MAYA reads from ThinkReservations

On every cycle, about every five minutes, MAYA reads from ThinkReservations:

- **Bookings** made or changed since the last read. A booking that holds several rooms arrives with one entry per room. MAYA keeps each room's room type, its nights, and its nightly room charge.
- **Room types**, by name, and your **list of rooms**, which MAYA counts room type by room type (see below).

About once an hour, MAYA reads your **rate types** (their names, whether each is standard or follows another, and which room types each covers) and your **own nightly rates** for the next 396 nights from the rate type it sends to. Once a day it re-reads the whole booking window, so a booking missed in between is picked up within the day.

At connect, MAYA reads the property's **name, time zone and currency** once. They are not refreshed afterwards. If you change your time zone or currency in ThinkReservations later, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Time%20zone%20or%20currency) and we change it in MAYA.

The **nightly rate** MAYA stores is the room charge on the booking, after any discount, placed on the night it was billed for. Pet fees, packages and other items are never read as a room rate. If a booking's room charges are not dated night by night, the room total is split evenly across its nights.

The **booking date** is the moment ThinkReservations recorded the booking, read on your property's own calendar. A booking made at 11 pm at a property in California is dated that day, not the next.

ThinkReservations sends guest details with every booking even though MAYA does not ask for them. MAYA drops them before anything is stored: names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, dietary notes, arrival times and special requests never reach MAYA's database. Read [what MAYA reads and writes](/docs/connect/what-maya-reads-and-writes).

## Which bookings count

MAYA keeps a booking when ThinkReservations lists it as **scheduled**, **checked in** or **checked out**. A booking marked **cancelled** or **no show** is removed on the next read, and its nights come off the count. Cancelling changes the booking, so the cancellation arrives in the same five-minute read as everything else.

Nights run from check-in up to, but not including, check-out. A same-day booking is one night. Each room in a booking is one booked room on each of its nights.

For occupancy, each room counts. For booking speed, a booking counts once however many rooms it holds. A 4-room booking for one night is 4 booked rooms and 1 booking. Read [booking speed](/docs/rules/booking-speed).

## How many rooms each room type has

On every cycle MAYA reads ThinkReservations' list of rooms and counts the rooms in each room type, so each room type has its own count. The <Ui>Room types</Ui> block on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab shows it as units beside each one. A room is left out when ThinkReservations marks it as inactive, as not a room, or as not included in occupancy. A room you do not sell online still counts, because it can still be sold at the desk. A room type with no rooms in the list counts as 0.

If a count looks wrong, check those three settings on the rooms in ThinkReservations. MAYA reads the change on the next cycle. If ThinkReservations cannot send the list of rooms on a cycle, MAYA keeps the counts it already has.

Sellable occupancy divides by these counts, and so does the room count on your bill, over the room types ticked as rooms. Read [your room count and what you pay](/docs/billing/your-room-count).

<Example title="Three sizes of room at The Harbour Inn">
The Harbour Inn has 12 Standard rooms, 6 Deluxe and 2 Suites in ThinkReservations, so MAYA reads 12, 6 and 2, and the property measures 20 rooms for its bill. On Fri, Nov 13 2026, 9 Standard and 5 Deluxe rooms are booked: Standard reads 75% full and Deluxe 83%.

<Ui>Rooms out of service</Ui> works on top of the counts: 3 Standard rooms out of service for that night leave 9 to sell, so Standard reads 100%.
</Example>

## Which rate type MAYA sends prices to

ThinkReservations marks each rate type as standard or as following another rate type (derived). MAYA sends its prices to your **Best Available** rate type: the standard rate type whose name contains "Best Available". A rate type called "Best Available Non-Refundable" has those words too, and MAYA may write to it instead, so give exactly one standard rate type a name with "Best Available" in it.

If no rate type is called Best Available, MAYA uses the one standard rate type that covers the most room types. That could be a non-refundable or a package rate type, because ThinkReservations marks those standard too. If two standard rate types tie for that, neither is used, and nothing is sent until one of them is named Best Available. A room type the chosen rate type does not cover is not sent to. MAYA never hands a room type's price to a non-refundable or package rate type instead.

Derived rate types reprice themselves from their parent by ThinkReservations' own rules, so writing the Best Available rate is the whole job. A non-refundable rate that follows Best Available at 10% off moves when MAYA moves Best Available. MAYA never writes to a derived rate type and never creates a rate type. A non-refundable or package rate type is written to only when it is the one chosen above, so name your main rate type Best Available.

<Example title="Which of my ThinkReservations rate types will MAYA change?">
For your property, ask in this order:

1. **Is there a standard rate type called Best Available?** If yes, that is the one MAYA changes, for every room type it covers.
2. **If not, which standard rate type covers the most room types?** If exactly one does, MAYA changes that one.
3. **Is a room type left out?** A room type not covered by the chosen rate type is priced on the calendar but not sent. Add it to that rate type in ThinkReservations.

At The Harbour Inn, "Best Available" covers Standard, Deluxe and Suite. "Non-Refundable" follows Best Available at 10% off. "Romance Package" is a package. MAYA writes each room type's price to Best Available. Non-Refundable follows on its own. Romance Package is untouched.
</Example>

### When a room type has no base rate

If no rate type MAYA can send to covers a room type, that room type is priced on the calendar but not sent, and the change log shows a "Prices not reaching Think Reservations" entry: "MAYA can't find a base rate for Deluxe in Think Reservations, so its rates can't be changed", with the action "Add a base rate for this room type in Think Reservations." Add the room type to your Best Available rate type in ThinkReservations. MAYA re-reads your rate types and sends the held prices on a later cycle. Read [when your property system does not accept a price](/docs/live/when-your-pms-does-not-accept-a-price).

## How a send is checked

ThinkReservations does not apply a price the instant it receives it. It answers "accepted" and applies the price shortly after, and there is no job for MAYA to ask about afterwards, as there is with Cloudbeds. So MAYA checks by reading. About once an hour it reads your rates from ThinkReservations, and a send counts as landed when that read finds MAYA's price there.

Until a send has been read back, a rate you change in ThinkReservations on that night is not kept as a manual price, because MAYA cannot yet tell your change from a price of its own that has not landed. Once the send has been read back and is at least 60 minutes old, a rate you change there is kept, exactly as on Cloudbeds. Read [rates you change in your property system](/docs/watch/rates-changed-in-your-pms).

If ThinkReservations accepts a batch and then never applies it, MAYA's calendar shows MAYA's price while ThinkReservations still holds the old one. The next time that night's price changes, MAYA sends it again. Nothing is sent again at the same price on its own. If a night in ThinkReservations still shows the old rate an hour after MAYA's calendar changed, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=ThinkReservations%20rate%20not%20applied) the night and room type and we check it.

<Callout kind="careful" title="A price of 0 is never sent">
MAYA does not send a price of 0 to ThinkReservations. A night you set to 0 in MAYA is a comp night that you set to 0 in ThinkReservations yourself. The save says so: "Saved. MAYA doesn't send a price of 0 to Think Reservations, so set the night to 0 there yourself."
</Callout>

## Permission problems

If ThinkReservations lets MAYA read but refuses the write, the change log shows "Think Reservations won't let MAYA change Standard rates because MAYA doesn't have permission to update rates", with the action "Give MAYA permission to update rates in Think Reservations, then reconnect on the PMS tab." Reads, pricing and the calendar carry on. The held prices go out on the cycle after you reconnect.

A refused write never disconnects a ThinkReservations property. The connection stays up, and only the sending waits.

## Disconnecting and reconnecting

ThinkReservations does not tell MAYA when access has been removed. MAYA reads the refusal when its next call fails. A failed read is tried again with a growing wait, up to an hour between tries, and nothing is priced or sent while reads fail. When ThinkReservations refuses to renew MAYA's access, the <Ui>Connection</Ui> card on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab reads <Ui>Error</Ui> and the banner "MAYA has lost its connection to Think Reservations" appears for a General Manager or Hotel Admin, with a <Ui>Reconnect Think Reservations</Ui> button. If it still reads <Ui>Error</Ui> an hour later, every General Manager and Hotel Admin gets one email saying so. Read [emails MAYA sends](/docs/billing/emails-maya-sends#the-connection-down-email). Until the status changes, the tab can still read <Ui>Connected</Ui> while calls are failing. If prices have stopped and the tab still reads <Ui>Connected</Ui>, a General Manager or Hotel Admin can click the quiet <Ui>Reconnect Think Reservations</Ui> link on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab anyway. Which ThinkReservations login you sign in with matters, so read the next paragraph first.

To reconnect, click <Ui>Reconnect Think Reservations</Ui> on the banner or on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab, sign in on ThinkReservations' site with a login that reaches this hotel and no other, and you are back on the dashboard's <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab with the note "Reconnected." Your rules, history and settings are untouched. Finish signing in within 15 minutes. If it takes longer, you see "That sign-in link ran out after 15 minutes. Start again from MAYA." Nothing has changed: click <Ui>Open MAYA</Ui> and start again from the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab. Syncing resumes within about five minutes, or up to an hour if reads had been failing for a while. Read [reconnecting and disconnecting](/docs/connect/reconnect-and-disconnect).

<Callout kind="careful" title="Reconnect with a login for this hotel only">
A reconnect clears which hotel MAYA reads, and the next read uses the hotel the new login reaches. A login to one other hotel moves this property onto that hotel: MAYA reads its bookings and, once you are live, sends prices to it. A login that reaches several hotels makes every read fail, while the tab still reads <Ui>Connected</Ui> and <Ui>Last sync</Ui> stops moving.
</Callout>

If <Ui>Last sync</Ui> stops moving after a reconnect, reconnect again with a login that reaches only this hotel. If your only login reaches several hotels (we set those properties up by hand), or you signed in to another hotel by mistake, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=ThinkReservations%20reconnect) and we sort it out.

MAYA has no disconnect button of its own. To remove MAYA's access in ThinkReservations, cancel your subscription first, then [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Disconnect%20ThinkReservations) and we walk you through it.

MAYA never sees your ThinkReservations password. ThinkReservations gives MAYA its own access to work with. If prices stop updating after you change your password, a General Manager or Hotel Admin can click <Ui>Reconnect Think Reservations</Ui> on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab, even while the tab still reads <Ui>Connected</Ui>.

## The PMS tab for a ThinkReservations property

The <Ui>Connection</Ui> card and <Ui>Last sync</Ui> are right for ThinkReservations. The <Ui>Recent requests to Think Reservations</Ui> list and the <Ui>Health (last 24 hours)</Ui> card both read "Not tracked for this system", because MAYA keeps a log of its requests to Cloudbeds only. It does not mean syncing has stopped. <Ui>Last sync</Ui> is the number to look at. Read [the PMS tab](/docs/watch/the-pms-tab).

<Callout kind="not-yet" title="Not yet">
There is no Marketplace install for ThinkReservations, so no free trial without a code. ThinkReservations does not tell MAYA about a disconnect (MAYA finds the refusal on its next call). A login covering several hotels cannot connect on its own (we set them up by hand). The PMS tab keeps no request log for ThinkReservations.
</Callout>

<Example title="The Harbour Inn goes live on ThinkReservations">
The Harbour Inn's Best Available rate for Standard is $150 on Fri, Nov 13 2026. That night is booking faster than similar nights did, so the starter rule "Warm-date bump" raises Standard 10%, from $150 to $165. (It is a booking speed rule, which counts bookings rather than rooms, so it can fire whatever the room count is.) On the next cycle, MAYA sends $165 to Best Available for that night. ThinkReservations answers "accepted" and applies it a moment later. The Non-Refundable rate, which follows Best Available at 10% off, now shows $148.50 in ThinkReservations without MAYA touching it.

Within the hour, MAYA reads its rates back from ThinkReservations, finds $165 on that night, and marks the send as landed. Two hours later, Sam changes the night to $175 in ThinkReservations by hand. On MAYA's next hourly read, the night shows "Changed in Think Reservations · $175.00" in MAYA and is kept as a manual price.
</Example>
