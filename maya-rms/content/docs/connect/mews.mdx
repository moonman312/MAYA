<InPlainWords>
Mews is read-only for now, and costs the same as any other property. We connect it for you with keys from Mews. MAYA reads your bookings and room categories about every five minutes and shows what your rules would do on the calendar and in the change log, but it sends nothing to Mews. There is no history import for Mews yet, so there are no starter rules at connect.
</InPlainWords>

With Mews, MAYA is a place to write rules and watch them run against your real bookings, without any price reaching Mews. It is useful for trying rules out. Here is what a Mews property does not have yet: sending, your own rates, a room count for each room category, the history import and a connect screen you can use yourself. This page says exactly what a Mews property gets today.

| MAYA reads your own rates | Not yet |

## How a Mews property is connected

The screen titled <Ui>Connect your property system</Ui> lists Mews under <Ui>Coming soon</Ui> with the label <Ui>Not yet available</Ui>. You cannot click it. Instead, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Connect%20Mews) after you pay and we connect Mews for you.

Mews connects with keys rather than a sign-in. One key identifies MAYA and we hold it, so you never need to find a client token. The other is an access token for your property, which Mews creates when your property is connected to MAYA. If your Mews account covers more than one enterprise, we also need this property's Enterprise ID.

We arrange a secure way to share keys when we connect you, so never paste them into an ordinary email. We test the keys against Mews, save them, and mark the property as connected. MAYA only ever talks to Mews' own service, and nothing is installed on your computers.

MAYA does not copy your property's name, time zone or currency from Mews, so we set them with you when we connect it. We also set how many rooms MAYA counts for each room category (more on that below). Check the time zone with us. The calendar, the booking window and every "past week" count run on it.

Every property starts in simulation, and for Mews it stays effectively that way, because nothing is sent either way. Read [simulation mode](/docs/live/simulation-mode).

## What a Mews property pays

A Mews property pays the same as any other property: the same price per room, in the same brackets, monthly or yearly. There is no lower price while nothing is sent to Mews. Read [what it costs](/docs/start/what-it-costs).

You pay before we connect Mews, and you pick <Ui>Mews</Ui> on the payment screen. A code may be needed: the field reads <Ui>Your code</Ui> when one is required and <Ui>Your code (optional)</Ui> when it is not.

There is no free trial for Mews. The trial (7 days) comes only with the Cloudbeds Marketplace. So unless a code gives free days, your first charge is when you finish checkout. Read [codes and the free trial](/docs/pay/codes-and-the-free-trial).

Your bill is per room, and MAYA checks the count you pay for against the rooms it counts for Mews. On Mews that is one number for each room category (read below), so it can come out higher than the rooms you run. Check the <Ui>Room count</Ui> section of the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page after we connect you, and [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Mews%20room%20counts) if it is higher. Read [your room count and what you pay](/docs/billing/your-room-count).

## What MAYA reads from Mews

On every cycle, about every five minutes, MAYA reads from Mews:

- **Bookings** (Mews calls them reservations) made or changed since the last read, with their room category, arrival and departure, and total price.
- **Room categories** (Mews' name for room types), by name. MAYA does not read room counts from Mews yet, so it uses one number for all of them (read below).

Once a day, MAYA re-reads the whole window: 30 days back and about 13 months ahead. A booking missed in between is picked up within the day.

The **nightly rate** MAYA stores comes from the booking's total price. MAYA adds up the charges on the booking and splits the total evenly across its nights, so a 3-night stay at $450 is stored as $150 a night, even when its nights were charged at different prices. That total includes taxes, and it can include extras on the booking as well as the room, so it can sit above your room rate.

The **booking date** is the moment Mews recorded the booking as created, read on your property's own calendar.

MAYA does not read your rate plans, your availability, or any guest details from Mews. Guest fields that arrive with a booking are dropped before anything is stored. Read [what MAYA reads and writes](/docs/connect/what-maya-reads-and-writes).

## Which bookings count

MAYA removes a booking only when Mews marks it **Canceled**. Every other state it reads counts as a booked room: **Confirmed**, **Started**, **Processed**, and also **Optional**, **Enquired** and **Requested**. So an option held for a group, or an enquiry not yet firm, counts towards occupancy and booking speed exactly like a confirmed booking.

If you hold many options that never firm up, occupancy and booking speed read higher than your firm bookings. There is no setting in MAYA for this yet. Cancelling an option in Mews takes it off the count on the next read. Mews records a no-show as a cancelled reservation, so a no-show comes off the count like any cancellation.

A Mews booking is one room, so one booking is one booked room on each of its nights and one booking for booking speed. Nights run from arrival up to, but not including, departure.

## How many rooms each room category has

MAYA does not read room counts from Mews yet, so it uses one number for every category at your property. We set that number with you when we connect Mews. The <Ui>Room types</Ui> block on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab shows it as "units" beside each category.

Sellable occupancy is rooms booked divided by rooms you can sell, so this number is the bottom half of every occupancy figure. It is right for a category that has exactly that many rooms and off for every other. A rule that measures several categories adds their numbers together. Booking window rules that also check occupancy depend on it too.

<Example title="One number for The Harbour Inn's room categories">
The Harbour Inn has 12 Standard, 6 Deluxe and 2 Suite rooms, and a Meeting Room that is unticked as not a room. On Mews, every category gets the same number, but the Meeting Room adds nothing because it is unticked. With 100, a night with 9 Standard rooms booked reads 9% full for Standard, and "Busy nights" (occupancy Greater than 80) does not fire. With 7, the three room categories add up to 21 rooms, close to the real 20, so a rule that measures all three reads close to the truth. A rule that measures Standard alone then reads 9 booked of 7, which is 129%, and fires on a night that is not full.
</Example>

There is no way to give each category its own number yet, and no field in MAYA to change the number yourself. If the units on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab look wrong, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Mews%20room%20counts) with your room counts and we correct it. Read [sellable occupancy](/docs/rules/sellable-occupancy).

## Nothing is sent to Mews

A Mews property has no starter rules, so the review has no go-live button. Going live would change nothing in Mews anyway. If you want the property to show as live, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Show%20my%20Mews%20property%20as%20live). Live or not, the calendar and the change log show the price your rules set for every night, which is what would have been sent.

Prices you type are saved and shown on the calendar the same way, and they are not sent either. In simulation the line after a save reads "Saved (simulation: not sent to Mews)." On a property we have set to show as live, it can read "Saved. Sending to Mews now." or "Saved. Sending to Mews on the next cycle (about 5 min)." Nothing is sent to Mews whatever that line says. If you want a price in Mews, set it there yourself.

Because nothing is sent, nothing is ever read back either. A rate you change in Mews is never shown as "Changed in Mews" and never kept as a manual price.

No date is promised for sending prices to Mews. To hear when that changes, join the waitlist on get-maya.com, or [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Sending%20prices%20to%20Mews) and we will tell you.

## Where a night's starting price comes from

MAYA does not read your rate plans from Mews, so it has no "your own rate" for a night. A night's starting price is a price you typed for it, else the nightly rate of the newest booking on that night for that room category, else the starting price that night had last time. A future night with no bookings yet and no earlier starting price is not priced at all until one arrives. Read [how rules run](/docs/rules/how-rules-run).

<Example title="The Harbour Inn on Mews">
The Harbour Inn's newest Standard booking for Fri, Nov 13 2026 was taken at $150 a night. "Busy nights" fires and the night goes to $165. The calendar shows $165 and the change log names the rule. Mews still shows whatever rate The Harbour Inn has set there. Sam types $175 for that night in MAYA. The calendar shows "Manual · $175.00", and Mews is still unchanged. To sell at $175, Sam sets it in Mews.
</Example>

## No history import yet

Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations properties get a history import at connect, a review of what MAYA found, and starter rules built from that history. Mews does not have that yet. A Mews property's history is what the reads cover: 30 days back at connect, growing by a day each day. If you would like us to read further back once, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Mews%20history) and we can read up to a year back. Read [the history import](/docs/review/the-history-import).

<Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui> on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab reads your history the same way, so on a Mews property it is greyed out, with the note "Not available for Mews yet. MAYA can't read its booking history." Build your rules in the rule builder. Read [build your first rule](/docs/rules/build-your-first-rule).

## When the keys stop working

If your Mews keys stop working, MAYA's reads fail quietly. Nothing on screen warns you: the <Ui>Connection</Ui> card on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab still reads <Ui>Connected</Ui>, and no banner appears above the calendar. The sign to look for is <Ui>Last sync</Ui> on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab, which stops moving.

MAYA keeps trying, less often after each failed read, until it tries about once an hour. While reads fail, nothing new is priced. Your rules, history and settings are untouched, and the first read that works catches up on everything that changed in between.

[Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Mews%20keys) and we arrange new keys with you and save them. Leave the keys themselves out of the email: we set up a secure way to share them. There is no button for this in MAYA.

When we remove the keys, the <Ui>Connection</Ui> card reads <Ui>Disconnected</Ui> and reads stop. The <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab and the banner above the calendar then show "Mews needs its keys re-entered", with the words "Send us a message and we'll walk through it". A Revenue Manager or Viewer sees "MAYA has lost its connection to Mews" instead. That panel says reconnecting takes one click. For Mews it does not: email us and we arrange new keys with you. Read [reconnecting and disconnecting](/docs/connect/reconnect-and-disconnect).

## The PMS tab for a Mews property

The <Ui>Connection</Ui> card changes only when we save or remove your keys, so it can read <Ui>Connected</Ui> while reads are failing. The <Ui>Recent requests to Mews</Ui> table stays empty today and the <Ui>Health (last 24 hours)</Ui> card reads <Ui>No recent activity</Ui>, because only calls to Cloudbeds are written to that log. <Ui>Last sync</Ui> is the number to look at. <Ui>Counts as a room</Ui> and <Ui>Rooms out of service</Ui> work the same as on any property. Read [the PMS tab](/docs/watch/the-pms-tab).

## Leaving

Cancel on the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page, then [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Disconnect%20Mews) and we remove the keys, so MAYA no longer reads from Mews. Nothing in Mews was ever changed by MAYA, so there is nothing to put back. Your rules and settings stay in MAYA. Read [Cancelling, restarting and leaving](/docs/billing/cancelling-restarting-and-leaving).

<Callout kind="not-yet" title="Not yet">
For Mews there is no sending (including prices you type), no read of your own rates, no room count per room category (one number is used for all), no warning on screen when the keys stop working (watch <Ui>Last sync</Ui>), no history import and no starter rules at connect, no <Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui>, no go-live, connect or reconnect from the app (email us), and no way to leave options and enquiries out of the count. No date is promised for any of these.
