<InPlainWords>
With Mews, you email us and we connect it for you. You build your rules in the rule builder, and MAYA reads your Mews bookings about every five minutes and shows on the calendar what your rules would do. Nothing is sent to Mews, so you set the prices you agree with in Mews yourself. Each step below links to the page that explains it.
</InPlainWords>

## Before you start

- **A MAYA account with payment set up**, and possibly a code. Read [Codes and the free trial](/docs/pay/codes-and-the-free-trial).
- **Nothing to find in Mews yet.** We set up MAYA's access with you. Read [How a Mews property is connected](/docs/connect/mews#how-a-mews-property-is-connected).

## Day one

<Steps>
<Step title="Pay">
Create your account and pay, choosing <Ui>Mews</Ui> as your system. Read [Setting up payment](/docs/pay/setting-up-payment).
</Step>
<Step title="Email us to connect">
The connect screen lists Mews under <Ui>Coming soon</Ui>, so [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Connect%20Mews) with your property's name, time zone and currency. We set up MAYA's access with you, and set the time zone, the currency and the room count MAYA uses.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the PMS tab">
<Ui>Last sync</Ui> should show a recent time. Check the units beside each room category, and tick or untick <Ui>Counts as a room</Ui> for each. Read [How many rooms each room category has](/docs/connect/mews#how-many-rooms-each-room-category-has).
</Step>
<Step title="Check your room count">
MAYA checks the rooms you pay for against the units of every category ticked as a room (21 in the example below, not 20). Open the <Ui>Room count</Ui> section of the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page, and [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Mews%20room%20counts) if it shows more rooms than you run. Read [Your room count and what you pay](/docs/billing/your-room-count).
</Step>
</Steps>

## The first days

- **No history import yet**, so there is no review and there are no starter rules. Read [No history import yet](/docs/connect/mews#no-history-import-yet).
- **Type your usual rates.** MAYA does not read your Mews rates, so a typed price gives your rules a starting point. It is never sent to Mews. Read [Where a night's starting price comes from](/docs/connect/mews#where-a-nights-starting-price-comes-from).
- **Type them before your rules run.** A price you type pauses any rule already raising or cutting that room category on that night. Rules that fire after it stack on it. Read [What your price does to the night](/docs/watch/setting-a-price-yourself#what-your-price-does-to-the-night).
- **Then build your rules.** Occupancy and booking window rules work from day one. Booking speed rules stay quiet until there are past nights to compare with. Read [Build your first rule](/docs/rules/build-your-first-rule).
- **Options count.** Optional, Enquired and Requested reservations count as booked until they are cancelled in Mews. Read [Which bookings count](/docs/connect/mews#which-bookings-count).

## A way to work with MAYA on Mews

Since nothing is sent, treat MAYA as the place where your rules are worked out, and the calendar as the sheet you price from.

1. Type your usual rate for each room category on the nights you sell, so rules have a starting point.
2. Build one or two occupancy rules and a booking window rule, and try them in the Rate Simulator.
3. Once a day, open the calendar and compare MAYA's prices with the rates in Mews. Where MAYA's price differs and you agree with it, set it in Mews.
4. Read the change log when a price surprises you. Each line names the rule and what it read. Read [The change log](/docs/watch/the-change-log).

## Going live

There is no go-live button for a Mews property today, because the button sits with the starter rules and Mews has none. Going live would not change anything in Mews anyway: nothing is sent to Mews, not even a price you type. Read [Nothing is sent to Mews](/docs/connect/mews#nothing-is-sent-to-mews).

## When something breaks

- **The keys stop working.** Nothing warns you on screen, and <Ui>Connection</Ui> can still read <Ui>Connected</Ui>. Watch <Ui>Last sync</Ui>: if it stops moving, email us. Read [When the keys stop working](/docs/connect/mews#when-the-keys-stop-working).
- **Occupancy looks wrong.** Check the units on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab, and enter blocked rooms under <Ui>Rooms out of service</Ui>. Read [Occupancy looks wrong](/docs/wrong/occupancy-looks-wrong).

## Leaving

Cancel on the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page, then [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Disconnect%20Mews) and we remove MAYA's access. Nothing in Mews was ever changed by MAYA, so there is nothing to put back. Read [Cancelling, restarting and leaving](/docs/billing/cancelling-restarting-and-leaving).

<Example title="The Harbour Inn on Mews, one Tuesday">
We set The Harbour Inn's room count to 7 for each of its three room categories: 21 rooms, close to its real 20. Its plan covers 20 rooms, so the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page reads "Your PMS currently shows 21 active rooms." and a room count email follows. If the count still reads 21 once 7 days have passed since that email, the plan moves to 21 rooms. On Tuesday, October 6, 2026, Priya opens the calendar. For Sat, Nov 14 2026 she had typed $150 on Standard. The night now has 18 rooms booked, 86% full, so "Busy nights" (occupancy Greater than 80, Increase 10%) has fired, and MAYA shows $165 on top of her $150. Mews still shows $150. Priya agrees with the raise and sets $165 in Mews. Two of those bookings are options held for a wedding party. If they are cancelled in Mews, the next read drops the night to 16 of 21, 76%, and the raise comes off.
</Example>

<Callout kind="not-yet">
For Mews there is no sending (including prices you type), no read of your own rates, no room count for each room category, no history import and no starter rules, no go-live button, and no connect or reconnect from the app (email us). Every category shares one number we set with you, so occupancy for one category is right only when your categories are the same size. Nothing warns you on screen when the keys stop working, and there is no way to leave options and enquiries out of the count.
</Callout>
