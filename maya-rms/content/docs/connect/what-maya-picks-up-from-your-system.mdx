<InPlainWords>
MAYA reads your bookings about every five minutes, your own rates about once an hour (Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations), and the whole booking window once a day. A new booking, a cancellation, a date change or a room move shows in MAYA on the next read. A rate you change in your system is read within the hour. Blocked rooms, allotments and restrictions are not read at all. Your property's name is read once, when you connect. On Cloudbeds the time zone and currency are checked again once a day; on ThinkReservations they are read once, when you connect; on Mews we set them with you.
</InPlainWords>

Three clocks set how soon a change in your system reaches MAYA. This page names them, then lists every change you can make and which clock it rides on. Where a system is different, its tab says so.

## The three clocks

| Clock | What is read | How often |
|---|---|---|
| The cycle | Bookings made or changed since the last read, and your room types | About every five minutes |
| The rates read | Your own nightly rate on the base rate plan, for tonight and the next 395 nights, and your rate plans | About once an hour, or when your hotel day changes (Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations only) |
| The daily re-read | Every booking in the window, 30 days back and about 13 months ahead | Once a day |

"Next read" below means the next cycle, up to about five minutes on a healthy connection. "Within the hour" means the next rates read.

## Every change, and when MAYA picks it up

Three kinds of booking people ask about:

- **An extended stay or a date change.** The added nights belong to the same booking and keep the date it was first made. They count as booked rooms from the next read, but for booking speed they are an old booking, so they rarely show in <Ui>Past day</Ui> or <Ui>Past week</Ui>.
- **A day-use booking** (in and out the same day). It is not skipped. In ThinkReservations and Mews it counts as one night on its date. In Cloudbeds it counts on each date Cloudbeds lists a room rate for, so on its date when Cloudbeds lists one. Either way it reads as a booked room that night.
- **A comp, house-use or owner stay at $0.** It counts by its status, like any booking: a booked room for occupancy and a booking for booking speed. It adds $0 to the calendar's revenue and lowers the ADR. Read [Comp, house-use and day-use bookings](/docs/connect/your-systems-words-and-mayas#comp-house-use-and-day-use-bookings).

## A test booking, made and then cancelled

A test booking counts like any other booking while it is in your system. What happened depends on whether a read found it before you cancelled it:

- **Cancelled before the next read** (within about five minutes). The read finds it already cancelled, so it is never counted and no price moves.
- **Still there at a read.** It counts as a booked room and a new booking on its nights, and your rules run with it on that cycle. The next read after you cancel it takes it off the count.

If your rules ran with it, here is what happens to what they did:

- **A rule with only occupancy and booking window conditions** follows the count. A change that needed the test booking comes off on the cycle that reads the cancellation, and a change it was holding back can now happen.
- **A booking speed or pickup change** comes off when its rule's undo box is ticked and the cancellation means the rule is no longer true. One test booking may not be enough, and bookings made since can keep the rule true. A cut made because a night was booking slowly never comes off this way. Read [When a price change comes off](/docs/rules/when-a-change-comes-off).

In simulation nothing was sent. If you are live on Cloudbeds or ThinkReservations, a price it moved was sent like any other (nothing is ever sent to Mews). To put a night straight back, type the price you want on the calendar. Read [Undo a price change](/docs/recipes/undo-a-price-change).

To test without moving any price, book a night more than 395 nights ahead and cancel it straight away. Your rules run on tonight and the next 395 nights only.

## Things MAYA never reads

From any system: availability by night, room blocks, out-of-order rooms, allotments, restrictions, minimum stays, closed-to-arrival flags and closed-to-departure flags. Guest details that come with a booking are dropped before anything is saved. Your property's name is read once, at connect, and never again. The time zone and currency depend on your system. On Cloudbeds they are checked once a day: MAYA follows a new time zone, and a new currency only while you are in simulation, so a property that connected before its time zone could be read, and started on UTC, is put right by the next daily read. On ThinkReservations they are read once, at connect. On Mews we set them with you. Read [what MAYA reads and writes](/docs/connect/what-maya-reads-and-writes).

## When it takes longer

- **A large property's first read** can take several cycles. Each cycle reads for a fixed slice of time and carries on from where it stopped. Until the whole window has been read, your rules run on what has arrived and nothing is sent.
- **A failing connection** is tried again with a growing wait, up to an hour between tries. Nothing is priced or sent on a cycle where the read failed, so bookings are never priced on stale data.
- **If your system keeps asking MAYA to slow down**, MAYA waits a minute or two before calling it again, and sends move to a later cycle.
- **Nights 396 or more days out** (past tonight and the next 395) are neither read for rates nor sent until they enter the window.
- **A rate you change within an hour of MAYA's send** is not yet kept as yours (or, with <Ui>MAYA's price wins</Ui> on, sent over), and MAYA's next price for that night can write over it. Wait an hour after a send before changing that night's rate by hand, or type the price in MAYA instead. Read [rates you change in your property system](/docs/watch/rates-changed-in-your-pms).

<Example title="A Tuesday at The Harbour Inn on Cloudbeds">
At 3:02 PM (US Pacific, the property's clock) a guest books a Standard room for Sat, Nov 14 2026. MAYA's next read, at about 3:06 PM, counts it. At 3:20 PM Priya changes that night's Standard rate in Cloudbeds from $150 to $160. MAYA last sent to that night at 2:10 PM, over an hour earlier, so on the next hourly rates read, by about 4:10 PM, the night shows "Changed in Cloudbeds · $160.00" and is kept. At 4:30 PM the front desk blocks room 204 for a plumber until Friday. MAYA does not see that. Priya records 1 unit of Standard out of service from Tue, Oct 6 2026 to Fri, Oct 9 2026 on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab, and sellable occupancy for those nights is right on the next run.
</Example>

<Callout kind="not-yet" title="Not yet">
There is no button to read your system right now. The cycle runs on its own about every five minutes. Blocks, allotments and restrictions are not read from any system. The name is read once, and so are the time zone and currency on ThinkReservations. Mews rates and Mews room counts are never read.
</Callout>
