<InPlainWords>
There is no password reset link yet. [Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Password%20reset) from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Password reset", and we send you a link to set a new one. The same goes for changing a password you still have.
</InPlainWords>

## Ask for a reset

<Steps>
<Step title="Write from your sign-up address">
Send an email to info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com from the address you use to sign in to MAYA. We match it against your account. If you cannot write from your sign-up address, tell us which address it was, and we will confirm it is you another way before sending a link.
</Step>
<Step title="Use the subject Password reset">
Subject: "Password reset". Nothing else is needed. Never put your old password in the email.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the new password from the link we send">
The link opens a page headed "Set your password". Type a password of at least 8 characters twice. Tick the box that reads <Ui>I agree to the Terms of Service and acknowledge the Privacy Policy</Ui>, then click <Ui>Set password & continue</Ui>. The button stays greyed out until the box is ticked. You land on the dashboard signed in.
</Step>
</Steps>

We aim to answer within one business day.

## Before you email

- "Invalid login credentials" on the sign-in page means a wrong email or password. Check the address, and check for a stray space or capital.
- If you created your account moments ago and were told to check your email, open the confirmation link first, then sign in.
- If a colleague invited you, use the link in the invitation email to set your password. An expired invitation link needs a new one from us: [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=New%20invitation%20link) from the invited address, as with a password reset. Your colleague cannot send one. You are on the property from the moment they invite you, so inviting you again sends no email. [Inviting and removing people](/docs/team/inviting-and-removing-people) has more.

<Callout kind="not-yet">
No reset link on the sign-in page, no way to change your email address or password inside MAYA, no two-factor sign-in and no Google or single sign-on. Passwords have a minimum of 8 characters and no other rule in MAYA.
</Callout>
