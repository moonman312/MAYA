<InPlainWords>
On the sign-in page, click <Ui>Forgot password?</Ui> under <Ui>Sign In</Ui>, type the email you sign in with and click <Ui>Send reset link</Ui>. Open the link in the email in the same browser, type a new password of at least 8 characters twice, and click <Ui>Save new password</Ui>. You land in MAYA, signed in.
</InPlainWords>

## Ask for a reset

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the reset form">
Go to maya-rms.com/login and click <Ui>Forgot password?</Ui>, just under the <Ui>Sign In</Ui> button. The card changes to "Reset your password", and any email you had typed stays in the box.
</Step>
<Step title="Send the link">
Type the email you sign in with and click <Ui>Send reset link</Ui>. The button reads "Sending..." for a moment, then the card says "Check your email": if that address has a MAYA account, a link to set a new password is on its way. The card says the same whether or not the address has an account, so it never tells anyone who uses MAYA.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the link in the same browser">
The email comes from the sign-in service MAYA uses. Open its link in the browser where you asked for it. It opens a page headed "Set a new password" with "Choose a new password for" your address. The link works once.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the new password">
Type a password of at least 8 characters in <Ui>New password</Ui> and again in <Ui>Confirm new password</Ui>, then click <Ui>Save new password</Ui>. The page says "Your new password is set. Taking you to MAYA…" and opens MAYA, signed in. There is no Terms box on this page: your account has one already.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Changing a password you still have

Use the same steps. There is no password setting inside MAYA, so open maya-rms.com/login, click <Ui>Forgot password?</Ui> and follow the link. You can do this while signed in.

## If it does not work

- **"Invalid login credentials"** on the sign-in page means a wrong email or password. Check the address, and check for a stray space or capital. If the password is lost, ask for a reset as above.
- **No email.** Check your spam folder, and check that you typed the address you sign in with. If you ask twice in quick succession, only the first sends a link, so wait a minute before asking again. Still nothing after a few minutes? [Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Password%20reset) from the address you sign in with.
- **"This link has expired or was already used. If you set your new password already, sign in. If not, ask for a new link."** Each link works once and runs out after a while. If you already set your password with it, sign in. If not, click <Ui>ask for a new link</Ui>, which opens the reset form again.
- **"Open this link in the browser where you asked for it. Or ask for a new link from this one."** You opened the link somewhere else, on your phone, say. Open it in the browser you asked from, or click <Ui>ask for a new link</Ui> and ask again from where you are.
- **"We couldn't check this link just now. Reload the page to try again."** The check got no answer. Reload the page. The link still works.
- **"That's the password you have now. Choose a different one."** Pick a password you do not use today.
- **"Password must be at least 8 characters."** or **"Passwords don't match."** Retype both boxes.
- **Invited by a colleague?** Use the link in the invitation email to set your first password. [Inviting and removing people](/docs/team/inviting-and-removing-people) covers an invitation link that has expired.

<Callout kind="not-yet">
No way to change your email address inside MAYA (email us), no two-factor sign-in and no Google or single sign-on. Passwords have a minimum of 8 characters and no other rule in MAYA.
</Callout>
