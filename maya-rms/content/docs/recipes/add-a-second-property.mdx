<InPlainWords>
For a Cloudbeds property, click <Ui>Connect App</Ui> on MAYA in that property's Cloudbeds Marketplace. On MAYA's page, click <Ui>Sign in</Ui>, use your existing account and pay for the property on its own payment screen. You land on your dashboard, where the <Ui>Property</Ui> dropdown switches between properties, each with its own subscription, rules and team. For ThinkReservations or Mews, email us.
</InPlainWords>

## Add a Cloudbeds property

<Steps>
<Step title="Connect from that property's Cloudbeds account">
Signed in to Cloudbeds for the second property, open <Ui>Marketplace</Ui>, find MAYA and click <Ui>Connect App</Ui>. Approve what Cloudbeds asks.
</Step>
<Step title="Sign in to your existing MAYA account">
Cloudbeds sends you to MAYA's sign-in page, which opens on "Create your MAYA account" with the line "Your Cloudbeds property is connected." Instead, click <Ui>Sign in</Ui> under the form and sign in as usual. The property attaches to your account with you as its Hotel Admin. The hand-over link lasts 24 hours.
</Step>
<Step title="Pay for it">
The payment screen for that property appears, headed with its name and "is connected", with the 7-day free trial. It is its own subscription at its own bracket. If your first property also came from the Cloudbeds Marketplace, Stripe's page offers the card you paid for it with. Otherwise you type your card in again.
</Step>
<Step title="Switch to it">
After payment you land on your dashboard. Look at the <Ui>Property</Ui> dropdown to the right of the tabs. If it shows your first property, pick the new one. The dashboard, the review and the setup questions all work on the property the dropdown shows.
</Step>
<Step title="Review and go live">
MAYA starts reading the new property's history before you pay and carries on in the background. When there is something to check, a blue banner under the tabs ends with the link <Ui>Review →</Ui>. The banner is checked only when the page loads, so reload after switching if it has not appeared. The review is also always at <AppLink to="review">maya-rms.com/onboarding/review</AppLink>. The property starts in simulation like the first, and a General Manager or Hotel Admin takes it live from the review.

The button for that, <Ui>Turn them on for real</Ui>, is there only if MAYA built starter rules for the property (about 60 days of history). It stays after anyone runs <Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui> on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab for that property. Without the button, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Go%20live) with the property's name and we switch it live.
</Step>
</Steps>

If you chose <Ui>Hold My Hand</Ui> or <Ui>Let Me Drive</Ui> for your first property, that choice carries over. Your choice is saved with your login, so you are asked once, not again for each property. The five setup questions are not asked for the new property either. To answer them, pick it in the <Ui>Property</Ui> dropdown and open <AppLink to="questions">maya-rms.com/onboarding/questions</AppLink>. If your login has never made the choice (you joined your first property by invitation, say), it appears after payment.

If one Cloudbeds login covers several properties, one <Ui>Connect App</Ui> connects them all. They line up for payment one at a time, headed "Property 2 of 3", with a <Ui>Not now</Ui> link to set one aside. Set-aside properties wait under "Properties not set up yet" on the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page with a <Ui>Set up</Ui> button.

## What is shared and what is not

- **Shared:** your login. One email and password reach every property you belong to. Your role can differ per property. The <Ui>Hold My Hand</Ui> or <Ui>Let Me Drive</Ui> choice is made once per login too.
- **Per property:** the subscription and its bracket (brackets are never pooled), the trial, the code, the rules, floors and ceilings, room type answers, manual prices, simulation or live state, and the team. Invite a colleague from each property's <Ui>Team</Ui> page.
- **Switching:** the <Ui>Property</Ui> dropdown to the right of the tabs lists your active properties by name. The dropdown opens on the property you used last in that browser. After you sign out, it opens on the first property in alphabetical order.

<Callout kind="good-to-know">
Rules are not copied between properties. Each property gets its own copy of the five starter rules, made only if that property has about 60 days of history. Any rule you build belongs to the property you built it on.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="not-yet">
No group view across properties, no shared rules, no one-click invite to every property, and no pooled billing. Each property is billed at its own room count.
</Callout>
