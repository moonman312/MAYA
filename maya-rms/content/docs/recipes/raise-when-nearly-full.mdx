<InPlainWords>
On the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab, click <Ui>+ Add a rule</Ui>, leave <Ui>Metric</Ui> on <Ui>Sellable occupancy (%)</Ui>, set <Ui>Greater than</Ui> 85, choose <Ui>Increase the rate</Ui> by 10%, keep every room type ticked, click <Ui>Add Rule</Ui> and then <Ui>Apply price adjustments</Ui>. From the next run, any night in the 396-night window with more than 85% of its sellable rooms booked is raised 10%. When the night drops back to 85% or under, the raise comes off.
</InPlainWords>

## Build the rule

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the builder">
On the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab, click <Ui>+ Add a rule</Ui>. In <Ui>Rule name</Ui>, type Nearly full.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the condition">
The first condition row is already <Ui>Sellable occupancy (%)</Ui>. Leave <Ui>Compare</Ui> on <Ui>Greater than</Ui> and change <Ui>Threshold</Ui> from 80 to 85.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the change">
Under <Ui>Rate adjustment</Ui>, choose <Ui>Increase the rate</Ui> in <Ui>Direction (required)</Ui>. Type 10 in <Ui>Adjust by percent (%)</Ui>. For a fixed amount instead, leave the percent empty and type it in <Ui>Adjust by fixed amount ($)</Ui>. A rule has one amount, so the box you leave empty greys out.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the room types and add it">
Under <Ui>Apply to room types</Ui> every room type that counts as a room is ticked. Leave them, and click <Ui>Add Rule</Ui>. A window shows the days the rule will change: click <Ui>Apply price adjustments</Ui>, or <Ui>Skip price adjustments</Ui> to leave those days' prices as they are. Read [Turning a rule on, Apply or Skip](/docs/rules/turning-a-rule-on). The rule appears in the list switched <Ui>On</Ui>.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What you will see

The row reads "Occupancy above 85%" under <Ui>Conditions</Ui> and "+10%" under <Ui>Price Change</Ui>. When a night crosses the mark, the change log shows a line such as "Standard · stay 2026-11-14: $150.00 up to $165.00 (+10%)" with the sentences "Nearly full" raised this night 10%, from $150.00 to $165.00. It was 90% full, past the 85% mark you set.

This is a standard rule: it switches on when the condition starts holding, holds one raise while it holds, and switches off when it stops. With its undo box ticked, as it is on every new rule, a cancellation that takes the night to 85% or under takes the raise off on the next run. Untick it and falling occupancy never takes the raise off. It never stacks on itself.

A sold-out night keeps the raise and is still priced like any other night. Once you are live on Cloudbeds or ThinkReservations, a room freed by a cancellation goes back on sale at the night's current price, raised as long as the night stays above 85%. On Mews it sells at the rate you set in Mews, because MAYA sends no prices there yet. A booking speed cut rule can still cut a sold-out night, because a full night takes no new bookings. Read [A price looks wrong](/docs/wrong/a-price-looks-wrong#we-are-sold-out-tonight-why-is-the-price-still-changing).

<Callout kind="example">
The Harbour Inn has 20 rooms that count. On Saturday, November 14, 2026, 18 are booked: 90%, so the rule fires and Standard goes from $150 to $165. A cancellation leaves 17 booked: 85% exactly, which is not more than 85, so the raise comes off and Standard returns to $150.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="good-to-know">
Occupancy is measured over the rule's own room types, and only over rooms that count as rooms, with rooms out of service taken off first. A rule on Suite alone measures Suite: 2 rooms, so 2 booked is 100% and 1 booked is 50%. The threshold cannot be set above 100.
</Callout>
