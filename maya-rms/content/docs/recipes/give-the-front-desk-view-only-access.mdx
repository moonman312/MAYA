<InPlainWords>
Click <Ui>Team</Ui> at the top right of the dashboard. Under <Ui>Add someone</Ui>, enter their email, set <Ui>What they can do</Ui> to <Ui>Viewer</Ui> and click <Ui>Send invitation</Ui>. They get an email with a single-use link to set a password. A Viewer sees everything on the property and can change nothing.
</InPlainWords>

## Invite them

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Team page">
Click <Ui>Team</Ui> in the dashboard header. You need General Manager or Hotel Admin access. The counter at the top right reads how many seats are used, for example "1 of 2 seats used · 20 rooms".
</Step>
<Step title="Fill in Add someone">
In <Ui>Their email</Ui>, type their work address. In <Ui>What they can do</Ui>, choose <Ui>Viewer</Ui>. The line under the form reads the role's description: "Can look, cannot change anything."
</Step>
<Step title="Send it">
Click <Ui>Send invitation</Ui>. The page reads "Invitation sent to name@yourhotel.com. They'll get an email with a link to join." They are on the property from that moment, before they open the email. Their address appears straight away under <Ui>On this property</Ui> as a Viewer, with a role dropdown and <Ui>Remove</Ui>, like everyone else. The email's link is only how they set their password.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What they receive

An email with the subject "You have been invited to The Harbour Inn on MAYA" and a button <Ui>Accept the invite</Ui>. The link is single-use and expires. It opens a page headed "Set your password" with an optional full name, a password of at least 8 characters, the Terms checkbox and <Ui>Set password & continue</Ui>. Then they are on the dashboard.

If the address already has a MAYA account, from another property say, no email goes out: they are added to the property at once and see it in the <Ui>Property</Ui> dropdown the next time they open MAYA. The page still says "Invitation sent".

## What a Viewer sees

All five tabs, the prices on the calendar, every change log entry including <Ui>How did we know?</Ui>, the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab, and any banner. They cannot type a price, add, switch or delete a rule, tick <Ui>Counts as a room</Ui>, set a date aside or answer the amber banner. The <Ui>Billing</Ui> and <Ui>Team</Ui> pages show them a line saying who handles those. A Viewer who tries to add a rule sees an error, and the rule switches and <Ui>Delete</Ui> buttons do nothing for them.

<Callout kind="good-to-know">
Seats follow your billed room count: 2 seats for 1 to 20 rooms, 4 for 21 to 40, 5 for 41 to 60, 6 for 61 to 80 and 8 for 81 to 500. Everyone under <Ui>On this property</Ui> uses a seat, including someone who has not set a password yet. When every seat is used the form is replaced by a note: remove someone, or raise your room count on the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page. The Terms ask for one login per person, so a front desk of three people needs three seats.
</Callout>

## Change or remove them later

Under <Ui>On this property</Ui>, each member has a role dropdown, which saves the moment you change it, and a <Ui>Remove</Ui> button. That includes someone who has not opened the email yet, so <Ui>Remove</Ui> is also how you take an invitation back.

There is no resend button, and inviting the same address again sends no new email, because they are already a member: the page answers "That person is already on this property." If their link expired or the email went astray, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=New%20invitation%20link) with their address and we send a new link.

<Callout kind="not-yet">
Access is per property. To let the same person see two properties, invite them from each property's <Ui>Team</Ui> page. There is no role between Viewer and Revenue Manager, so someone who should type prices but not build rules needs Revenue Manager, which allows both.
</Callout>
