<InPlainWords>
When the connection breaks, an amber panel headed "MAYA has lost its connection to Cloudbeds" appears with a <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> button. A General Manager or Hotel Admin clicks it, signs in at Cloudbeds or ThinkReservations, and lands back on the MAYA dashboard with the panel gone. Nothing about your rules, history or settings changes. Mews connects with keys we enter, so email us.
</InPlainWords>

## Reconnect

<Steps>
<Step title="Find the panel">
Open the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab. When the connection is broken (the <Ui>Connection</Ui> card reads <Ui>Error</Ui> or <Ui>Disconnected</Ui>), an amber panel sits above the cards: "MAYA has lost its connection to Cloudbeds" and "Your prices aren't updating while this is down." The same panel appears as a banner at the top of the dashboard, whichever tab is open. When the card reads <Ui>Degraded</Ui>, MAYA is still reading and sending, and you see one calm line instead: "Your Cloudbeds connection needs a refresh soon. Prices are still updating." If it is still there in an hour, click the quiet <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> link at the end of that line. It does no harm.
</Step>
<Step title="Click Reconnect">
Click <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui> (or <Ui>Reconnect Think Reservations</Ui>). You are sent to your system's own sign-in page. Sign in and approve. The link is good for 15 minutes.
</Step>
<Step title="You are back">
You land on the MAYA dashboard. There is no confirmation message: the panel is gone and the <Ui>Connection</Ui> card reads <Ui>Connected</Ui>. Bookings are read on the next cycle, and any prices that were held back go out with it.
</Step>
</Steps>

Reconnecting needs General Manager or Hotel Admin access. On Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations, anyone else sees the panel with "Ask this property's General Manager or Hotel Admin to reconnect it" and no button.

<PmsTabs>
<PmsTab pms="cloudbeds">
Another way: in Cloudbeds, open <Ui>Marketplace</Ui> and click <Ui>Connect App</Ui> on MAYA again. Cloudbeds sends you to MAYA's sign-in page with the line "Your Cloudbeds connection is active again. Sign in to pick up where you left off." This is also the way back if you uninstalled the app by accident. If someone removed MAYA's permission to update rates rather than uninstalling it, restore the permission in Cloudbeds first, then reconnect.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="thinkreservations">
The button reads <Ui>Reconnect Think Reservations</Ui> and sends you to ThinkReservations' sign-in. ThinkReservations does not tell MAYA when access is removed. The next read fails, and MAYA tries again with a growing wait of up to an hour. The panel appears once the connection has stopped working. Until then the tab can still read <Ui>Connected</Ui>.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="mews">
The panel reads "Mews needs its keys re-entered" with no button, for every role, because Mews connects with keys we enter rather than a login. It says "Ask us to re-enter the Mews keys: email info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com.", and the address opens an email to us. [Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Mews%20keys) and we arrange new keys with you.
</PmsTab>
</PmsTabs>

## While it is down

Nothing is read, priced or sent. The rates already in your system stay as they are. Your rules, history and settings are untouched. The <Ui>Change Log</Ui> tab may show an item headed "Prices not reaching Cloudbeds" with the action "Reconnect Cloudbeds on the PMS tab." That item is the other route to the panel if you have not opened the PMS tab.

<Callout kind="good-to-know">
A property that came from the Cloudbeds Marketplace, never paid and sat unused for 180 days can have its booking history removed. Its panel reads "Your rules and settings are still here, and your booking history comes back once you reconnect." Reconnecting starts a fresh import, and pricing resumes once it completes.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="not-yet">
The banner above the calendar depends on the PMS tab having been opened in the session, so open that tab if you suspect a problem. There is no email or text when a connection drops, and no manual "sync now" button.
</Callout>
