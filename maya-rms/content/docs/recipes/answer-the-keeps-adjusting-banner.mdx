<InPlainWords>
Once three of a booking speed or pickup rule's changes are on the same night, an amber banner at the top of every tab asks what to do, and the rule keeps adjusting until you answer. <Ui>Keep adjusting</Ui> lets it carry on and stops the question for that night. <Ui>Stop for this night</Ui> means no more changes there, while what it already did stays. A stopped night shows under the rule on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab, where <Ui>Let it run again</Ui> puts the rule back on it.
</InPlainWords>

## Read the banner

The banner is headed with the rule and the count, for example "Hot-week surge" has 3 raises on Sat, Nov 14 2026., and the line under it: It keeps raising these nights until you stop it. For several nights it reads "Hot-week surge" has 3 raises on each of 4 nights. The count is the rule's changes still on the price, so one that came off, for cancellations or because you typed a price, is not counted.

Each night has a card: the date, how many changes are on which room types ("3 raises on Standard and 1 raise on Deluxe"), what the rule read at its latest change (Since the raise before its latest one, by it or a stronger rule, 9 bookings came in. A night like this usually gets about 4 in a whole week.) and where the price can go: If it keeps raising, the price can climb to your $300.00 ceiling for Standard. If the limit is still MAYA's default, the card says so and offers <Ui>Ask MAYA for a ceiling</Ui> or <Ui>Ask MAYA for a floor</Ui>, which takes you to the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab.

While your property is simulating the wording is "would have 3 raises" and "would climb", because nothing has been sent.

## Answer

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose for one night or for all of them">
With several nights, two buttons come first: <Ui>Keep adjusting on all 4 nights</Ui> and <Ui>Stop on all 4 nights</Ui>. They count and answer every night waiting. Under them the nights are listed. Click <Ui>Answer night by night</Ui> to open a card per night, each with <Ui>Keep adjusting</Ui> and <Ui>Stop for this night</Ui>. The cards show the nearest 30 nights; with more, a line under them says how many more there are ("And 358 more nights after these."), and each one you answer brings up the next.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep adjusting">
The rule carries on as it is, and MAYA stops asking about that night. Other rules are unaffected.
</Step>
<Step title="Stop for this night">
The rule makes no more changes on that night. What it already changed there stays, unless its undo box is ticked and cancellations mean the rule is no longer true. Your other rules keep working on the night.
</Step>
</Steps>

Answering needs Revenue Manager access or higher. Anyone else sees the banner with "Only a Revenue Manager or above can answer this." The <Ui>?</Ui> beside the buttons repeats what each answer does.

Your answer is written to the change log under <Ui>Your answer</Ui>: Priya stopped "Hot-week surge" on Sat, Nov 14 2026. What it already changed stays, unless cancellations mean the rule is no longer true. For a rule with its undo box unticked it ends "What it already changed stays."

## Let it run again

A rule you stopped on nights still to come shows an amber chip under its switch on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab: "Stopped on 4 nights", with a <Ui>?</Ui> that names them and a <Ui>Let it run again</Ui> link. One click clears the stop on every stopped night of that rule. The rule can adjust them again from the next run, starting from the price it left, and MAYA asks again after three more changes on the night.

<Callout kind="good-to-know">
The question is per rule and per night. It can arrive a few minutes after the third adjustment. A price you type on the night, or deleting the rule, closes the question without an answer. Deleting and re-adding a rule starts its count at zero.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="not-yet">
You cannot change the three-times threshold, and you cannot stop a rule on a night before it has asked. A price you type on the <Ui>Calendar</Ui> tab takes the rule's changes off that room type and night, and starts the rule's wait again. Once the wait is over, the rule can adjust your price again, and the count of three restarts from your price. To keep your number, switch the rule <Ui>Off</Ui> on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab, which stops it on every night, as [Hold a price for an event](/docs/recipes/hold-a-price-for-an-event) explains.
</Callout>
