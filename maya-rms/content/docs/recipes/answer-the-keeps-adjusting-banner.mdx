<InPlainWords>
Once a booking speed or pickup rule has adjusted the same night three times, an amber banner at the top of every tab asks what to do, and the rule keeps adjusting until you answer. <Ui>Keep adjusting</Ui> lets it carry on and stops the question for that night. <Ui>Stop for this night</Ui> means no more changes there, while what it already did stays. A stopped night shows under the rule on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab, where <Ui>Let it run again</Ui> puts the rule back on it.
</InPlainWords>

## Read the banner

The banner is headed with the rule and the count, for example "Hot-week surge" has raised Sat, Nov 14 2026 3 times, and the line under it: It keeps raising these nights until you stop it. For several nights it reads "Hot-week surge" has raised 4 nights, 3 times each.

Each night has a card: the date, how many times on which room types ("3 times on Standard, once on Deluxe"), what the rule read (In the 7 days it measured, 9 bookings came in. A night like this usually has about 4 by then.) and where the price can go: If it keeps raising, the price can climb to your $300.00 ceiling for Standard. If the limit is still MAYA's default, the card says so and offers <Ui>Ask MAYA for a ceiling</Ui> or <Ui>Ask MAYA for a floor</Ui>, which takes you to the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab.

While your property is simulating the wording is "would have raised" and "would climb", because nothing has been sent.

## Answer

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose for one night or for all of them">
With several nights, two buttons come first: <Ui>Keep adjusting on all 4 nights</Ui> and <Ui>Stop on all 4 nights</Ui>. Under them the nights are listed. Click <Ui>Answer night by night</Ui> to open a card per night, each with <Ui>Keep adjusting</Ui> and <Ui>Stop for this night</Ui>.
</Step>
<Step title="Keep adjusting">
The rule carries on as it is, and MAYA stops asking about that night. Other rules are unaffected.
</Step>
<Step title="Stop for this night">
The rule makes no more changes on that night. For a rule that raises, the raises it already made stay, unless enough of the bookings behind them cancel. For a rule that cuts, what it cut stays. Your other rules keep working on the night.
</Step>
</Steps>

Answering needs Revenue Manager access or higher. Anyone else sees the banner with "Only a Revenue Manager or above can answer this." The <Ui>?</Ui> beside the buttons repeats what each answer does.

Your answer is written to the change log under <Ui>Your answer</Ui>: Priya stopped "Hot-week surge" on Sat, Nov 14 2026. The raises it already made stay, unless enough of the bookings behind them cancel.

## Let it run again

A rule you stopped on nights still to come shows an amber chip under its switch on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab: "Stopped on 4 nights", with a <Ui>?</Ui> that names them and a <Ui>Let it run again</Ui> link. One click clears the stop on every stopped night of that rule. The rule can adjust them again from the next run, starting from the price it left, and MAYA asks again after three more adjustments.

<Callout kind="good-to-know">
The question is per rule and per night. It can arrive a few minutes after the third adjustment. A price you type on the night, or deleting the rule, closes the question without an answer. Deleting and re-adding a rule starts its count at zero.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="not-yet">
You cannot change the three-times threshold, and you cannot stop a rule on a night before it has asked. A price you type on the <Ui>Calendar</Ui> tab takes the rule's changes off that room type and night, and starts the rule's wait again. Once the wait is over, the rule can adjust your price again, and the count of three restarts from your price. To keep your number, switch the rule <Ui>Off</Ui> on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab, which stops it on every night, as [Hold a price for an event](/docs/recipes/hold-a-price-for-an-event) explains.
</Callout>
