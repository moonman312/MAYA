<InPlainWords>
Open the <Ui>Rate Simulator</Ui> tab, set up a made-up night, click <Ui>+ Build a test rule</Ui> and fill it in. The <Ui>What would happen</Ui> table shows what each room type would become. Nothing is saved and no price reaches your property system until you click <Ui>Save This Rule</Ui>, and even then the rule arrives on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab switched <Ui>Off</Ui>. Turn it <Ui>On</Ui> when you are ready.
</InPlainWords>

## Try the rule

<Steps>
<Step title="Set up the night">
On the <Ui>Rate Simulator</Ui> tab, under <Ui>The night</Ui>, pick a <Ui>Stay date</Ui> (it starts 30 days ahead) and a <Ui>Booking speed</Ui> level. Leave it on <Ui>Not enough history</Ui> and booking speed rules stay quiet, which is what happens on a night MAYA cannot measure.
</Step>
<Step title="Set up the rooms">
Under <Ui>Your rooms</Ui>, each room type that counts as a room has a <Ui>Base price</Ui> (starting at the nearest price MAYA has published), an <Ui>Occupancy</Ui> percentage (starting at 70%) and a <Ui>Picked up</Ui> count. Its floor and ceiling show under <Ui>Guardrails</Ui>. Change the numbers to the night you want to test.
</Step>
<Step title="Build the test rule">
Under <Ui>Rules in play</Ui>, click <Ui>+ Build a test rule</Ui>. Give it a <Ui>Rule name</Ui>, one to four conditions, a direction under <Ui>Then</Ui> (<Ui>raise the rate</Ui> or <Ui>lower the rate</Ui>), an amount under <Ui>By</Ui>, a <Ui>Unit</Ui> (<Ui>percent</Ui> or <Ui>dollars</Ui>) and the <Ui>Room types</Ui>.
</Step>
<Step title="Read the result">
The <Ui>What would happen</Ui> table shows each room type's <Ui>Base</Ui>, what it <Ui>Becomes</Ui>, the <Ui>Change</Ui>, and <Ui>Because</Ui>, which names the rules that fired. A price held at a limit is tagged "held at your floor" or "held at your ceiling". The <Ui>Didn't fire</Ui> list says why a rule stayed quiet, for example "Conditions not met" with what it saw.
</Step>
<Step title="Save it, switched off">
Click <Ui>Save This Rule</Ui>. A green line reads "Rule Added to Rules Tab and Initialized as Disabled". Until you press it, the rule exists only on this screen. On the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab, switch it <Ui>On</Ui> when you are ready.
</Step>
</Steps>

Saving a rule needs Revenue Manager access or higher.

## What the simulator does and does not do

The math is the same a real run uses, so the numbers are what your rules would produce for the night you typed. Your existing rules are all ticked under <Ui>Rules in play</Ui>, switched-off ones included, so you can see what turning one on would do. Untick any to leave it out of the test.

Three things it simplifies, as the note at the foot of the page says:

- A standard rule (occupancy or booking window) fires on the way into its condition and holds, so the table shows where the night settles rather than each step.
- When several booking speed or pickup rules match, a real run lets only one of them act on each night and room type, while the simulator adds them all.
- A booking speed or pickup rule that is still true once its wait is over adjusts the night again, which the simulator shows only once.

It is one run, not where a night ends up over a week.

<Callout kind="good-to-know">
The test-rule form has no <Ui>Measured over</Ui> or <Ui>Then waits</Ui> controls. A booking speed test rule is saved with the defaults: <Ui>Past week</Ui> and a wait of <Ui>1 week</Ui>. To choose others, build the rule on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab instead, where a new rule is <Ui>On</Ui> at once.
</Callout>
