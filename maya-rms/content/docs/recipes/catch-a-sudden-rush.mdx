<InPlainWords>
On the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab, add a rule with <Ui>Metric</Ui> <Ui>Booking speed (recommended)</Ui>, <Ui>Speed</Ui> <Ui>Surging</Ui>, <Ui>Measured over</Ui> <Ui>Past day</Ui>, <Ui>Then waits (advanced)</Ui> <Ui>1 day</Ui>, and <Ui>Increase the rate</Ui> by 25%. A night that received far more bookings today than similar nights had on the same day of their run-up is raised 25%. The next day the rule counts only the bookings made since that raise, and raises again if those still read Surging. This is the starter rule "Sudden-spike catcher", so check your list first.
</InPlainWords>

## Build the rule

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the builder and name it">
On the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab, click <Ui>+ Add a rule</Ui>. In <Ui>Rule name</Ui>, type Sudden rush.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose Surging over the past day">
Change <Ui>Metric</Ui> to <Ui>Booking speed (recommended)</Ui> and pick <Ui>Surging</Ui> in <Ui>Speed</Ui>. Set <Ui>Measured over</Ui> to <Ui>Past day</Ui> and <Ui>Then waits (advanced)</Ui> to <Ui>1 day</Ui>.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the raise, pick the room types, add it">
Under <Ui>Rate adjustment</Ui>, choose <Ui>Increase the rate</Ui> and type 25 in <Ui>Adjust by percent (%)</Ui>. Leave every room type ticked and click <Ui>Add Rule</Ui>.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What Surging takes

<Ui>Past day</Ui> counts the bookings dated today, on your property's own calendar, so a reading soon after midnight has almost nothing in it yet. MAYA compares that count with what similar past nights received on the same single day of their own run-up. Surging is the top of seven levels: the night needs at least 3.5 times the usual count, and at least 4 more bookings than usual, to read as Surging. With an expected count of 5, that is 18 bookings or more in one day. With fewer than 5 similar nights to compare with, the call is capped at one step from Normal and Surging cannot be reached.

Picking <Ui>Surging</Ui> means the rule fires on Surging only. To fire on a broader rush, pick <Ui>Much Faster Than Normal</Ui> (at least twice the usual count) or <Ui>Faster Than Normal</Ui>, which fire on that level and everything above it.

## What happens after it raises

The raise stays on the price, up to the room type's ceiling. For 1 day the rule leaves that night alone. Then it counts only the bookings made since the newest raise still on that night by this rule or a stronger one. If those alone read Surging against what a similar night gets in a whole day, it raises another 25%.

A raise by a weaker rule, or by an occupancy or booking window rule, does not restart that count. One burst of bookings raises a night once, not once per rule. Once three of its raises are on the same night, the amber banner asks whether to keep adjusting or stop.

The rule's undo box, <Ui>Undo the change if cancellations mean this rule is no longer true</Ui>, starts ticked. With it ticked, a raise comes off when bookings it counted cancel and the ones still booked no longer read Surging, unless bookings made since keep the rule true. The change log then reads: Cancellations meant "Sudden rush" was no longer true, so its 25% raise came off. Untick the box and cancellations never take the raise off. Read [When a price change comes off](/docs/rules/when-a-change-comes-off).

<Callout kind="careful">
Set a ceiling first. A rule that raises stops at the room type's ceiling, and MAYA's default of $99,999.99 stops nothing in most currencies. Read [Set or change a floor or ceiling](/docs/recipes/set-a-floor-or-ceiling).
</Callout>

<Callout kind="good-to-know">
The usual five starter rules include "Sudden-spike catcher" (Surging, past day, raise 25%, waits 1 day), "Hot-week surge" (Much Faster Than Normal, past week, raise 25%, waits 2 days) and "Warm-date bump" (Faster Than Normal, past month, raise 10%, waits 3 days). Together they step up as the pace climbs. If you answered "Find money I'm leaving on the table" and your nights filled early, they raise 30%, 30% and 15%.
</Callout>
