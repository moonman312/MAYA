<InPlainWords>
In the rule builder, the chips under <Ui>Apply to room types</Ui> are the room types the rule covers. Every type that counts as a room starts ticked. Click a chip to untick it and the rule leaves that room type alone, in what it measures and in what it changes. For a saved rule, click <Ui>Edit</Ui> on its row, change the chips and click <Ui>Save changes</Ui>.
</InPlainWords>

## Untick the room type

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the builder">
On the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab, click <Ui>+ Add a rule</Ui> and fill in the name, condition and rate adjustment as usual.
</Step>
<Step title="Untick the chips you want left out">
Under <Ui>Apply to room types</Ui>, each room type is a chip. A ticked chip is highlighted. Click Suite to untick it. The summary beside the label counts what is left, for example "2 of 4 room types". <Ui>Select all</Ui> ticks every chip and <Ui>Clear</Ui> unticks them all.
</Step>
<Step title="Add the rule">
Click <Ui>Add Rule</Ui>, then <Ui>Apply price adjustments</Ui> in the window that opens. The row's <Ui>Room Types</Ui> column lists the types it covers: "Standard, Deluxe".
</Step>
</Steps>

<OpenInMaya to="rules.new" focus="room-types" words="Open the rule builder at the room types" />

At least one room type must be ticked. Leaving none gives "Select at least one room type."

## What the ticks mean

With the single list, the rule measures and changes the same room types. It measures the ticked types that count as rooms, and it changes every ticked type. So a rule ticked on Suite alone measures Suite's occupancy on its own (The Harbour Inn has 2 Suites, so 1 booked is 50%) and moves Suite's price only.

A chip tagged "not a room" is a type you have unticked as a room on the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab, such as a Meeting Room. It starts unticked in the builder. Tick it and the rule changes its price, and once you are live that price is sent to your system; leave it out of every rule and MAYA sends nothing for it, apart from a price you type for one of its nights on the calendar, which is sent as you typed it. A rule never measures it: a court or meeting room has no occupancy to read.

If you tick only "not a room" types, the rule has nothing to measure. An occupancy, booking speed or pickup condition then can never be true, so a rule with any of them never fires. Only a rule whose single condition is booking window (the number of days until the stay) still fires, because it counts days, not bookings.

So tick at least one type that counts as a room as well, unless booking window is the rule's only condition. The rule then changes the price of every type you tick, rooms included. Read more in [Take a meeting room out of occupancy](/docs/recipes/take-a-meeting-room-out-of-occupancy).

<Callout kind="good-to-know">
To measure one set of room types and change another, tick <Ui>Change prices on different room types</Ui> under the chips. For example, measure Standard and change only the Meeting Room. Read [Let suites follow your standard rooms](/docs/recipes/let-suites-follow-standard-rooms).
</Callout>

## An existing rule

Click <Ui>Edit</Ui> on the rule's row. The rule builder opens with its chips as saved. Change them and click <Ui>Save changes</Ui>. On a rule that is on, a window shows the days that change: with <Ui>Apply price adjustments</Ui>, the room types you unticked lose the rule's changes from the next run, and the ones you ticked get them where the rule holds. With <Ui>Skip price adjustments</Ui>, the prices on those days stay as they are until the rule stops being true on a day and then becomes true again, and every other night works as with Apply. The starter rules MAYA built from your history cover every room type that counts as a room; to keep one of them off your suites, edit it the same way. Read [Change a rule](/docs/recipes/change-a-rule).
