<InPlainWords>
Open the <Ui>Change Log</Ui> tab. Each pricing run lists the nights whose price moved, one bold line per room type and night, followed by a sentence that names the rule and the reason. For a booking speed change, click <Ui>Show the numbers</Ui> under the entry to see the counts behind it. The change log covers your 10 most recent runs that changed a price. For an older change, check the night on the <Ui>Calendar</Ui> tab first: a badge means the price was typed in MAYA or changed in your system, and <Ui>Current price</Ui> shows where your rules have taken your rate. For the exact reason, email us the night and room type within 90 days.
</InPlainWords>

## Open the change log

<Steps>
<Step title="Go to the Change Log tab">
It opens on changes only. Each entry is headed <Ui>Pricing run</Ui> with the time of the run in your browser's local time, such as "Sat, Nov 14, 2026, 3:05 PM PST", and how long ago it was, such as "(12 min ago)". Ongoing problems, headed <Ui>Prices not reaching Cloudbeds</Ui> (or your system's name), sit above every run.
</Step>
<Step title="Find the night">
Inside a run, each change starts with a bold line in the shape "Standard · stay 2026-11-14: $150.00 up to $165.00 (+10%)". Look for the stay date. Down moves read "down to" with a minus percentage. In a run made while your property was simulating, the bold line reads "Simulation: the price for Sat Nov 14, Standard would have gone from $150.00 to $165.00." instead, because nothing was sent.
</Step>
<Step title="Read the sentence under it">
The first sentence names the rule and the move: "Busy nights" raised this night 10%, from $150.00 to $165.00. The next sentence gives the reason in your own numbers: It was 85% full, past the 80% mark you set.
</Step>
<Step title="Show the numbers for a booking speed change">
When a booking speed rule acted, a <Ui>Show the numbers</Ui> link sits under the entry. It shows how many bookings the night received, how many a night like it usually has by that point, and the level that reads as. <Ui>Which nights were compared?</Ui> lists which past nights were compared and why, and <Ui>List the nights</Ui> lists them one by one.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the sentences mean

| You read | It means |
|---|---|
| "Busy nights" raised this night 10%, from $150.00 to $165.00. | That rule acted on this night and room type. |
| Then "Hot-week surge" raised it 25%, from $165.00 to $206.25. | A second rule acted on top. Percentages compound, so the 25% is applied to $165.00. When the same rule acts again after its wait, the sentence says "another". |
| It was 85% full, past the 80% mark you set. | The condition that made an occupancy rule true. |
| Bookings came in faster than normal this past week: 9, against the 5 a night like this usually has by now. | The count behind a booking speed rule. It counts bookings, not rooms. Once the rule, or a stronger booking speed or pickup rule, has raised the night, it counts only the bookings made since the newest of those raises still on the night, and the sentence reads "in the 3 days since this rule or a stronger one's latest raise still on this night". Those bookings alone have to beat what a night like this usually gets in the rule's whole window. A raise from an occupancy or booking window rule does not change what it counts. A rule that cuts counts full days only, ending yesterday, and the sentence says "up to yesterday" or "after this rule or a stronger one's latest cut still on this night". |
| That would have gone past your $300.00 ceiling for Standard, so it stopped there. | The rules asked for more, and the ceiling held the price. |
| Cancellations meant "Warm-date bump" was no longer true, so its 10% raise came off. | Guests cancelled and the rule was no longer true for the night, so its change came off. The next sentence gives the numbers. |
| That took this night from $165.00 to $150.00. | The run put the night back at its starting point. The sentences before it say what came off. |
| Sam set the base rate to $180.00. | A person typed a price on the Calendar tab. No rule is named because no rule acted. |
| The base rate was changed in Cloudbeds to $180.00. | The rate was changed in your property system on a night MAYA had already sent to, and MAYA kept that rate. |
| Fri, Nov 13, Standard: changed in Cloudbeds to $175.00. MAYA sent its price, $165.00, again. | An item of its own, not under a run: your setting is <Ui>MAYA's price wins</Ui>, so MAYA sent its own price over a rate changed in your property system. Before the send goes it reads "MAYA's price, $165.00, is waiting to be sent again." |
| Simulation: the price for Fri Nov 13, Standard would have gone from $150.00 to $165.00. | A run made while your property was simulating, tagged <Ui>Simulation</Ui>. Nothing was sent, and the sentences under it say what would have happened. |
| "Pricing rule" raised this night 10%. | The rule that acted has since been deleted, so its name is gone. |

## How far back the log goes

The tab covers the 10 most recent runs that changed a price, however long ago they ran. On a busy day that may be the last hour, on a quiet week several days. A change older than the tenth is no longer listed. The runs in between that changed nothing are counted into one quiet line per stretch, shown under <Ui>Show All Cycles</Ui>.

If no run has changed a price yet, no runs are listed. Within a run you see the 40 biggest moves. When a run had more, a line under them counts the rest, such as "And 23 more changes in this run."

## If nothing is listed for the night

When the price moved before the oldest run on screen, its run has dropped off the list (see above). You can still narrow it down yourself. Open the night on the <Ui>Calendar</Ui> tab and check three things:

- **A badge on the room type's card.** "Manual · $180.00" means someone typed that price in MAYA. "Changed in Cloudbeds · $180.00" (or your system's name) means the rate was changed in your property system and MAYA kept it. Either way, the starting price did not come from your rules. Hover the badge for the time it was set (or, for a rate changed in your system, when MAYA read it).
- **<Ui>Current price</Ui> against your own rate.** Current price is your rate for that night after your rules and your floor and ceiling. A gap between the two is what they added or took off. (MAYA reads no Mews rates, so on Mews the starting point is always a price you typed.)
- **Which of your rules could apply.** On the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab, hold each rule that is <Ui>On</Ui> up against the night: how full it is, how many days away it is, and whether the rule changes that room type.

For the exact reason, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Why%20did%20a%20price%20change) the night and room type within 90 days. The detail behind every change is kept for 90 days, and we can read it for you.

If the price did not move, most runs change nothing. Click <Ui>Show All Cycles</Ui> to include the quiet lines, such as "Prices checked 47 times from Sep 24, 10:05 AM to 1:55 PM, nothing needed to change". That is normal: about every five minutes MAYA reads your bookings and runs your rules on the nights that changed, and a price only moves when a rule's condition changes.

A night 396 or more nights away, past tonight and the next 395, is not priced yet, so it has no entries. A night before today is never priced.
