<InPlainWords>
Open the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab, scroll to <Ui>Room types</Ui>, and untick <Ui>Counts as a room</Ui> on the meeting room's row. It saves at once. From then on that type is left out of sellable occupancy, the calendar's room counts, booking speed and the room count you are billed for. It can still be priced if a rule changes it.
</InPlainWords>

## Untick the room type

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the room types">
Go to the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab. Under the connection cards is a section headed <Ui>Room types</Ui>, one row per active room type with its unit count and a <Ui>Counts as a room</Ui> checkbox.
</Step>
<Step title="Untick Counts as a room">
On the Meeting Room's row, untick the box. It saves straight away, with no confirmation. The row dims and gains a "not a room" tag. If it did not save, the box snaps back and a short error shows.

If the box is already unticked with a "needs your answer" tag, nobody has answered for that type yet, and it still counts in occupancy. Tick the box, then untick it: the tag changes to "not a room".
</Step>
</Steps>

Changing it needs Revenue Manager access or higher. The change re-prices every night of the window from the next cycle, nearest nights first, so occupancy rules on the coming nights read the new figure within a few minutes, and the furthest nights over the next few cycles.

## What changes

- **Sellable occupancy.** Only ticked types, and types still tagged "needs your answer", are divided by. The Harbour Inn has 20 rooms plus a Meeting Room. With it ticked, 18 booked rooms read as 18 of 21 (86%). Unticked, they read as 18 of 20 (90%).
- **The calendar.** The day card's "18/20 rooms" and its percentage leave out types tagged "not a room", and so do its ADR and RevPAR when your calendar shows them. Revenue on the card still sums every type.
- **Booking speed.** Bookings for an unticked type are not counted, and it is never measured by a rule.
- **Sending.** MAYA sends no price to your property system for an unticked type unless one of your rules ticks it under <Ui>Change</Ui>, or you type a price for one of its nights on the calendar (that night is sent as you typed it). Your own rate for the Meeting Room stays as it is there. The floor and ceiling answers from setup are not written onto it either.
- **Rules.** In the rule builder the type is tagged "not a room" and starts unticked. A rule that ticks it can still change its price, and that price is sent. A rule that measures only unticked types (the Meeting Room alone, say) never fires on occupancy, pickup or booking speed. A booking window condition counts days, not bookings. So a rule whose only condition is booking window still fires and changes the room types it is set to change.
- **Your bill.** The room count MAYA measures from your system leaves out unticked types. Lowering the count never lowers your bill on its own: set it on the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page. Read [Change the room count you pay for](/docs/recipes/change-the-room-count-you-pay-for).

<Callout kind="good-to-know">
During setup MAYA unticks types whose names are plainly not bedrooms (parking, meeting, conference, spa treatment and the like) and asks about softer cases on the review page with a card headed "Is "Pool Cabana" actually a room?". A name with a bedroom word beside such a word ("King Room with Parking"), or a type that sells at a nightly rate like your rooms', is asked about rather than unticked. Your answer is final either way, and you can change it here any time.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="not-yet">
Room types cannot be renamed, merged or hidden in MAYA, and their unit counts come from your property system. On ThinkReservations they are counted from your list of rooms, so a room marked there as not a room, or as not included in occupancy, is left out of its room type's count. Read [ThinkReservations](/docs/connect/thinkreservations#how-many-rooms-each-room-type-has).
</Callout>
