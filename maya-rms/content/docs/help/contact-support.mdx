## One address for everything

Support is by email, at info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com. The same address takes billing questions, requests about your data and anything under the Terms.

When a MAYA screen says "email us", "get in touch" or "send us a message", this is the address it means. Some screens show the address or open an email to it. Where they don't, this is the address to use, so keep this page handy.

Write from the email address you sign in to MAYA with. We match it against your account, so an email from another address needs a second step. If you have more than one property, name the one you mean.

## How quickly we answer

We aim to answer within one business day. That is an aim, not a guarantee. The Terms of Service (section 3.7) say support is given by email on a reasonable-efforts basis, with no set response time unless we have agreed one with you in a signed document.

Business days are Monday to Friday. We are based in Austin, Texas, so they run on US Central time, and weekends and US public holidays do not count towards the aim. An email sent on a Friday evening or a Saturday is usually answered the following Monday. There is no out-of-hours cover, and answers are in English, the language of MAYA and these docs. Your rules keep running the whole time: MAYA does not wait for us.

## What to put in your email

Most of what we need is already on your screen. The more of it you copy into your email, the fewer questions we send back.

| Your question is about | Include |
|---|---|
| Anything | Your property's name and the property system it uses |
| A price | The night, the room type, the price you see and the change log entry: the bold line and the sentences under it |
| Prices not reaching your system | The sentence at the top of the <Ui>Change Log</Ui>, and your system's own words (press <Ui>Show tries</Ui> and hover a line) |
| A booking speed change | What the <Ui>How did we know?</Ui> button opens, or a screenshot of it |
| A screen that misbehaves | The tab, what you clicked, what you saw (the exact words of any message), a screenshot, and your browser's name and version |
| A charge | The invoice number and the date of the charge |

Keep these out of every email:

- **Passwords**, yours or your property system's. We never need them.
- **Mews keys.** When we connect Mews or replace its keys, we arrange a secure way to share them.
- **Card numbers.** Stripe holds your card, and we never ask for it.
- **Guest names or any guest details.** MAYA does not store them, so a screenshot from MAYA is fine. Crop guest names out of a screenshot of your property system.

<Example title="An email about a price at The Harbour Inn">
Priya, the Revenue Manager at The Harbour Inn, has a question about a Deluxe price. She writes from the address she signs in with, subject "Deluxe price on November 13":

Property: The Harbour Inn, on Cloudbeds. Night: Friday, November 13, 2026. Room type: Deluxe.

Change log, run of "Fri, Oct 2, 2026, 3:05 PM PDT": "Deluxe · stay 2026-11-13: $200.00 up to $220.00 (+10%)", then "Busy nights" raised this night 10%, from $200.00 to $220.00. It was 85% full, past the 80% mark you set.

Question: we meant "Busy nights" to leave Deluxe alone. Did we set it up wrong?

That is everything we need to look at the same entry she is looking at.
</Example>

## Prices stay in your hands

Can we change your prices for you? No. We do not type, raise or cut prices on your behalf. The Terms of Service (section 4.1) make you responsible for every price MAYA publishes, and the change log names who typed each price in MAYA.

What we do instead:

- **Talk it through.** Send us the change log entry and we look at the same night with you, and say which rule acted and why.
- **Stop the sending.** At your request we put a live property back into simulation. Nothing more is sent, and the prices already in your system stay as they are.
- **Change a setting you cannot change on a screen yet**, such as a room count, a time zone or a currency, using the numbers you send us. Whatever we change at your request, we change on your instruction (Terms section 3.3).

The change itself is quicker in your hands anyway. Anyone with the Revenue Manager, General Manager or Hotel Admin role can type a price on the <Ui>Calendar</Ui> tab, and [Setting a price yourself](/docs/watch/setting-a-price-yourself) shows how. On Mews, where MAYA does not send prices yet, you change the rate in Mews itself.

## Is it MAYA or your property system?

MAYA's screens say which system did what. The problem items at the top of the <Ui>Change Log</Ui> name it, for example "Cloudbeds refused", "Cloudbeds isn't responding" or "MAYA doesn't send". <Ui>Show tries</Ui> shows your system's own words when you hover a line.

| What you see | Who can help |
|---|---|
| A refusal that names a setting in your system (a rate rule, a permission, a plan that follows another) | You, in your system. For Cloudbeds, [Cloudbeds Support](https://myfrontdesk.cloudbeds.com/) can see those settings and we cannot. |
| Your system slow or not responding | Wait. MAYA tries again every cycle. If it lasts, check with your system's own support. |
| Trouble signing in to your system, a missing permission or data missing inside it | Your system's own support. For Cloudbeds, that is Cloudbeds Support at myfrontdesk.cloudbeds.com. |
| A price, a rule, occupancy, the calendar, the change log, your bill or "MAYA couldn't tell why" | Us, at info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com |

[When your property system does not accept a price](/docs/live/when-your-pms-does-not-accept-a-price) explains every problem sentence.

## No phone, chat or status page

Support is by email only. There is no phone line, no chat and no public status page.

To check your own connection, open the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab. The <Ui>Connection</Ui> card says whether your system is connected, and <Ui>Last sync</Ui> says when bookings were last read. On Mews, keys that stop working show no warning, so <Ui>Last sync</Ui> is the thing to watch. Problems sending prices sit at the top of the <Ui>Change Log</Ui> while they last. Read [the PMS tab](/docs/watch/the-pms-tab).

On Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations, setup is designed so nobody needs a call: connect, answer five optional questions, review what MAYA found, go live. Mews we connect with you by email. If you would rather have a person walk you through any of it, email us and say so.

## Reporting a bug

[Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Something%20is%20not%20working) with the subject "Something is not working", and include:

1. Your property's name, and the tab or page you were on.
2. What you did, click by click.
3. What you expected, and what happened instead, with the exact words of any message.
4. A screenshot, and your browser's name and version.
5. When it happened, as the time your screen showed.

If the screen showed a message, [Messages you might see, explained](/docs/wrong/messages-explained) may already cover it.

## Asking for a feature

[Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Feature%20request) with the subject "Feature request". Say what you would like MAYA to do and the situation you would use it in. It helps us see how many owners want the same thing.

We do not promise dates. These docs describe only what MAYA does today, so a feature appears here once it is in the app. [What rules cannot do yet](/docs/rules/what-rules-cannot-do-yet) lists what rules cannot do today, with what to do instead.

## Our staff and your property

Our staff can open your property to help, for example to switch it back to simulation at your request or to check a price with you. We never need your password for that, so never send one. [Roles: who can do what](/docs/team/roles) has the details.

## Legal letters and requests about your data

Requests about your data (a copy, a correction or a deletion) go to the same address. The [Privacy Policy](https://www.get-maya.com/privacy) (section 10) lists your rights.

For anything formal under the Terms, email the same address with "Legal Notice" in the subject line, or write to Modern Hospitality Solutions LLC, 1515 E Cesar Chavez St. Suite 100, Austin, TX 78702 (Terms section 18.2).
