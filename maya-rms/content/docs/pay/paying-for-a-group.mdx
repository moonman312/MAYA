<InPlainWords>
One <Ui>Connect App</Ui> in Cloudbeds can hand MAYA every property your Cloudbeds login covers. You pay for them one at a time (the screen says <Ui>Property 2 of 3</Ui>), the same card is offered for each, and each property is its own subscription, with its own billing period, rules and settings. <Ui>Not now</Ui> parks a property until you are ready, and the <Ui>Billing</Ui> page is where you pick it up.
</InPlainWords>

## How a group arrives

<PmsTabs>
<PmsTab pms="cloudbeds">
If your Cloudbeds login covers several properties, one click on <Ui>Connect App</Ui> in the Marketplace connects all of them. You create one MAYA account (or sign in to one you have), and every property attaches to it with you as its Hotel Admin. All of them start in simulation. From maya-rms.com directly it works the other way round: you pay first, then connect one property at a time, and a Cloudbeds login that covers several properties is refused at the connect step, after you have paid. So for a group, start with <Ui>Connect App</Ui> in the Marketplace, before paying anything. If you have already paid on maya-rms.com and the connect step tells you "MAYA signs one property up at a time right now", [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Cloudbeds%20group) rather than starting again from the Marketplace, which would add your properties beside the one you paid for.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="thinkreservations">
There is no group hand-over for ThinkReservations. Each property is connected and paid for on its own, and a ThinkReservations login that covers several properties is refused at the connect step. [Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=ThinkReservations%20group) and we set the rest up with you.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="mews">
Mews properties are connected by us, one at a time, each with its own payment. [Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Connecting%20Mews) for each one.
</PmsTab>
</PmsTabs>

## Property 2 of 3

After you create your account, the payment screen comes up for the first property, headed with its name and "is connected". Above the heading a small line reads <Ui>Property 1 of 3</Ui>. You pay, and the same screen returns for the next property, <Ui>Property 2 of 3</Ui>, and so on. Properties come up oldest first, by when Cloudbeds handed them over.

A property you set aside with <Ui>Not now</Ui> counts as done for the numbering, so after skipping the first the next reads <Ui>Property 2 of 3</Ui>, not <Ui>Property 1 of 2</Ui>.

While the payment screen is up, MAYA reads the booking history of the property on screen. One unpaid import runs at a time, for the property you are looking at, so the next property's history starts loading when you reach it.

## One card, one subscription per property

Every property in the group bills on one billing account in Stripe, so the card taken for the first is offered again for each of the others. You can pick it from a list rather than typing it again. The Terms line above Stripe's pay button says so: your card "may be charged for room-count changes and offered for your other properties".

Each property is still its own subscription:

- **Its own bracket.** Two 15-room properties are two subscriptions at $5.50 a room, not one 30-room property at $5.00. Brackets are never pooled, and there is no group discount beyond what a code grants.
- **Its own invoice.** One invoice per property, each on its own renewal date. There is no combined invoice.
- **Its own billing period.** Each property's payment screen has its own <Ui>Monthly</Ui> and <Ui>Yearly</Ui> buttons, so one property can be monthly and another yearly.
- **Its own trial.** Each Marketplace property gets the 7-day trial at its own checkout.
- **Its own rules, limits, team, settings and simulation state.** Going live on one changes nothing on another.

To switch one property between monthly and yearly later, open that property's <Ui>Billing</Ui> page and click <Ui>Update card, receipts & cancellation</Ui>. In a Cloudbeds Marketplace group, the owner's portal lists every property's subscription, so choose the one for that property. If you cannot tell which is which, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Switch%20one%20property). A General Manager or Hotel Admin of only that property gets Stripe's screen for changing that one property's plan. [Switching between monthly and yearly](/docs/billing/cards-payments-and-invoices#switching-between-monthly-and-yearly) says what a switch charges.

<Example title="Two properties, one owner">
Sam owns The Harbour Inn (20 rooms) and a 15-room guesthouse, both in Cloudbeds. One <Ui>Connect App</Ui> brings both over. Sam pays $110 a month for the inn as <Ui>Property 1 of 2</Ui>, then $82.50 a month for the guesthouse as <Ui>Property 2 of 2</Ui>, choosing the saved card. Two subscriptions, two invoices, two trials, one card.
</Example>

## Different cards or different companies

<PmsTabs>
<PmsTab pms="cloudbeds">
**Different cards: yes, when you pay.** Once you have paid for one property, Stripe's page for the next offers the same card, and you can type a different card there instead. That property is then charged to the card you paid for it with. Both cards are saved on the same billing account. To move one property to a different card after you have paid for it, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Card%20for%20one%20property).

**Different companies: not inside one group.** Every property you pay for through the Cloudbeds Marketplace bills on the same billing account in Stripe, the one that belongs to your MAYA login. That account has one billing email and, if you add them, one billing address and one business tax ID. So each property gets its own invoice, but every invoice is addressed the same way.

**Billing a property separately.** From the screens, a Marketplace property goes on a different billing account only when someone else pays for it from their own MAYA login.

**Before your group is connected**, the other company can connect its property itself. Someone there clicks <Ui>Connect App</Ui> in the Cloudbeds Marketplace with a Cloudbeds login that covers only that property, and creates their own MAYA account. Once they pay, it bills on their account with their card. They can invite you from its <Ui>Team</Ui> page, so it also appears in your <Ui>Property</Ui> selector.

**Once the property is on your MAYA account**, there is no screen to move it. Connecting it again from another login leaves it where it is, and a restart after cancelling uses the same billing account. If you have not paid for it yet, set it aside with <Ui>Not now</Ui> so nothing is charged. Either way, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Separate%20billing) and tell us who should pay for it.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="thinkreservations">
Outside the Cloudbeds Marketplace, each property's checkout opens a billing account for that property alone. So each ThinkReservations property can have its own card and billing details. When we set up your group with you, tell us who pays for which property.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="mews">
Outside the Cloudbeds Marketplace, each property's checkout opens a billing account for that property alone. So each Mews property can have its own card and billing details. When you [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Connecting%20Mews) to connect one, tell us who pays for it.
</PmsTab>
</PmsTabs>

## Not now

Under the pay button, a link reads <Ui>Not now</Ui>, followed by "set this property up later". Beside it a small <Ui>?</Ui> says "You can come back to it from Billing any time." Clicking it skips the property: it leaves the payment queue, its history import pauses where it is, and the next property comes up. Nothing is charged for a property you set aside, and it does not appear in the <Ui>Property</Ui> selector until it is paid for.

The link is offered only while another property is waiting to be paid for, or one is already set up. Setting aside the last property with nothing live would leave you on a payment screen with nothing to pay for, so on that property the link does not appear. Only a General Manager or Hotel Admin can set a property aside.

If the property cannot be set aside, a message above the pay button says why:

- **"This is the only property left to set up."** The last property cannot be set aside. Pay for it, or leave the page.
- **"This property is already set up."** or **"This property is already paid for."** There is nothing to set aside. Reload the page.
- **"This property isn't waiting on a Marketplace setup."** It did not come from the Marketplace, so there is nothing to park.
- **"That property isn't yours."** Sign in with the account that claimed it.
- **"This needs General Manager access or higher on this property."** Your role on this property is below General Manager. Ask its Hotel Admin or a General Manager to set it aside.
- **"That property no longer exists."** Reload the page.

If the request itself does not go through, the same place shows one of these instead:

- **"Something went wrong on our side. Try again in a moment."** MAYA could not finish the request. Try again in a moment.
- **"Couldn't set that aside just now."** The request failed and no reason came back. Try again.
- **Your browser's own words**, such as "Failed to fetch" or "Load failed". The connection dropped. Check your internet connection, then try again.

## Picking it back up

Open <Ui>Billing</Ui> from any property you have set up. At the bottom, a section headed <Ui>Properties not set up yet</Ui> lists each parked property with "Set aside" and the date, and a <Ui>Set up</Ui> button. Click it (it reads "Opening…") and the payment screen opens with that property first. If it fails: "Could not pick that property back up." or "Could not reach MAYA. Try again."

## Billing for a group

Each property has its own <Ui>Billing</Ui> page, showing only that property's subscription. The whole group bills on the owner's one account in Stripe, and the full billing portal would show every property's subscription and the card. So a General Manager or Hotel Admin of one property who does not also manage every other property on that account gets a narrower screen: <Ui>Update card, receipts & cancellation</Ui> opens only Stripe's screen for changing that property's plan.

On that screen there is no card, no invoice list and no way to cancel. For those, ask the owner, or [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Group%20billing). If Stripe refuses even the narrow screen, the page says "Billing for a shared group is still being set up. Ask the account owner, or try again later."

The owner who paid, being Hotel Admin of every property, opens the full portal and can see every subscription there. [The billing page](/docs/billing/the-billing-page) covers what each section means.

## If the shared card fails

The properties do not all stop at once. Each one is charged on its own renewal date, so a failing card fails one property at a time, as each renewal comes round. Each property then follows its own path: "Payment overdue" while the bank retries, with MAYA still working on it, then "Unpaid" and stopped if the retries run out. A property whose renewal has not come yet keeps running as normal.

Only the owner can fix it, because the card belongs to the owner's billing account. The owner adds a working card in the full portal and saves it as the default. A subscription can keep the card it was set up with, so the owner also [emails us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Move%20to%20a%20new%20card) which properties to move onto the new card (or all of them), and we move each one. Read [Moving to another card](/docs/billing/cards-payments-and-invoices#moving-to-another-card). A General Manager of only one property cannot reach the card from their <Ui>Billing</Ui> page, so they ask the owner or [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Group%20card). [Cards, payments and invoices](/docs/billing/cards-payments-and-invoices#when-a-payment-fails) has each step of a failed payment.

## Cancelling one property in a group

Cancelling one property's subscription leaves the others running. For a connected property, the owner cancels it from the full portal on their own <Ui>Billing</Ui> page. A General Manager of only that property cannot cancel from their screen (the section above explains why), so they ask the owner or [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Cancel%20one%20property). If the property has not been connected yet, the payment screen carries a link <Ui>Manage billing or cancel for</Ui> that property's name, which opens Stripe straight onto cancelling that one subscription. [Cancelling, restarting and leaving](/docs/billing/cancelling-restarting-and-leaving) has the rest.
