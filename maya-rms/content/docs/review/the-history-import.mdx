<InPlainWords>
As soon as your property is connected, MAYA reads your past bookings: your current bookings first, then one year at a time back through up to ten years. You can close the page, because the import runs on its own. The first results (your starter rules and the review) are ready after three past years are in, or once everything is read if you have less than three years. Pricing comparisons reach three years back.
</InPlainWords>

## When it starts

The import starts on its own. You do not press anything.

- **Cloudbeds, from the Marketplace**: it starts as soon as you create your MAYA account (or sign in) and claim the property, before you have paid. By the time you reach the payment screen it is usually already running, and paying does not start it over.
- **Cloudbeds or ThinkReservations, from inside MAYA**: it starts the moment your connection succeeds.
- **Mews**: there is no history import for Mews yet. See the Mews tab below.

You can start a fresh read later from the **Rules** tab with <Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui>. That read makes suggestions only and changes nothing on its own. Read [Getting suggestions later](/docs/review/getting-suggestions-later).

<Callout kind="good-to-know" title="A login that covers several properties">
If you came from the Cloudbeds Marketplace and your login covers several properties, MAYA reads one unpaid property at a time. Choosing <Ui>Not now</Ui> for a property pauses its import where it is, and it carries on from the same place when you come back to that property. From inside MAYA, a Cloudbeds or ThinkReservations login that covers several properties cannot connect yet, so nothing is imported: [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Group%20login) and we will set them up.
</Callout>

## What it reads, in order

The progress bar names each step as it goes. In order:

1. **Reading your property setup.** Your time zone and currency (only where MAYA has nothing yet), and your room types with how many units each has. The import never changes your property's name.
2. **Reading your current bookings.** The same read MAYA makes about every five minutes from then on: bookings from 30 days ago to about 13 months ahead. For a large book this takes several passes, and the bar says so: "Reading your current bookings (part 2)".
3. **Importing past years.** One 365-day stretch at a time, going backwards from where the current read started. Up to ten stretches, so at most about ten years. It stops earlier when a year comes back empty.
4. **Building your starter rules.** After the first three past years are in, MAYA reads what it has so far and writes the review cards, sets floors and ceilings that were still at their defaults, and builds your starter rules. From here the bar reads "First results ready. Importing older years", and the review can open while the rest loads.
5. **Checking your full history.** Once every year is in, MAYA reads everything again. This pass refines the review cards: it can sharpen a card, add one, or take one away. With less than three years on record, step 4 is skipped and this is the only read, so your first results come once all of your history has been read.
6. **Import complete.**

<Callout kind="good-to-know" title="Why three years first">
Every comparison MAYA makes for pricing (similar past nights, seasons, the checks behind a "Not a fair comparison?" answer) reaches at most three years back. So three years is the smallest history on which the review and the starter rules work as they will with the full import.
</Callout>

## What is kept

For each past stay night MAYA keeps six things: the booking's reference number from your property system, the stay date, the room type, the nightly price, the date the booking was made, and the number of days between the two. The reference number is how a booking with several rooms counts as one booking in booking speed.

It keeps nothing about the guest. No names, no emails, no card details. For past stays nothing else about the booking is stored, only those six fields. What MAYA keeps about your current and future bookings is covered in [What MAYA reads and writes](/docs/connect/what-maya-reads-and-writes).

## How long it takes

There is no fixed time, and MAYA does not promise one. It depends on how much history your property system holds and how quickly that system answers. A small property with two years of bookings finishes far sooner than a 200-room property with ten.

The import runs on our side in short bursts and picks itself up again between them, so it does not need your browser open. Close the page, come back later, and the progress bar shows where it got to.

Two things you may see on a large book:

- "Reading your current bookings (part 2)", "(part 3)" and so on. One pass was not enough to cover the window, so it is taking another.
- A very large history can stop before ten years. MAYA reads up to a set number of room nights (one room for one night), and the limit grows with the size of your property.

<Example title="The exact limit, for the curious">
MAYA reads at most 300,000 room nights, or more for a larger property: about one and a half times your room count, times 366 nights, times ten. It never reads more than 3,000,000. The Harbour Inn's 20 rooms come to about 110,000, so its limit is 300,000.
</Example>

## Your first day: using MAYA while it runs

You can. Here is what to expect:

- The dashboard fills in as the import goes. Your current bookings arrive in step 2, so the calendar shows real occupancy early on, while older years are still loading.
- Your rules run during the import. MAYA runs them on each night whose bookings changed, within about five minutes, and on every night up to 395 nights ahead once a day, and writes the result in the <Ui>Change Log</Ui> tab. Each stretch of runs that change nothing is counted into one line, such as "Prices checked 47 times from Sep 24, 10:05 AM to 1:55 PM, nothing needed to change."
- Your property starts in simulation, so no rule, starter or yours, changes anything in your system until you go live.
- With Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations, MAYA also reads the rate you already have for each night, about once an hour, and your rules work from it. Mews rates are not read.
- When the first results are in and there are cards waiting for your answer, a blue banner on the dashboard links to the review (it ends with the link <Ui>Review →</Ui>). If there is nothing to ask you, there is no banner. Open the review at <AppLink to="review">maya-rms.com/onboarding/review</AppLink>.

The progress page has a link, <Ui>Go to my dashboard →</Ui>, at the bottom.

## The progress bar and the progress page

The progress bar sits under the five questions and on the progress page. It shows the current step on the left and, on the right, how many room-nights have been read and, once an oldest stay is known, "back to" that month (for example "back to 2019-03").

The dot beside the step pulses while the import runs, turns green when it is complete, and turns amber if it has paused or stopped.

The progress page adds three tiles: <Ui>Room-nights</Ui>, <Ui>Years covered</Ui> (the span between your oldest and newest stay, at least 1) and <Ui>Oldest stay</Ui>. It moves on by itself: to the review as soon as there is something to review, or to the dashboard if the import finished with nothing to review. If the work was already done when you arrived, it moves on straight away. Otherwise it waits a moment so you can see it finish.

<Callout kind="good-to-know">
Until your first results are ready, the heading on the progress page says the work is under way. MAYA is reading your bookings, and the review lists what it found. If the import pauses or stops, the heading says so instead ("Your import is paused" or "Your import has stopped"), and the bar says what happens next.
</Callout>

## "Import stopped"

If the bar reads "Import paused" or "Import stopped" with an amber dot, the import has stopped trying for now: your property system stopped answering for a long stretch, or a read kept ending in the same place. Your connection, your rules and everything already read are safe.

When it stops, an automatic alert goes to our team. Then the import tries again by itself, up to three times, each time from where it had got to. It waits an hour before the first try, four hours before the second and twelve hours before the third. While it waits, the bar says when the next try is, for example "Import paused. We've been told, and it tries again by itself around 3:40 PM." Once a try is under way, the bar shows the step it is on again.

If the third try stops too, the bar reads "Import stopped. We've been told." and the import does not try again by itself. [Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Import%20stopped) and we will restart it from where it left off.

"We've been told" appears only when the alert reached us. If the bar says "Import stopped. Email us and we'll restart it." instead, nothing reached us, so please [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Import%20stopped). A Cloudbeds Marketplace property you have not paid for yet does not try again by itself: paying for it (a free trial counts) starts the import again from where it stopped.

If your connection drops while the import runs, the dot turns amber, the bar keeps the name of the step it had reached, and the reconnect prompt appears on your dashboard. Read [Reconnect and disconnect](/docs/connect/reconnect-and-disconnect).

Reconnecting brings the connection back, but the import does not carry on by itself. [Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Restart%20my%20import) and we will restart it from where it left off. Everything it had already read is kept.

A Cloudbeds Marketplace property is the exception. If you pay for it (a free trial counts), clicking <Ui>Connect App</Ui> for MAYA in the Marketplace again picks the import up where it left off. If you have not paid yet, it picks up when the property next comes up on the payment screen.

The prompt takes the bar's place in one case only: a Cloudbeds Marketplace property you never paid for, left unused for 180 days, whose connection was removed (see [Reading again later](#reading-again-later)).

## What comes out of it

When the read has run, you have:

- your room types with their unit counts, and MAYA's first proposal for which of them are rooms,
- the review cards: closures, room types that may not be rooms, duplicates, rates that look like typos, stays with no rate, stays that point at deleted room types,
- floors and ceilings for room types that were still at their defaults,
- five starter rules, if you have enough history and no rules yet.

Your property's own rates (the starting point your rules work from) are not part of the import. The regular cycle reads them once your property is paid for. A free trial counts.

Read [Checking what MAYA found](/docs/review/checking-what-maya-found), [Your starter rules](/docs/review/your-starter-rules) and [Floors and ceilings](/docs/review/floors-and-ceilings).

## Reading again later

- <Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui> on the **Rules** tab reads your whole history again and turns what it finds into cards you accept or reject one by one. Read [Getting suggestions later](/docs/review/getting-suggestions-later).
- A Cloudbeds property from the Marketplace that never paid may have its booking history removed after 180 days without use. When you come back, reconnecting starts a fresh import, and nothing is priced until it completes. Your rules and settings are kept.

## Per property system

<PmsTabs>
<PmsTab pms="cloudbeds">
MAYA reads each booking with every room in it, each with its own room type and nightly rates. Every booking status is read. Confirmed, checked-in, checked-out and not-yet-confirmed stays are kept. Cancelled and no-show bookings are left out, and a booking cancelled since an earlier read has its nights removed.

A few Cloudbeds accounts refuse the detailed booking read. MAYA then reads a plainer list for your past years. From that list, a past booking's nightly price is an average of its total, and a past booking with several rooms may be counted as one room. The list holds only the kept stays, so a past booking cancelled since an earlier read keeps its nights. Current bookings are read in full either way.

Cloudbeds reports how many rooms each room type has, so your occupancy percentages are right from the first read. Bookings marked Confirmation Pending count as booked, as they do in Cloudbeds' own occupancy.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="thinkreservations">
One listing carries the booking, its status and its line items, so there is no fallback path.

MAYA counts each room type's rooms from ThinkReservations' list of rooms, so your occupancy percentages are right from the first read. A room marked inactive, as not a room, or as not included in occupancy is left out. [ThinkReservations](/docs/connect/thinkreservations) has the details.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="mews">
MAYA does not read Mews history yet. A Mews property has no import, no review cards, no starter rules and no floors or ceilings from data. Occupancy and booking window rules work from the bookings MAYA reads each cycle. Booking speed rules fire less often at first: with little history a reading stays within one step of Normal, so rules set to Much Faster Than Normal, Surging, Much Slower Than Normal or Stalled stay quiet. <Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui> is greyed out on a Mews property. Email us if you want history read by hand.
</PmsTab>
</PmsTabs>

<Callout kind="not-yet">
There is no button to start, pause or restart the import by hand. It starts when you connect, and the only later re-read is <Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui>. If the bar says "Import stopped", or the dot is still amber after you reconnect, email us.
</Callout>

<Example title="The Harbour Inn's import">
Sam connects The Harbour Inn (20 rooms) on a Tuesday evening. The bar reads "Reading your property setup", then "Reading your current bookings". Within a few minutes the calendar shows the coming nights with real occupancy. The bar moves to "Importing past years" and the right side counts up: "4,120 room-nights · back to 2024-09". After three past years it reads "First results ready. Importing older years", and the review opens with two closure cards and the five starter rules. Sam closes the laptop. In the morning the bar reads "Import complete", "Years covered" says 7, and one extra card has appeared: a seasonal closure that only showed once four years of history were in.
</Example>
