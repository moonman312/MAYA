<InPlainWords>
Off freezes: the rule stops acting and the prices it already set stay exactly as they are. Delete undoes: every night it adjusted goes back to what it would have been. There is no edit yet. To change a rule, delete it and add it again.
</InPlainWords>

## The On/Off switch

Each rule on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab has a switch under <Ui>Status</Ui> that reads <Ui>On</Ui> or <Ui>Off</Ui>. Click it to flip it. There is no confirmation, and it takes effect from the next cycle. A rule cannot be switched <Ui>On</Ui> while 40 others are on (read [How many rules you can have](#how-many-rules-you-can-have)).

Off means the rule stops acting. It does not undo anything:

- a standard rule's change that was on a night stays on that night, and does not come off when the condition stops holding, because the rule is no longer being checked
- a booking speed or pickup rule's raises and cuts stay on the price, and its raises are not tested for cancellations while it is off
- on the day cards and in your property system, nothing moves

Turn the rule back <Ui>On</Ui> and it picks up from the next cycle. A standard rule that was holding a change then lets it go if the condition no longer holds.

<Callout kind="good-to-know" title="A switched-off rule still marks where counting starts">
After a night has been raised, a booking speed rule that raises counts only the bookings made since that raise, whichever booking speed or pickup rule made it. A rule you switched off still made its raise, so it still marks where the other raise rules start counting on that night. Read [Counting after a rule acts](/docs/rules/booking-speed-in-detail#counting-after-a-rule-acts).
</Callout>

## Delete

The red <Ui>Delete</Ui> button at the end of a rule's row opens a dialog headed "Delete "Busy nights"?". It says that deleting the rule also undoes the price changes it has already made, and that any night it adjusted goes back to what the price would be if the rule had never run. Then, in amber: "Deleting cannot be undone." Three buttons:

- <Ui>Delete Rule and Undo Its Changes</Ui>: removes the rule and every change it made, from the next cycle
- <Ui>Turn It Off and Keep Its Changes</Ui>: switches the rule off and closes the dialog
- <Ui>Cancel</Ui>

The change log keeps its price entries, but older entries lose the deleted rule's reasons. A standard rule's move still shows, named "Pricing rule", with no sentence saying why. A booking speed or pickup rule's move is left out of those entries. Your answers to the rule's keeps-adjusting banner (the "Your answer" entries) are removed along with the rule. New entries show the price without it. Read [The change log](/docs/watch/the-change-log).

<Callout kind="careful">
Deleting a rule changes prices. Every night the rule had adjusted is re-priced without it on the next cycle. On a live Cloudbeds or ThinkReservations property, those prices are sent to your system. On Mews they change in MAYA only. If you want the prices to stay where they are, turn the rule off instead.
</Callout>

## Changing a rule

There is no edit control. To change a rule:

1. Click <Ui>Delete</Ui>, then <Ui>Delete Rule and Undo Its Changes</Ui>. Its changes come off.
2. Click <Ui>+ Add a rule</Ui> and build it again with the new settings. It starts <Ui>On</Ui> and acts from the next cycle.

If you want to keep the old prices in place while the new rule takes over, turn the old rule off instead of deleting it, and leave it off.

The one change that can be made in place is a "Tune ... ?" card after <Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui>, which moves the occupancy threshold of a standard rule. From the next cycle the rule checks every night against the new threshold, so a night that no longer meets it loses the change, and a night that now meets it gains one. Read [Getting suggestions later](/docs/review/getting-suggestions-later) and [Change a rule](/docs/recipes/change-a-rule).

<Callout kind="not-yet">
You cannot edit, rename, copy or duplicate a rule, or copy one to another property. For all of these, add the rule again. A rule you build in the <Ui>Rate Simulator</Ui> arrives on the Rules tab switched off, which is handy for building the replacement before you delete the original.
</Callout>

## The list

The rules table has seven columns: <Ui>Name</Ui>, <Ui>Fired</Ui>, <Ui>Conditions</Ui>, <Ui>Room Types</Ui>, <Ui>Price Change</Ui>, <Ui>Status</Ui> and the <Ui>Delete</Ui> button. On a narrow screen it scrolls sideways.

**Order.** Most fired first, then rules that are on before rules that are off, then by name.

**Filter.** The pills above the table, <Ui>All</Ui>, <Ui>Enabled</Ui> and <Ui>Disabled</Ui>, show every rule, only the ones that are on, or only the ones that are off.

**Fired.** How many times the rule has acted, counted once per night and room type. A dash means never. A "?" beside the heading explains that a booking speed or pickup rule can act on the same night more than once, and each time counts, and that a change that came off later still counts. So the count is usually higher than the changes you can see on the calendar now, and higher than the change log shows, which lists only the 40 biggest moves of each run.

**Conditions.** In words: "Occupancy above 80%", "Booking window below 7 days", "Pickup above 5 bookings", "booking speed at least Faster Than Normal (past week), then waits 1 week". Several are joined with a dot.

**Room Types.** The types the rule changes, or "Watches Standard, Deluxe · Changes Suite" when it measures different ones.

**Price Change.** "+10%", "-15%", "+$25", "-$25". The sign in this column is always a dollar sign, whatever your property's currency. The amount is in your currency. The change log and the keeps-adjusting banner use your currency's own symbol.

**Stopped on N nights.** An amber chip under the switch when you have told the rule to stop on some upcoming nights, with a "?" and a <Ui>Let it run again</Ui> link. Read [Rules that keep adjusting](/docs/rules/rules-that-keep-adjusting).

## How many rules you can have

At most 40 rules can be <Ui>On</Ui> at once on a property. Rules that are <Ui>Off</Ui> do not count, and a rule saved from the <Ui>Rate Simulator</Ui> arrives Off. At 40, adding a rule shows "This property already has 40 active rules, which is the maximum." in red under the form, and the switch on an Off rule will not turn it On (no message appears, and the switch stays Off). Turn a rule off or delete one first.

Every occupancy or booking window rule whose condition holds applies, and their changes stack. Booking speed and pickup rules take turns: at most one fires on a night and room type per run. Read [How rules run](/docs/rules/how-rules-run).

## Who can do this

Revenue Managers, General Managers and Hotel Admins can switch and delete rules. A Viewer sees the switch and the <Ui>Delete</Ui> button, but clicking them changes nothing. Read [Roles: who can do what](/docs/team/roles).

## Who did what

The app does not show who added, switched or deleted a rule. If you need to know, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Who%20changed%20a%20rule) with the rule's name and roughly when.

The change log has no entry for adding a rule, switching it on or off, or deleting it: it shows only what those steps did to prices. The people the change log does name are whoever typed a price ("Priya set the base rate to $180.00.") and whoever answered the keeps-adjusting banner (the "Your answer" entries).
