<InPlainWords>
Pickup count is how many room nights (one room for one night) or how much revenue a night gained over 1, 3 or 7 whole days at your property: what is booked now minus what was booked when the window opened. A rule set to Greater than counts today so far and the days before it; one set to Less than counts full days up to yesterday. Cancellations pull it down, so it can be negative. It compares with a fixed number you type. Booking speed is usually the better choice, because it compares with your own similar nights instead.
</InPlainWords>

## The definition

MAYA records what is booked for a night each time your rules price it: within about five minutes of its bookings changing, and at least once a day. A record stands until the next one, since nothing changed in between. A pickup rule compares two of those records, over the room types the rule measures:

- **now**: what is booked for the night now (for a rule set to <Ui>Less than</Ui>, when today began, less anything cancelled since)
- **when the window opened**: what was booked when the first day of the lookback began at your property

Pickup is now minus then. For pickup, each room on each night counts one: a four-room booking for one night is 4 room nights of pickup. That is different from [booking speed](/docs/rules/booking-speed), where the same booking counts once.

<Ui>Pickup measures</Ui> sets the unit:

- <Ui>Room nights (units)</Ui>: the number of booked rooms, rounded to a whole number.
- <Ui>Revenue (currency)</Ui>: the sum of each booked room night's current rate, as your property system reports it, in your property's currency, to the cent. Nothing is converted.

A booking counts the same whatever channel it came from. There is no way to count only direct bookings.

<Callout kind="example" title="Three days at The Harbour Inn">
When Monday, October 5, 2026 began, the 12 Standard rooms for Friday, November 13 had 5 booked. On Wednesday, October 7, 8 are booked. Pickup over 3 days (Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday so far) is 8 minus 5, which is 3 room nights. A rule set to <Ui>Greater than</Ui> 2 fires. If two of those guests had cancelled on Tuesday, pickup would be 1 and the rule would not fire.
</Callout>

## The fields

Choose <Ui>Pickup count (advanced)</Ui> in the <Ui>Metric</Ui> dropdown. The row shows:

- <Ui>Compare</Ui>: <Ui>Greater than</Ui> or <Ui>Less than</Ui>. Comparisons are strict: <Ui>Greater than</Ui> 5 means 6 or more room nights, not 5 exactly.
- <Ui>Threshold</Ui>: the number of room nights, or the amount, starting at 5. It must be 0 or more. Decimals are kept.
- <Ui>Lookback window</Ui>: <Ui>1 day</Ui>, <Ui>3 days</Ui> or <Ui>7 days</Ui>, starting at <Ui>3 days</Ui>.
- <Ui>Then waits (advanced)</Ui>: how long the rule leaves a night alone after acting on it. It starts on <Ui>Same as the lookback window</Ui>, and also offers <Ui>1 day</Ui>, <Ui>2 days</Ui>, <Ui>3 days</Ui>, <Ui>1 week</Ui> and <Ui>2 weeks</Ui>.
- <Ui>Pickup measures</Ui>: <Ui>Room nights (units)</Ui> or <Ui>Revenue (currency)</Ui>, starting at room nights.

## Lookback windows are whole days

A lookback window counts whole days on your property's calendar. It moves on only at your property's midnight, so the passing of time never changes a pickup count in the middle of the day. Only bookings do.

- **A rule set to <Ui>Greater than</Ui>** counts today so far and the days before it. With <Ui>3 days</Ui>, that is today, yesterday and the day before: it compares what is booked now with the most recent record taken at or before the moment the day before yesterday began.
- **A rule set to <Ui>Less than</Ui>** counts full days only, up to yesterday, because a day that is not over yet would read as slow. With <Ui>3 days</Ui>, that is the three days before today. A cancellation made today counts against it straight away. A booking made today counts from tomorrow.

Booking speed counts whole days too, in booking dates. Read [Time, weekends, currency and holidays](/docs/reference/time-weekends-currency-and-holidays).

## Negative pickup

When more rooms cancel than book, pickup is below zero. A rule set to <Ui>Less than</Ui> 0 fires on nights that are losing bookings. A rule set to <Ui>Greater than</Ui> anything does not fire on them. The threshold itself cannot be negative.

<Example title="A pickup rule at The Harbour Inn">
"Quick pickup" is <Ui>Pickup count (advanced)</Ui> <Ui>Greater than</Ui> 4 over <Ui>3 days</Ui>, measuring room nights, and raises Standard, Deluxe and Suite (the builder's default: the types that count as rooms) by 10%.

When the day before yesterday began, the Saturday had 8 rooms booked across Standard, Deluxe and Suite. Now it has 14. Pickup is 6, which is more than 4, so the rule fires and raises Standard, Deluxe and Suite by 10%. It then waits 3 days (the same as its lookback window) before it can act on that night again.

If two of those bookings cancel, the pickup the raise counted is down to 4, not more than 4. With the undo box ticked, the raise comes off on the next cycle, unless bookings made since keep the rule true (read below).
</Example>

## When pickup cannot be measured

A pickup rule needs a record from when the window opened. When there is none for one of the measured room types, or the most recent one is more than 12 hours older than that moment and your property had no cycle at all in the 12 hours before it, the condition is false and the rule does nothing on that night. An older record on a quiet night is fine: it is still what the night had. This happens:

- **On a newly connected property, or for a new room type.** MAYA starts keeping records the first time your rules run. A 3-day lookback can only be measured once your rules have been running for 3 days.
- **After a long gap in runs**, for example a connection that was down for days. Once your rules run again, the rule measures again after a full lookback.
- **When every room type the rule measures is unticked as a room, or has been switched off in MAYA.** There is nothing to record. Read [Measure one set of room types, change another](/docs/rules/measure-one-set-change-another).
- **On the furthest nights of the 396-night window.** A night is first recorded a few minutes after it enters the window at midnight, and needs a record from before its lookback began. A rule set to <Ui>Greater than</Ui> cannot act on the furthest 1, 3 or 7 nights of the window (its lookback in days), and one set to <Ui>Less than</Ui> on one night more. Each night becomes measurable as it moves closer.

Records are kept for at least 14 days, whatever lookback your rules use. That is a week more than the longest lookback, 7 days, so a 7-day rule has what it needs once it has run for a week, except on those furthest nights.

## A pickup rule fires, holds, waits and can fire again

A pickup rule works like a booking speed rule. When it fires, its change stays on the price. It then leaves that night and room type alone for its wait: its lookback window unless you chose another in <Ui>Then waits (advanced)</Ui>. A "?" beside the dropdown says which wait the rule keeps.

When the wait is over, it counts pickup over its lookback window again, but only what came in since the newest change still on the night by itself or by a stronger rule that moves the price the same way: the room nights (or revenue) booked after that change and still booked. A booking from before the change cancelling takes nothing from that count. So a wait shorter than the window never counts the same burst twice, and a 3-day rule with a 1-day wait can raise again the next day only on new pickup.

A rule set to <Ui>Less than</Ui> looks for low pickup, and a short stretch always looks low. Its change counted the full days before the day it was made, so it judges again only once a whole lookback window of full days, starting with that day, has passed: a 3-day rule that cut on Monday judges again as Thursday begins, whatever wait you chose. The shorter options say "(low pickup holds it to 3 days)".

A rule that has both a pickup row and a booking speed row waits the longer of the two, and each <Ui>Then waits (advanced)</Ui> dropdown says so beside its shorter options: "(pickup holds it to 3 days)" on the booking speed row, "(booking speed holds it to 1 week)" on the pickup row. Read [How rules run](/docs/rules/how-rules-run).

<Callout kind="good-to-know">
Pickup rules and booking speed rules rank on one list, so a stronger rule's raise covers the weaker ones whichever kind each is: they count only the bookings made after it. A stronger rule changes the price by more, and at the same change a rule that watches booking speed is stronger than one that does not. Read [Counting after a change](/docs/rules/how-rules-run#counting-after-a-change).
</Callout>

## When a pickup raise comes off

With the rule's undo box ticked, as it is on every new rule, a raise made by a <Ui>Greater than</Ui> pickup rule comes off when cancellations take the pickup it counted to your number or below, and the rule, counted as it would be now with bookings made since, is not true either. A rule set to <Ui>Less than</Ui> only gets truer as bookings cancel, so cancellations never undo its change. With the box unticked, cancellations never take a change off. While the rule is switched off, its changes stay on the price and are not checked for cancellations. Switch it back on and the check resumes. Read [When a price change comes off](/docs/rules/when-a-change-comes-off).

## What the rule card and the change log say

On screen, pickup always says "bookings". Read it as room nights (or your amount, for a revenue rule).

On the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab a pickup condition reads "Pickup above 5 bookings (past 3 days), then waits 3 days" or "Pickup below 2 bookings (past week), then waits 1 week". A rule that also watches booking speed names its wait once, on the booking speed condition. In the change log it reads "9 bookings arrived that day and the 2 days before, past the 4-booking mark you set.", where 9 is room nights (a two-room booking for the night is 2) and "that day and the 2 days before" is the lookback, counted from the day of the change. A rule set to <Ui>Less than</Ui> reads "1 booking arrived in the 3 full days before that day, under the 2-booking mark you set." After a change on the night it reads "6 bookings arrived since this rule or a stronger one's latest raise still on this night, past the 4-booking mark you set." When a rule measures different room types from the ones it changes, the sentence names them: "9 Standard and Deluxe bookings arrived that day and the 2 days before, past the 4-booking mark you set."

The <Ui>Rules that keep adjusting</Ui> banner names the unit: "Pickup over the 3 days it counted came to 6 room nights, against the 4 you set." A revenue rule shows money there. When the count ran from a raise, it reads "Pickup since the raise before its latest one, by it or a stronger rule, came to 6 room nights".

To be sure what an older rule measures, build it again with the settings you want and switch the old one off, which keeps its prices. Deleting it would undo its changes.

## Pickup or booking speed

Both look at what a night gained recently. The difference is what the count is compared with. Pickup's fixed number is right for some nights and wrong for others: 5 room nights in 3 days is a rush for a quiet Tuesday and nothing special for a Saturday in peak season.

| | Pickup count | Booking speed |
|---|---|---|
| Compares with | a fixed number you type | what similar past nights had received by the same point |
| Counts | room nights, or revenue | bookings (a booking with several rooms counts once) |
| Window | 1, 3 or 7 whole days at your property | past day, week or month, in booking dates |
| Needs history | no, only records from the window start | yes, similar past nights |
| Same count in February and August | reads the same | reads differently, against each season's own pace |

Pickup suits a fixed line you already trust, a property with no history yet, or a rule that should watch revenue. Booking speed suits everything else, which is why the rule builder labels it <Ui>Booking speed (recommended)</Ui>.

When you click <Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui> on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab, MAYA may suggest removing a pickup rule that one of your booking speed rules already covers (it measures and changes at least the room types your pickup rule does, on at least the same days and dates). The suggestion says the pickup rule reacts to a fixed booking count that the booking speed rules now cover with pace awareness, and that keeping both would stack two price reactions on the same demand. You choose <Ui>Remove it</Ui>, <Ui>Turn it off, keep it</Ui> or <Ui>Leave it running</Ui>. Nothing changes unless you accept. Read [Getting suggestions later](/docs/review/getting-suggestions-later).

## In the Rate Simulator

The <Ui>Rate Simulator</Ui> has a <Ui>Picked up</Ui> box per room type, in rooms. A revenue rule is tested by valuing those rooms at the base price you typed for that room type.

The rule card does not show whether a pickup rule measures room nights or revenue. Put it in the rule's name when you build it, for example Pickup 3 days revenue.
</Callout>
