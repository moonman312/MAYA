<InPlainWords>
Settings opens from the gear at the top of the dashboard. Under <Ui>Calendar</Ui> you choose what each day on the calendar shows and which way its colours read, and everyone on your property sees your choice. On Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations, a section named for your system sets what MAYA does when a price it sent is changed there: keep the change as your price, or MAYA's price wins. Under <Ui>Display</Ui> you choose your own text size, and it follows you to any device you sign in on. Every change saves as soon as you make it.
</InPlainWords>

## Where it is

At the top right of the dashboard, between <Ui off>Help</Ui> and <Ui>Billing</Ui>, a small gear button opens <Ui>Settings</Ui>. On a phone it is a button reading <Ui>Settings</Ui> in the column under the MAYA logo. It opens over the dashboard, whichever tab you are on. Close it with the <Ui>×</Ui>, the Esc key, or a click outside it. The <Ui off>Help</Ui> link at its top opens this page.

Its sections come one after the other: <Ui>Calendar</Ui>, then one named for your property system (Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations only), then <Ui>Display</Ui>. Each says who it is for under its heading, and a short "Saved" appears beside the heading once a change has saved.

## Calendar

This section reads "For everyone on" and your property's name. What you choose here is what everyone on the property sees on their <Ui>Calendar</Ui> tab. Your own calendar changes behind the open Settings as soon as you choose. Everyone else sees it the next time their calendar refreshes: within five minutes, or as soon as they come back to the browser tab or reload the page.

### What each day shows

Every day on the calendar shows its date, one big number, up to two small lines under it, and the colour bar. You pick the numbers in three lists:

| List | What it sets | As it starts |
|---|---|---|
| <Ui>Big number</Ui> | The large figure on each day, never <Ui>None</Ui> | <Ui>Occupancy</Ui> |
| <Ui>First small line</Ui> | The line under it, or <Ui>None</Ui> | <Ui>Rooms booked</Ui> |
| <Ui>Second small line</Ui> | The line under that, or <Ui>None</Ui> | <Ui>Room revenue</Ui> |

As it starts, a day reads exactly as it always has: "60%", then "12/20 rooms", then "$2.2k".

A day shows each number once. Pick a number that is already showing in another place and the two swap: make <Ui>Rooms booked</Ui> the big number and occupancy moves down into its line. Set the first small line to <Ui>None</Ui> and the second moves up. The second list is greyed out while the first reads <Ui>None</Ui>.

When a price shows anywhere on the day, a fourth list appears, <Ui>Price for</Ui>, with your room types. It starts on the first room type that counts as a room, in name order. Pick the one whose price you want to watch.

### The numbers you can pick

| In the list | On a day | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| <Ui>Occupancy</Ui> | 60% | Sellable occupancy: rooms booked divided by rooms you can sell that night, over the room types ticked as rooms, rounded to a whole percent. |
| <Ui>Rooms booked</Ui> | 12/20 rooms | Rooms booked, then rooms you can sell that night. |
| <Ui>Room revenue</Ui> | $2.2k | Room revenue on the books for the night, every room type included. |
| <Ui>ADR</Ui> | ADR $182 | The room revenue of the room types ticked as rooms, divided by the rooms booked in them. A dash when nothing is booked. |
| <Ui>RevPAR</Ui> | RevPAR $109 | That same room revenue divided by the rooms you can sell that night. |
| <Ui>Price for a room type</Ui> | Price $165 | The price MAYA has published for the room type you pick: the "Current price" on its card in the day detail. A dash when there is none, for the same reasons that card shows one. |

On a small line, ADR, RevPAR and the price have that word in front. As the big number they show the figure alone. Rest the mouse on any number to see what it is, "Price for Standard" for example.

Amounts carry your property's currency sign. Under 1,000 they are whole ("$182"); from 1,000 they are thousands with one decimal ("$2.2k"). The day detail below the calendar has every amount in full.

ADR and RevPAR leave out room types you unticked under <Ui>Counts as a room</Ui>, as occupancy does. The room revenue figure counts every room type, so on a night a meeting room is booked, ADR is not simply that figure divided by the rooms booked. RevPAR here is per room you can sell that night, so rooms out of service are taken off first. The calendar's colours use their own revenue per room, which [The calendar](/docs/watch/the-calendar#what-the-colours-mean) explains.

On a phone the days are narrow, so each shows its big number only. A day too narrow for the whole number shows a shorter form: "12/20" for rooms booked, and whole thousands from 10,000, such as "$11k". Tap a day and its detail opens under the calendar with everything.

<Example title="The Harbour Inn watches its Standard price">
The Harbour Inn picks <Ui>ADR</Ui> as the big number, <Ui>RevPAR</Ui> as the first small line and <Ui>Price for a room type</Ui> as the second, with <Ui>Price for</Ui> on Standard. On Friday, November 13, 2026, 12 of 20 rooms are booked for $2,180 of room revenue, and Standard's current price is $165. The day reads "$182", then "RevPAR $109", then "Price $165". ADR is $2,180 over 12 rooms booked, $181.67, and RevPAR is $2,180 over 20 rooms you can sell.

A night further out than MAYA prices shows "Price –", because Standard has no price there yet.

### Colours

Two choices, <Ui>Standard</Ui> and <Ui>Reversed</Ui>. Every property starts on <Ui>Standard</Ui>.

| | Green | Amber | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Ui>Standard</Ui> | Strong night | Typical night | Weak night |
| <Ui>Reversed</Ui> | Weak night, keep working on it | Typical night | Strong night, leave it |

<Ui>Reversed</Ui> only swaps which colour marks which nights. A night is strong, typical or weak in the same way in both: by its revenue per room, against your own nights. Every colour bar on the calendar follows your choice, and so does the key on the left of the calendar, which reads "Weak night, keep working on it" beside green and "Strong night, leave it" beside red. On a narrower screen, such as a phone, the key is one short line above the days and leaves off "keep working on it" and "leave it".

The <Ui>?</Ui> beside <Ui>Colours</Ui> says the same in three lines.

### Who can change it

A Revenue Manager, General Manager or Hotel Admin can change this section. Anyone else on the property sees it greyed out, with the property's choices in it and the line "Only a Revenue Manager or above can change these." MAYA support staff see it the same way unless they are in their support mode, and a change they make then shows in your change log as "Changed by MAYA support".

When a change cannot be saved, the line beside the heading says why in red, and the list goes back to what was saved.

## Changes made in your property system

This section is named for your property system, <Ui>Cloudbeds</Ui> or <Ui>Think Reservations</Ui>, and reads "For everyone on" and your property's name. It holds one setting, <Ui>When a price MAYA sent is changed in Cloudbeds</Ui> (or your system's name), with two choices:

| Choice | On a night MAYA has sent a price to |
|---|---|
| <Ui>Keep the change as your price</Ui> | A rate you change there is kept as your price for that night, and a rate you remove there stops MAYA pricing the night until your system has a rate again. Every property starts on this one. |
| <Ui>MAYA's price wins</Ui> | Nothing changed or removed there is kept. MAYA sends its own price again, lists each night in the <Ui>Change Log</Ui>, and emails your General Managers and Hotel Admins once a day about them. |

<Ui>MAYA's price wins</Ui> is for a property that also runs another pricing tool, which changes rates in your system on its own. Your system cannot tell MAYA who or what changed a rate, only that it differs from what MAYA sent. A price you type on MAYA's calendar is kept either way. The <Ui>?</Ui> beside the setting says the same in three lines. [Rates you change in your property system](/docs/watch/rates-changed-in-your-pms#your-setting) has every detail.

The section shows only on Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations, the systems MAYA reads your rates from. On Mews, or before your system is connected, there is no such section.

### Turning MAYA's price wins on

When no night ahead keeps a rate changed in your system, choosing <Ui>MAYA's price wins</Ui> saves at once.

When some do, nothing is saved yet. A short box opens under the choices, reading for example "3 nights keep a rate changed in Cloudbeds. MAYA will replace them with its prices.", with two buttons:

- <Ui>Replace them</Ui> saves the setting. Each of those nights is handed back to MAYA, as <Ui>Clear</Ui> on the calendar does: the rate kept from your system stops being your price, and the rules it had paused apply again. A night whose rate you removed in your system is priced by MAYA again. MAYA sends its prices to those nights on its next run, within five minutes and usually straight away. Prices typed in MAYA are not touched.
- <Ui>Cancel</Ui> closes the box and changes nothing.

Only tonight and the nights after it count. Turning <Ui>MAYA's price wins</Ui> off changes nothing that has happened: from then on, a change in your system is kept as your price again.

### Who can change the property system setting

The same people as the calendar: a Revenue Manager, General Manager or Hotel Admin. Anyone else sees the choices greyed out with the line "Only a Revenue Manager or above can change these." A change MAYA support makes in their support mode shows in your change log as "Changed by MAYA support".

## Display

This section reads "Just for you". What you choose here changes MAYA for you alone, whatever your role.

### Text size

<Ui>Text size</Ui> has three choices: <Ui>Standard</Ui>, <Ui>Large</Ui> and <Ui>Larger</Ui>. <Ui>Large</Ui> makes MAYA's text and the space around it an eighth bigger, and <Ui>Larger</Ui> a quarter bigger. It changes the whole page at once: the dashboard, the Billing and Team pages, the setup screens and these docs.

Your choice is saved with your login, so it follows you to any computer, phone or tablet you sign in on, from the first page after you sign in. The browser also keeps it, so each page opens at your size straight away. These docs use the size your browser keeps, even when you are not signed in.

If the size cannot be saved with your login, the line beside the heading reads "Couldn't save your text size. Try again." The page you are on still shows it, but the dashboard goes back to your saved size the next time it opens. Choose it again to keep it.

A few small details keep their size at <Ui>Larger</Ui>, such as the little day squares in the popup that opens when you switch a rule on. On a phone, <Ui>Larger</Ui> leaves less room for the calendar's big numbers, so they shrink to fit their day.
