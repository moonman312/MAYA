<InPlainWords>
The <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab shows whether your property system is connected, how the last 24 hours of requests to it went, when bookings last synced, and the most recent requests MAYA made. Problems sending prices are not here. They are in the change log. When the connection breaks on Cloudbeds or ThinkReservations, a General Manager or Hotel Admin sees a <Ui>Reconnect</Ui> button here, and in a banner at the top of the dashboard whichever tab is open. On Mews, the panel asks you to send us a message.
</InPlainWords>

## Where it is

The <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab is the last of the five tabs. Everyone on the property can open it. The heading reads "Property System (Cloudbeds)", with your system's name in brackets once connected, and one line under it: "Bookings sync automatically on a schedule. This page shows how that connection is doing."

Before it loads: "Loading connection status…". With no property chosen: "Select a property to view status." With no system connected yet: "No property system is connected for this property yet."

The <Ui>Room types</Ui> section sits under the connection cards on the same tab. [Room types and rooms out of service](/docs/watch/room-types-and-rooms-out-of-service) covers it.

## The three cards

### Connection

A single word, colour-coded. It is the state of the connection as MAYA last recorded it.

| Word | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Connected (green) | Healthy. MAYA reads bookings and, when you are live, sends prices. | Nothing. |
| Pending (amber) | Connected but not yet paid for. Nothing runs. | Finish setting up payment. |
| Degraded (amber) | Still working. MAYA keeps reading bookings and, when you are live, sending prices. Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations only. | Usually nothing: it tends to clear by itself on the next read. If it is still there in an hour, click Reconnect. It does no harm. |
| Error (red) | Your system rejected MAYA's saved access. Nothing is read or sent while it lasts. MAYA keeps trying, up to an hour between tries, and the card goes back to Connected by itself once a try works. | If it has not cleared within a couple of hours, click Reconnect. It does no harm sooner. |
| Disconnected (red) | MAYA's access is gone: the app was uninstalled, its permission removed, or your system refused it. Nothing runs until someone reconnects. | Reconnect. |
| unknown (grey) | MAYA has no status on file for the connection yet. | Open the tab again in a few minutes. If it stays, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Connection%20status%20unknown). |

Degraded looks more worrying than it is. While the card reads Degraded, the amber panel headed "MAYA has lost its connection to Cloudbeds" (or your system's name) shows anyway, and says your prices aren't updating. For Degraded that line is not true: nothing has stopped and nothing is lost.

The Health card below has a "Degraded" of its own, about failed requests. The two are separate.

### Health (last 24 hours)

How the requests MAYA made to your system in the last 24 hours went. It counts every request, reads and writes alike.

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Healthy | 98% or more of the requests succeeded. |
| Degraded | 80% to under 98% succeeded. |
| Having trouble | Under 80% succeeded. |
| No recent activity | No requests in the last 24 hours. |

Beside the badge: "97% of 412 requests OK".

A short spell of "Degraded" is normal when your system is slow or asks MAYA to wait. "Having trouble" for more than an hour, with a Connection card that is not green, means it is time to reconnect. [Connection lost](/docs/wrong/connection-lost) walks through the cases.

### Last sync

The last time MAYA read your bookings successfully, on your own computer's clock with its time zone, for example "Sep 22, 2026, 3:05:12 PM PDT". Someone in another time zone sees the same moment on their own clock. A dash means it has never read them. On a healthy connection this time is at most a few minutes old, because MAYA reads about every five minutes.

## Recent requests

Under the cards is a technical list of MAYA's last 50 requests to Cloudbeds, headed "Recent requests to Cloudbeds". You do not need to read it. If you email us about the connection, it helps us. A request that failed shows its status code, or ERR, in red. Its times show the same way as Last sync, on your own clock. Requests are kept for 7 days. When there are none, it reads "No requests recorded yet".

## Reconnecting from here

Above the cards, what you see depends on the Connection card and on your role. Broken means the card reads Error, Degraded or Disconnected. Any other word counts as not broken.

- **Not broken, Cloudbeds or ThinkReservations, General Manager or Hotel Admin:** a quiet link, <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui>. Use it if you have been asked to, for example after your system's permissions changed.
- **Broken, Cloudbeds or ThinkReservations, General Manager or Hotel Admin:** an amber panel headed "MAYA has lost its connection to Cloudbeds" with the button <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui>. One click sends you to your system to confirm and brings you back. Your rules, history and settings are untouched.
- **Broken, any system, Revenue Manager or Viewer:** the same heading, with a note to ask the property's General Manager or Hotel Admin, since it takes them one click.
- **Broken, Mews, General Manager or Hotel Admin:** a panel headed "Mews needs its keys re-entered", asking you to send us a message. Mews connects with keys from your Mews account rather than a login, so it cannot be repaired from this screen.

The same panel appears as a banner at the top of the dashboard while the connection is broken, from the moment the dashboard opens and whichever tab you are on. On a live Cloudbeds property, the change log's "Prices not reaching Cloudbeds" item also points you here with "Reconnect Cloudbeds on the PMS tab." [Reconnect and disconnect](/docs/connect/reconnect-and-disconnect) has the full walk-through.

## How fresh the tab is

MAYA reads the connection when the dashboard opens, again when you switch property, and each time you open this tab. The banner at the top of the dashboard follows the same read. The tab does not refresh on its own while open. Click another tab and come back to reload it.

<Example title="The Harbour Inn on a quiet Tuesday">
Sam opens the <Ui>PMS</Ui> tab. Connection reads Connected. Health reads Healthy, "99% of 412 requests OK". Last sync reads "Sep 22, 2026, 3:05:12 PM PDT", a couple of minutes ago on Sam's clock at The Harbour Inn. Under the cards, the newest request is from a minute ago. Nothing needs doing.
</Example>

<Callout kind="not-yet">
The tab does not show whether a price reached your system, or when. Sending problems live in the change log, as "Prices not reaching" items. There is no button to sync now: MAYA reads your bookings about every five minutes on its own. There is no badge here that says whether you are live or in simulation. [Tell if you are live](/docs/recipes/tell-if-you-are-live) shows where to look.
</Callout>
