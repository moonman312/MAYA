<InPlainWords>
Click <Ui>Go live</Ui> on the amber strip at the top of the dashboard (or <Ui>Turn them on for real</Ui> on the review screen), read the confirm, click <Ui>Go live</Ui>. From the next cycle MAYA sends prices for tonight and the next 395 nights to your property system, and sends a night again whenever its price changes. Only a General Manager or Hotel Admin can go live, and only they see the strip's button. It is one-way in the app: to go back to simulation, email us.
</InPlainWords>

## Who can go live

A General Manager or a Hotel Admin. The strip at the top of the dashboard shows its <Ui>Go live</Ui> button only to them. Everyone else sees the strip without it, and its <Ui>?</Ui> says "A General Manager or Hotel Admin can switch this property to live." Our staff never get the button when they look at your property to help: they switch a property live only when you ask.

The review screen's <Ui>Turn them on for real</Ui> follows the same rule: a General Manager or Hotel Admin sees the button, and everyone else sees "A General Manager or Hotel Admin can switch this property to live." in its place. The switch itself is refused for anyone below General Manager, too, and nothing changes: a Revenue Manager who gets that far sees "Taking pricing live requires General Manager access", a Viewer "You need admin access on this property to go live." Either way, ask a General Manager or Hotel Admin.

If a General Manager or Hotel Admin sees "You need admin access on this property to go live.", something on our side is missing for this property, and asking someone else will not help. [Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Go%20live) with your property name.

[Roles: who can do what](/docs/team/roles) says it in one line: a Revenue Manager "Cannot take pricing live or change the team."

## Before you go live

MAYA enforces none of these checks. Each one saves a surprise.

Start with [the checklist for your property system](/docs/connect/setting-up-your-system-for-maya): a base rate of its own for every room type, rates loaded for tonight and the next 395 nights, your time zone confirmed with us, anything nobody sleeps in unticked under <Ui>Counts as a room</Ui>, blocked rooms recorded, and floors and ceilings set. Load those rates before you go live. Your rules start from your own rate for each night, and a night your system has at 0 is left alone.

Then four checks in MAYA itself.

1. **Floors and ceilings.** Each room type has a floor and a ceiling: the lowest and highest price that can go out for it.
   - **Where to see them.** The review screen lists every one under "Your floors and ceilings", with a × to remove each (read [Checking what MAYA found](/docs/review/checking-what-maya-found#your-floors-and-ceilings)). The <Ui>Guardrails</Ui> column on the <Ui>Rate Simulator</Ui> tab shows them too, for every room type that counts as a room. Your answers to the five questions set some of them, and the import filled others from your own past rates where there was enough history.
   - **A limit still at $1.00 or $99,999.99** stops nothing in the currencies MAYA works in. To set it, answer the floor or ceiling question again at <AppLink to="questions">maya-rms.com/onboarding/questions</AppLink> (your floor answer goes to every room type at once), or [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Floor%20and%20ceiling) the number. <Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui> on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab offers a card for a room type still at a default when there is a number to offer, and the go-live button stays on the review after it.
   - **A limit that is already set** can be removed on the review, and stays removed, but not changed to another number on a screen yet. [Floors and ceilings](/docs/review/floors-and-ceilings) explains how to move one. Coming from PIE on Cloudbeds, <Ui>Import from PIE</Ui> sets them from PIE's price limits.
2. **Starter rules.** They are on, and the prices they set already show on the calendar. Turning a rule <Ui>Off</Ui> keeps every change it has made to your prices so far, even in simulation, and those prices go out when you go live. To be rid of a starter rule and what it did, <Ui>Delete</Ui> it and choose <Ui>Delete Rule and Undo Its Changes</Ui>. Turn it off only if you are happy for its changes so far to be sent.
3. **Watch for a week.** Open the change log a few times a day (it opens on the 10 most recent runs that changed a price, and <Ui>Older</Ui> reaches back 90 days), check the <Ui>Fired</Ui> column on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab to see which rules act most (click a rule's number for each time it acted), and copy any entry you want to keep. If a price would have surprised you, change the rule or the limit before you go live.
4. **Switch off other pricing tools.** If another tool changes rates in your system, switch it off before you go live. That includes PIE's rules in Cloudbeds, once you have imported them into MAYA. MAYA reads a rate another tool changes the same way as a rate you change by hand. [Rates you change in your property system](/docs/watch/rates-changed-in-your-pms) says what happens to those.

<Example title="A seasonal lodge's limits">
Juniper Lodge usually sells its King at $380, so the import set a floor of $150 and a ceiling of $780. Its own rate in Cloudbeds is $120 on some winter nights and $900 on its festival weekend. Going live sends $150 for those winter nights and $780 for the festival, with no rule behind either, and the go-live confirm says how many nights that is. The owner removes the King's floor and ceiling on the review, so on a night where no rule fires the King's own rate goes out as it is.
</Example>

## Where the button is

There are two ways in, and both open the same confirm.

**The strip at the top.** While your property simulates, a slim amber strip runs across the top of every tab of the dashboard, of the <Ui>Billing</Ui> and <Ui>Team</Ui> pages, and of the review screen: "Simulation · MAYA works out prices but sends nothing to Cloudbeds". For a General Manager or Hotel Admin a green <Ui>Go live</Ui> button sits at its right. It is there for every Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations property, starter rules or not. A Mews property has none (see [the confirm dialog](#the-confirm-dialog)).

The go-live control on the review screen sits inside the card headed "While you were here, we built your first pricing rules", near the end of the screen and above the <Ui>Finish</Ui> button. Under the list of starter rules sits a green <Ui>Turn them on for real</Ui> button, with "Or leave them in simulation and watch for a while. That works too." beside it. Like the strip's button, it shows only for a General Manager or Hotel Admin.

You can come back to that screen at any time at <AppLink to="review">maya-rms.com/onboarding/review</AppLink>. If it opens on "First: does this match reality?", click <Ui>Continue to recommendations</Ui>. The card is near the end of the next screen. The blue banner on the dashboard that points to the review appears only while the review still has cards you have not looked at.

There is no way to schedule going live for a later date, such as next Monday morning. Sending starts on the next cycle after a General Manager or Hotel Admin confirms, so click the button when you want sending to start, after switching off any other tool that changes your rates. If we switch you live by email, send the email when you want sending to start.

<Callout kind="not-yet" title="No card, no button">
The card appears only when setup built starter rules for you. They need at least 60 days of booking history from the history import, which runs for Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations but not yet for Mews. Once setup has built them, the card stays, including after anyone on your team runs <Ui>Get suggestions from my data</Ui> on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab. Without the card, a General Manager or Hotel Admin uses <Ui>Go live</Ui> on the strip at the top of the dashboard. You can also [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Go%20live) with your property name and we switch the property live for you.
</Callout>

Nothing happens until you click <Ui>Go live</Ui> inside it. <Ui>Not yet</Ui>, or the Escape key, closes it and leaves you in simulation. While the switch is being made the button reads "Switching…". The title names your property, and the words depend on your property system.

Under those lines, above the buttons, one more sentence: "Going live sends MAYA's prices to Cloudbeds automatically. You're confirming you've reviewed your rules and limits (Terms 3.3)." (with your system's name). The word "Terms" is a link to the Terms of Service.

The button takes live the property whose name is in the title. If you switched to another property in another tab since the page loaded, nothing changes and the dialog says "This page is for another property. Reload and try again." Reload, check the name, and go live from there.

If the switch fails for a reason other than your role, the dialog stays open and shows the reason above the buttons. It is the reason MAYA sent back, or your browser's own words if the connection dropped. When no reason came back at all, it reads "Couldn't switch to live. Try again." Click <Ui>Go live</Ui> once more. "Not signed in" means your session has ended: sign in again and try once more. If it keeps failing, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Go%20live%20failed) with the words you see.

### The nights outside your limits

On Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations, when some nights in the window have your own rate in your system outside a room type's floor or ceiling, a line above the Terms sentence counts them: "12 nights have a rate outside your floor or ceiling; MAYA will move them inside when it sends." (or "1 night has a rate…, MAYA will move it inside…"). A night counts once however many of its room types are outside. It counts the nights from tonight through the window, on a rate your system has for the night above 0, leaving out nights with a price you typed (those go out as typed) and nights past the last one your system returned a rate for. Room types that are not active are left out, and so is a type you unticked as a room unless a rule changes it, since MAYA sends nothing for it. When none are outside, or the count cannot be read, there is no line, and the button works the same either way.

On those nights, a rate under the floor goes out at the floor and one over the ceiling goes out at the ceiling, even where no rule fired. To keep your own rate on those nights, remove the limit on the review before you go live. Read [What "Each price it sends replaces that night's rate" means](#what-each-price-it-sends-replaces-that-nights-rate-means).

### What "Each price it sends replaces that night's rate" means

For each room type MAYA writes one nightly rate to one rate plan in your system: the base rate in Cloudbeds, the Best Available rate type in ThinkReservations. The number that was there is replaced by the price your rules set for that night. That price starts from the rate you had, so on a night where no rule fired the number that goes out is your own rate, kept between that room type's floor and ceiling. If your rate is under the floor or over the ceiling, the floor or ceiling goes out instead. The confirm counts those nights, and the review lists every floor and ceiling with a remove.

Rate plans that follow the base rate (derived plans, and packages built on it) move as your system's own rules move them. MAYA never writes those directly. [How prices reach your property system](/docs/live/how-prices-reach-your-pms) has the detail.

"A night is sent again whenever its price changes" is the other half. After the first send, a night goes out again only when MAYA's price for it changes. A night whose price stays the same is not sent again.

### Terms 3.3

Clause 3.3 of the Terms of Service says who can switch a property to live: a General Manager or Hotel Admin, or another role we designate, and you choose who holds those roles. It treats your click on <Ui>Go live</Ui> as your confirmation of two things. First, that you have reviewed the property's rules and limits: every active rule, whether you wrote it or MAYA built it, and every floor and ceiling. Second, that you accept responsibility for every rate MAYA publishes for the property. It also says that if we switch a property live at your request or with your permission, we act on your instruction. That is why the sentence sits beside the button rather than in Terms accepted weeks earlier, possibly by someone else. [Terms and legal](/docs/reference/terms-and-legal) has the clause.

## What MAYA records when you go live

- Who clicked the button, the version of the Terms shown on the screen, your IP address and your browser. This is kept as the record of the go-live.
- The moment the property went live. It matters later: a rate you changed in your system while simulating is read as your base rate, not as a manual price, and this moment is how MAYA tells the two apart.
- A request to read your own rates again before the first send.

Nothing else changes. Your rules, your floors and ceilings, your history, your manual prices and your team are exactly as they were. Going live flips one switch.

## What happens next

1. **The strip becomes a tag.** The amber strip is gone and a small green <Ui>Live</Ui> tag sits at the top right of every screen. On the review screen the button becomes a pill: "✓ Live: your rules are now managing prices". In the change log a "Went live" item marks the moment ("Sam took pricing live. From the next cycle, MAYA sends its prices to Cloudbeds."), runs from before keep their <Ui>Simulation</Ui> tag, and runs from now on say whether each price was sent. A simulated change whose price the night still has then reads "Nothing was sent to Cloudbeds at the time." with a second line once the first send has gone, such as "Sent to Cloudbeds after you went live." ([The change log](/docs/watch/the-change-log#runs-made-in-simulation)).
2. **The next cycle reads your rates first.** Cycles run about every five minutes. The first live cycle reads your own rate for every night in the window before it prices. Whatever you had in your system at that moment is the starting point your rules work from.
3. **Then it sends.** Prices for tonight and the next 395 nights go out, nearest nights first, in batches. Nothing has been sent before, so every priced night goes out on this first send: nights where a rule fired at the rule's price, nights where none fired at your own rate (kept between the room type's floor and ceiling), and a price you typed while simulating as typed. A change made while simulating by a rule you have since turned off goes out too.
4. **After that, changes only.** A night is sent again when its price changes. A cycle where nothing changed sends nothing.

Everything about what is sent, held back, confirmed and retried is on [How prices reach your property system](/docs/live/how-prices-reach-your-pms).

### How long until prices appear in my system

Usually on the next cycle, so about five minutes. Cloudbeds applies each price a few seconds after receiving it. ThinkReservations accepts each send and applies it shortly after.

It takes longer when the first read of your rates fails or runs out of time (the first send waits for the next cycle that reads them), when your property is large (a big first send continues on the next cycle), or when your system asks MAYA to slow down. If nothing has changed in your system after 15 minutes, open the change log. A problem that needs you appears there as "Prices not reaching Cloudbeds". [Prices are not reaching your property system](/docs/wrong/prices-not-reaching-your-pms) walks through what to check.

## Going live during the trial

Yes. A property on its free trial is treated as paid for everything MAYA does, sending included. The only gate on going live is your role.

## Going live for some room types only

Not yet. The switch is for the whole property. What you can do instead: a rule only changes the room types you tick under <Ui>Apply to room types</Ui>, so a room type no rule names is sent at your own rate, kept between its floor and ceiling. Its number in your system does not move unless your rate sits under its floor or over its ceiling, and then the floor or ceiling goes out instead. There is no way to keep a room type out of sending altogether.

<Callout kind="careful" title="One-way in the app">
There is no switch to put a property back into simulation. To pause, turn your rules off (the prices they set stay). To go back to simulation, email us. [Pausing, or going back to simulation](/docs/live/pausing-or-going-back) covers both.
</Callout>

<Example title="The Harbour Inn goes live">
On Tuesday at 3:05 PM (your browser's clock) Sam, the Hotel Admin, clicks <Ui>Go live</Ui> on the amber strip at the top of the dashboard, reads "Send The Harbour Inn's prices to Cloudbeds?" and clicks <Ui>Go live</Ui>. The strip gives way to the green <Ui>Live</Ui> tag.

At about 3:10 PM the next cycle reads the Harbour Inn's Cloudbeds rates for the 396 nights, then runs the rules. "Busy nights" has fired on two Friday nights in November, so on those two Fridays Standard goes out at $165, Deluxe at $220 and Suite at $330. Every other night goes out at the rates Sam already had: Standard $150, Deluxe $200, Suite $300. Cloudbeds' derived plans follow their base rates by Cloudbeds' own rules.

At about 3:15 PM the next cycle finds nothing changed and sends nothing. On Thursday a booking takes another night over 80% full. "Busy nights" fires, and only that night's three room types are sent.
</Example>
