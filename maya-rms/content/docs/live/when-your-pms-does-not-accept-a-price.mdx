<InPlainWords>
If a price does not land for a reason that usually clears on its own, MAYA sends it again every cycle, up to 10 times at one price, then once a day. If the reason needs you, such as a rate rule, the night is held and tried again after a day, when its price changes, or (for a connection or permission problem) after a reconnect. A missing base rate is different: MAYA looks for one again every cycle, so the night usually goes out on the cycle after you add it. You are told in the change log, in an item headed "Prices not reaching Cloudbeds", straight away for a cause that needs you, otherwise after 2 hours and 5 tries. Each item names the cause and, when there is something for you to do, says what.
</InPlainWords>

## Where you see a problem

Sending problems live in one place: the <Ui>Change Log</Ui> tab. There is no email and no badge for them. The exception is a lost connection: the connection banner shows when Cloudbeds stops accepting MAYA's connection, and once the connection has read Error or Disconnected for about an hour, the General Managers and Hotel Admins get one email ([emails MAYA sends](/docs/billing/emails-maya-sends#the-connection-down-email)). While a problem is ongoing its item sits at the top of the log, above every pricing run, with an amber border. Once it is over it drops into the timeline at the point it ended, and only while the log still shows runs from that time.

Only a live property has these items. In simulation nothing is sent, so nothing can fail to land. Anyone on the team who can open the change log can read them.

## Reading the item

An item has four parts, and a fifth you can open.

1. **The header**: "Prices not reaching Cloudbeds", then the date it opened and, in brackets, how long ago. The name is your system's: "Prices not reaching Think Reservations" for ThinkReservations.
2. **The cause**, one sentence, for example "Cloudbeds refused MAYA's price for Deluxe because it breaks a rate rule set in Cloudbeds, such as a minimum or maximum rate."
3. **The size and the state**: "6 nights, 2 room types · Still happening", or "· Resolved \<when\>" once the prices landed, or "· Ended \<when\>" when something else closed it.
4. **What to do**, one line, shown only while the problem is ongoing and only for causes where there is something for you to do: "Check the rate limits for this room type in Cloudbeds."
5. **<Ui>Show 12 tries</Ui>** opens the list of tries behind it, oldest first, one line per group that went the same way: "12 tries, Sep 17, 10:05 AM to 10:50 AM, 6 nights: Cloudbeds refused them". Hover a line to read your system's own words. When every line is a check MAYA made without reaching your system, the button reads <Ui>Show 12 checks</Ui>. A footer such as "3 other tries not shown" means more tries were counted than listed.

The words at the end of a try line say how it went: "Cloudbeds refused them", "Cloudbeds accepted them, then rejected them", "Cloudbeds never confirmed them", "Nothing to send to in Cloudbeds", "Couldn't read the rates in Cloudbeds", "MAYA held them back", or "Went through".

Room types are named in the cause: "Deluxe rates", "Deluxe and Suite rates", or "rates for 3 room types". When MAYA could not name them it says "some rates" or "some room types".

## Every cause, and what to do

The sentences below are exactly what the change log says, with Cloudbeds and the Deluxe room type as the example. For ThinkReservations the name changes and nothing else.

| The item says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Cloudbeds stopped accepting MAYA's connection, so Deluxe rates can't be changed. | Your system no longer accepts MAYA's connection, so nothing can be written. The connection banner appears at the same time. | "Reconnect Cloudbeds on the PMS tab." A General Manager or Hotel Admin clicks <Ui>Reconnect Cloudbeds</Ui>. Held prices go out on the next cycle after. |
| Cloudbeds won't let MAYA change Deluxe rates because MAYA doesn't have permission to update rates. | Your system lets MAYA read but refuses the write. Someone changed MAYA's permissions there. | "Give MAYA permission to update rates in Cloudbeds, then reconnect on the PMS tab." |
| Cloudbeds won't let MAYA change Deluxe rates because that rate follows another rate plan. | The room type's only rates are derived from another plan, and your system does not let an app write a derived rate. | "In Cloudbeds, give this room type a base rate of its own that doesn't follow another plan." The nights usually go out on the next cycle after you do. |

<Callout kind="good-to-know" title="How a refusal gets its cause">
When your system refuses a price, MAYA matches its words against a fixed list of phrases and answer codes. A message on the list gets its cause. Anything else is filed as "couldn't tell why". Your system's own words are behind the hover on the try line, so you can check the match yourself.
</Callout>

### Matching MAYA's floor or ceiling to your system

There is no screen for changing one room type's floor or ceiling in MAYA. So when your system's minimum sits above MAYA's floor (or its maximum below MAYA's ceiling), you have three ways to line them up. They differ in what else they change.

1. **Change your system to match MAYA.** Lower the minimum, or raise the maximum, for that room type in your system. Only that room type is touched. MAYA's floor and ceiling for each room type are in the <Ui>Guardrails</Ui> column on the <Ui>Rate Simulator</Ui> tab.
2. **Answer the floor question again.** Your answer becomes the floor of every room type at once, not only this one. Use it when one floor suits them all. A new ceiling answer goes only to room types that have never sold above it. [Set or change a floor or ceiling](/docs/recipes/set-a-floor-or-ceiling) has the steps and the catches.
3. **Email us for one room type.** [Email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Floor%20and%20ceiling) with the room type and the numbers.

The night that was refused waits. MAYA is not told about a change you make in your system, so the night goes again within a day, or sooner if its price changes. To send it now, type a price on it that sits inside both your system's limits and MAYA's.

### The time zone case

MAYA counts days on your property's own calendar, using the time zone it read from your system when you connected. If the time zone in your system has since changed, or was wrong then, the two can disagree about when tonight ends. If midnight comes first in your system's time zone, your system counts tonight as over until midnight comes in MAYA's. It refuses any new price MAYA sends for tonight in those hours.

Don't wait for the change log to tell you, because it will usually not show this cause at all. A refused night is held rather than sent every cycle, and at midnight in MAYA's time zone it leaves the 396-night window. An item appears only once one night has had at least 5 tries over 2 hours or more. A held night rarely gets there before midnight.

Look at your rates instead. The sign is a new price for tonight, set in those hours, that shows as the <Ui>Current price</Ui> in MAYA but never reaches your system. Prices for later nights go through as normal. If you see it, check the property's time zone in your system first. To change the time zone MAYA uses, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Time%20zone). [Time, weekends, currency and holidays](/docs/reference/time-weekends-currency-and-holidays) explains how MAYA counts days.

<Callout kind="example" title="Eastern in Cloudbeds, Pacific in MAYA">
Say Cloudbeds is set to US Eastern and MAYA has US Pacific. From 9:00 PM to midnight Pacific time, Cloudbeds already counts tonight as over and refuses any new price for tonight. Later nights go through as normal.
</Callout>

## Retries, and holds

- A price that fails for a cause that clears on its own (slowing down, not responding, never confirmed, cause unrecognised) is sent again every cycle, up to 10 tries at that price, then once a day. For a price you typed, the line under the price box on the calendar counts them down: "Saved. It couldn't be sent yet. MAYA will retry 3 more times."
- A price that fails for a cause that needs a person (connection, permission, a rate rule, a night already begun) is held. It is not sent again until the price changes, the rate plan behind it changes, a day has passed, or, for a connection or permission problem, the connection has been reconnected. MAYA is not told about a change you make in your system. So after you do what the item asks there, the held night goes again within a day, or sooner if its price or rate plan changes. You do not have to touch the price. A different price you type goes out now; typing the same price again does not, because the tries are counted per price. To send the same price again now, use <Ui>Try again</Ui> under the price box on the calendar: it appears once the line there reads "This price couldn't be sent to Cloudbeds.", and sends that price once more, whatever held it. [Setting a price yourself](/docs/watch/setting-a-price-yourself#what-the-line-says-afterwards) has the lines.
- A room type with no base rate, or whose only rates follow another plan, is not held. While its nights have nowhere to go, MAYA reads your system's rate plans again on every cycle. So once you add a base rate, the nights usually go out on the next cycle.
- A rate MAYA cannot find is looked up again once, after re-reading your catalogue, and then held.
- A price of 0 is never sent. MAYA checks each cycle whether your system now has the night at 0, and closes the item when it does.
- A night is never given up on for good. Every held or exhausted night gets one more try each day, so a fix nobody told MAYA about, such as a rate rule loosened in your system, is picked up within a day.

## When you are told

- **At once** for a cause that needs you: a connection or permission problem, a derived-only rate, a missing base rate, a rate rule, a price of 0. And for a rate MAYA cannot find, once its second try has failed.
- **After 2 hours and at least 5 tries** for the causes that usually clear on their own. A price that lands on a retry before then is never mentioned. That is deliberate: an item you cannot act on is noise.
- **Usually not at all** for a night that had already begun. Such a night leaves the window at midnight, nearly always before it reaches 5 tries. [The time zone case](#the-time-zone-case) says what to look for instead.

An item that closed within the last hour and then fails again is reopened rather than duplicated.

## Resolved, and Ended

An item is over when no night is still failing for that cause. The second line then says how it ended:

- **Resolved** when your system took the prices (for ThinkReservations, when it accepted them).
- **Ended** when something else closed it: a new price replaced the failing one, the night left the 396-night window, the night is no longer priced, or the property stopped sending (it went back to simulation, or its subscription lapsed).

A resolved or ended item is not deleted. It sits in the timeline at the point it ended, for as long as the log shows runs from that time.

## Is this a MAYA problem or a Cloudbeds problem?

The cause sentence names which system did what: "Cloudbeds refused", "Cloudbeds isn't responding", "MAYA doesn't send". The hover shows your system's own words. As a rule:

- A refusal that names a setting (a rate rule, a permission, a plan that follows another) is settled in your system. For Cloudbeds, Cloudbeds Support can see those settings; we cannot.
- Slowing down, not responding and never confirmed are your system being busy or down. Wait.
- "MAYA couldn't tell why" and anything you cannot place: email us with the sentence, the hover text and your property name.

[Contact support](/docs/help/contact-support) has our address and the link to Cloudbeds Support.

## Do I need to do anything?

Only when the item has an action line. An item with no action line is MAYA keeping you informed while it retries. If the action line asks you to add a base rate, the nights usually go out on the next cycle after you do. If it asks for another change in your system, such as a rate limit, make the change, and the held nights go again within a day, or sooner if their price changes. If it asks for a reconnect, a General Manager or Hotel Admin reconnects on the PMS tab, and the held prices go out on the cycle after. [Reconnect your property system](/docs/recipes/reconnect-your-property-system) has the clicks.

For a night you typed a price on, you need not wait the day. Open the night on the <Ui>Calendar</Ui> tab: the line under the price box reads "This price couldn't be sent to Cloudbeds." once MAYA has stopped retrying it, and <Ui>Try again</Ui> beside it sends the price once more now (Revenue Manager or higher). <Ui>See the error log</Ui> next to it opens this item.

## ThinkReservations and Mews

<PmsTabs>
<PmsTab pms="cloudbeds">
Everything on this page applies as written. A refused write for a gone connection also marks the connection disconnected, so the reconnect banner appears at the same time.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="thinkreservations">
The sentences read "Think Reservations" in place of "Cloudbeds". Three differences:

- A refused write never disconnects ThinkReservations. It is held as a permission problem and the connection stays up for reading. So the cause "stopped accepting MAYA's connection" is never filed for ThinkReservations; you see "won't let MAYA change ... because MAYA doesn't have permission to update rates" instead.
- ThinkReservations answers "accepted" and applies the rates later, and tells MAYA nothing more after that. So "never confirmed it saved them" appears only when a send was interrupted before ThinkReservations answered, and MAYA sends it again. The item reads "Resolved" as soon as ThinkReservations accepts the prices. MAYA's check that they are really there comes with its next hourly read.
- If ThinkReservations quietly drops an accepted batch, the night keeps showing MAYA's price in MAYA while ThinkReservations keeps the old rate, until the price next changes and is sent again. Compare a night in both places if you suspect this.
</PmsTab>
<PmsTab pms="mews">
Nothing is sent to Mews, so a Mews property never has a "Prices not reaching" item.
</PmsTab>
</PmsTabs>

## MAYA's own holds are not shown

Before every send MAYA checks each night against the room type as it is now: inside the window, room type still active, price above 0, inside the floor and ceiling, backed by a recent pricing run, and your system not holding the night at 0 with no typed price. A night held by one of those checks is recorded but not shown to you, except a price of 0, which needs you. Most of the others clear on a later cycle without you. A room type switched off as a duplicate is never meant to be sent, and a night your system has at 0 waits until you load a rate there. [How prices reach your property system](/docs/live/how-prices-reach-your-pms) lists the checks.

<Callout kind="not-yet">
MAYA does not email you about a sending problem. The only banner is the connection banner, when Cloudbeds stops accepting MAYA's connection. Everything else appears only in the change log. [Emails MAYA sends](/docs/billing/emails-maya-sends) lists the few emails that exist.
</Callout>

<Example title="A rate rule at the Harbour Inn">
Sam sets a minimum rate of $120 on Deluxe in Cloudbeds. MAYA's floor for Deluxe is $110, lower than that minimum. On Monday, "Last minute" (booking window Less than 3 and occupancy Less than 50, Decrease 15%) cuts Deluxe for a quiet Wednesday night from $140 to $119. That is above MAYA's floor, so it goes out, and Cloudbeds refuses it.

Because a rate rule is a cause that needs a person, the change log shows at once: "Prices not reaching Cloudbeds", then "Cloudbeds refused MAYA's price for Deluxe because it breaks a rate rule set in Cloudbeds, such as a minimum or maximum rate." and "1 night, 1 room type · Still happening", with "Check the rate limits for this room type in Cloudbeds."

Sam lowers the Deluxe minimum in Cloudbeds to $110, to match MAYA's floor. MAYA is not told about that change, so the night stays held. On Tuesday it goes again at $119, and Cloudbeds takes it. The item now reads "· Resolved" with the time. Typing $120 on the night on Monday would have sent it at once instead.
</Example>
