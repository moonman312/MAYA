<InPlainWords>
To pause, turn your rules off: the prices they set stay where they are. To undo what a rule did, delete it. To hold a price on some nights, type it. To put the whole property back into simulation, email us; only we can switch it. Turning rules off, typing a price or going back to simulation never puts back a rate MAYA has replaced. Deleting a rule is the one route that does: its changes come off and the new prices are sent.
</InPlainWords>

## Five ways to stop, and what each keeps

| You want to | Do this | Prices already in your system |
|---|---|---|
| Stop every rule acting from now on | Turn each rule <Ui>Off</Ui> on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab | Stay as they are |
| Undo everything one rule did | <Ui>Delete</Ui> the rule | Go back to what they would be without it, and are sent |
| Stop one rule on certain nights | Answer <Ui>Stop for this night</Ui> on the amber banner | Stay as they are, unless enough of the bookings behind a raise cancel |
| Hold a price on a night or a stretch | Type the price on the <Ui>Calendar</Ui> tab, with <Ui>through…</Ui> for a range | Become the price you typed |
| Stop sending altogether, keep everything else | Email us to go back to simulation | Stay as they are |

## Turn rules off

Every rule on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab has an <Ui>On</Ui> / <Ui>Off</Ui> switch. Click it and the rule is off from the next pricing run. There is no confirmation.

Off freezes. The rule stops acting, and every change it has already made stays on the price: a raise stays raised, a cut stays cut. Those prices are already in your system and MAYA does not send them again or send anything to undo them. Turn the rule <Ui>On</Ui> again and it resumes from the next run, starting from the prices as they are.

Two details are easy to miss. A rule that is off is not checked for cancellations, so a raise it made does not come off when the bookings behind it cancel. And a booking speed or pickup count rule that is off still marks the last time it raised a night (or cut it). The other booking speed rules that move the price the same way count only bookings since then. [Turning a rule off, deleting it, or changing it](/docs/rules/off-delete-or-change) has the detail.

To pause everything, turn every rule off. Nothing else happens: MAYA keeps reading your bookings and your rates, and sends a price only when one changes. With every rule off, a price changes when you type or clear one, or when a floor or ceiling you change moves it. And each morning one new night enters the window. It goes out at your own rate, kept between your floor and ceiling. [Pause everything for a while](/docs/recipes/pause-everything) is the short version.

<Callout kind="careful" title="Off does not undo">
Turning a rule off keeps every price it set. If a rule has set prices you do not want, delete it, or type the prices you want. Off alone leaves them in place.
</Callout>

## Delete a rule

The red <Ui>Delete</Ui> button on a rule opens a dialog headed "Delete "Busy nights"?". It says: "Deleting this rule also undoes the price changes it has already made. Any night it adjusted goes back to what the price would be if this rule had never run." It then offers the other choice, turning the rule off instead, with the prices it set staying exactly as they are. And it warns: "Deleting cannot be undone."

Three buttons: <Ui>Delete Rule and Undo Its Changes</Ui>, <Ui>Turn It Off and Keep Its Changes</Ui> (which does the same as the switch and closes the dialog), and <Ui>Cancel</Ui>.

Delete removes the rule and every effect it had. The next pricing run prices every night as if the rule had never run, and the prices that changed are sent to your system on that cycle. The change log keeps only its last 10 runs (about 50 minutes), so entries from before the delete soon drop off. Until they do, an occupancy or booking window rule's move in them is named "Pricing rule", and a booking speed or pickup rule's move is left out. [The change log](/docs/watch/the-change-log) has the detail.

## Stop a rule on certain nights

Once a booking speed or pickup rule has adjusted the same night three times, the amber Rules that keep adjusting banner asks you what to do. <Ui>Stop for this night</Ui> stops that rule on that night. What it already cut stays. A raise it already made stays, unless enough of the bookings behind it cancel. Your other rules keep working there. The rule then shows a "Stopped on 3 nights" chip on the <Ui>Rules</Ui> tab, and <Ui>Let it run again</Ui> puts it back. [Rules that keep adjusting](/docs/rules/rules-that-keep-adjusting) explains the banner. There is no way to stop a rule on one night before the banner asks, other than turning the rule off everywhere. A typed price takes off what the rule already did on that night, but the rule can fire on it again.

## Hold a price on a night or a stretch

Click a night on the <Ui>Calendar</Ui> tab, type a price for a room type, click <Ui>through…</Ui> to pick the last night if you want a range, and click <Ui>Save</Ui>. On a live property, nights inside the 60-night window go to your system as typed, now. Nights further ahead are sent as they enter the window. In simulation nothing is sent. Rules that had already moved those nights pause for that room type. Rules that fire later still apply on top of your price, so a typed price is a starting point, not a lock. A range can run from tonight up to 364 days ahead (365 nights at most).

<Ui>Clear</Ui> takes your price off the night, and your rules set its price again. [Setting a price yourself](/docs/watch/setting-a-price-yourself) has every detail, and [Hold a price for a wedding or event weekend](/docs/recipes/hold-a-price-for-an-event) is the short version.

## Back to simulation

There is no switch in MAYA to put a property back into simulation. Going live is one-way in the app. To go back, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Back%20to%20simulation) from your MAYA sign-in address with your property name. We switch it for you.

What happens once we do:

- **Sending stops on the next cycle.** Everything else carries on: bookings are read, rules run, the change log fills, the calendar shows what MAYA would send.
- **The prices already in your system stay.** MAYA never sends a rate to undo an earlier one. If you want different rates, set them in your system, or wait until you are live again and type them in MAYA.
- **On a night MAYA never sent to, a rate you change in your system becomes the base.** For example, a night that entered the window after we switched you back. MAYA reads your rate again within the hour (Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations) and uses it as the night's starting price. It is not kept as a manual price.
- **On a night MAYA sent to while you were live, a rate you change in your system waits.** While you simulate, your rules keep working from the base that night already had. When you go live again, MAYA reads your rate as that night's base (next point).
- **When you go live again**, from the button on the review screen if it is still there (otherwise email us and we switch you live again), MAYA reads your rates once more before its first send, and your rules work on top of them. A night whose rate you changed while simulating starts from that rate. A rate you changed in your system on a night MAYA had sent to, and that MAYA had already kept as a manual price ("Changed in Cloudbeds"), stays a manual price.
- **Your rules, limits, history, manual prices and team are untouched.**

[Simulation mode](/docs/live/simulation-mode) describes the state you go back to.

## Cancelling, and disconnecting

Cancelling your subscription is a different thing from pausing. It is done in the billing portal or by email, takes effect at the end of the paid period, and MAYA keeps working until then. After that, reading, pricing and sending stop, and the rates in your system stay as they are. Your rules and history stay too, and <Ui>Restart your subscription</Ui> brings MAYA back. [Cancelling, restarting and leaving](/docs/billing/cancelling-restarting-and-leaving) has the steps.

There is no disconnect button in MAYA. On Cloudbeds, uninstalling MAYA from your Cloudbeds account disconnects it: Cloudbeds tells MAYA, and reading, pricing and sending stop. On ThinkReservations or Mews, [email us](mailto:info@modern-hospitality-solutions.com?subject=Disconnect) and we handle it with you. Disconnecting reverts nothing and does not cancel billing. To stop completely, cancel first, then disconnect. [Reconnecting and disconnecting](/docs/connect/reconnect-and-disconnect) has the steps for each system.

<Callout kind="not-yet" title="No pause on billing">
The billing portal has no pause option. Turn your rules off to pause pricing while you keep paying, or cancel and restart later.
</Callout>

## A closed season

MAYA does not read future closures from your system, and there is no screen to enter one. Nights you are closed are priced and sent like any others while they are inside the 60-night window. Three ways to handle a closed stretch, from simplest:

1. **Close the nights in your system.** Set the rate to 0 there (or close the night the way your system does, if that reports as a rate of 0). On a night MAYA has already sent to, MAYA reads the 0 on its next hourly read, your rules stop running on that night, and any manual price there is cleared. On a night MAYA has never sent to, a rate of 0 means the night is not priced at all. Either way nothing more is sent for it. When you reopen and load a rate, MAYA reads it as the night's base and your rules work on top of it. This works for Cloudbeds and ThinkReservations. Nothing is sent to Mews in any case.
2. **Turn your rules off** for the closed stretch and back on afterwards. Simple to do, but it pauses every night, open or closed.
3. **Type a price** for the closed nights as a range. A typed price is sent as typed, so this only helps if a placeholder rate is what you want in your system.

A closure in the past is a separate matter: it should not skew comparisons. MAYA finds long stretches of zero occupancy in your history and asks on the review, "Were you closed ...?". Confirming keeps those nights out of comparisons. [Checking what MAYA found](/docs/review/checking-what-maya-found) explains that card.

<Example title="January at the Harbour Inn">
The Harbour Inn closes every January. In late November, Sam sets every January night to 0 in Cloudbeds. Nights up to 59 days ahead have already been sent to by MAYA. On its next hourly read MAYA finds the 0, the rules stop running on those nights, and the manual price Sam had typed for New Year's Day is cleared. January nights further out have never been sent to and are read as 0, so they are never priced. Nothing more goes to Cloudbeds for January.

On February 1 Sam loads $150 for Standard from February 15 onwards. MAYA reads $150 as the base for those nights and "Busy nights" raises the ones over 80% full to $165 as usual.
</Example>
